# Voice mastering and the viewer-first standard (13 November 2026)

Two changes for all ten channels: the audio every video is delivered with, and
the title and opening every new script is written with. Both follow from
measurements taken on 13 November 2026, recorded here with their limits.

## 1. What was measured

| What | Value |
|---|---|
| Long-forms published | 78 |
| Long-forms with 25 views at 3 days old | 0, on every channel |
| Views on the only channel with a metrics issue (#320, 6 Nov) | long-form 1 to 11, Shorts 59 to 394 |
| Title pattern on 38 of the 40 most recent long-forms | `<reference> <daypart> Prayer \| <channel>` |
| Most recent 12 scripts opening "…welcome to `<channel>`." | 10 |
| Narration as synthesised | about -19.7 LUFS, true peak -7.4 dBFS |
| Finished mix (voice + music) | about -19.1 LUFS, true peak -5.6 dBFS |

Audio figures come from the narration and renders committed under
`assets/deepdive/` and `docs/media/`, and from a bed generated by
`tools/score.py`. A current Charon take could not be measured from the build
sandbox (no speech key there); the mastering measures every take itself, so it
does not depend on these samples being typical.

Not measured, because the repository does not hold it: click-through rate,
retention curves, and subscriber counts for nine of the ten channels. Per-video
numbers exist only as 30-day workflow artifacts.

## 2. Voice: `pipeline/voice_master.py`

YouTube plays against a -14 LUFS reference. It turns loud videos down and never
turns quiet ones up, so a -19 LUFS video plays about 5 dB quieter than the one
before it. That, plus a music bed only 10 LU under the voice, is the audibility
problem.

The mastering runs inside the final encode of both long-form
(`pipeline/prayer_visuals.py`) and Shorts (`tools/shorts.py`):

1. Tone: high-pass at 75 Hz, -1 dB at 220 Hz, +1.5 dB at 3.4 kHz.
2. Dynamics: 2.5:1 compression from -22 dB.
3. Loudness: the take is measured, then given one fixed gain to -15 LUFS with a
   -1.5 dBTP ceiling (two-pass linear `loudnorm`; it cannot pump on pauses).
4. Mix: the music drops 2.8 dB while words are spoken and returns in the
   pauses; a limiter holds the mix under -1 dBFS.
5. Music level (added 14 November, recipe `voice-master-v2`): the bed is 3 dB
   louder than in the first mastered videos. See "Music balance" below.

Measured on the committed Psalm 91 narration over a generated bed:

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Mix loudness | -19.1 LUFS | -14.9 LUFS |
| Mix peak | -5.1 dBFS | -1.8 dBFS |
| Voice over music | 10 LU | 11.6 LU in pauses, 14.4 LU under speech |
| Duration | 38.00 s | 38.00 s |

### Music balance (14 November)

The first mastered videos (`voice-master-v1`) left the music where it was while
the voice came up, so the bed sat 14.6 LU under the voice in pauses and 17.4 LU
under it during speech. After listening to them the channel owner said the
voice is perfect and the background music is a little low.

`MUSIC_LIFT_DB = 3.0` in `pipeline/voice_master.py` raises the bed by 3 dB in
the mastered mix, for long-form and Shorts alike. Measured on the same sample:

| | v1 | v2 (now) |
|---|---|---|
| Music alone (pauses) | -29.6 LUFS | -26.6 LUFS |
| Music under speech | -32.4 LUFS | -29.4 LUFS |
| Voice over music, pauses / speech | 14.6 / 17.4 LU | 11.6 / 14.4 LU |
| Voice | -15.0 LUFS | -15.0 LUFS |
| Finished mix | -14.9 LUFS, peak -1.7 dBFS | -14.9 LUFS, peak -1.5 dBFS |

The voice does not move and the video is no louder; only the balance changes.
To go further, change the one number: +4 gives 10.6 / 13.4 LU. Past +6 the
music is closer to the voice than it was before mastering.

Only videos rendered after this change carry it. Videos already on the channels
keep the audio they were uploaded with; YouTube does not allow replacing it.

What it does not touch: the voice (Charon, locked), the words, the pacing, the
narration file, its checksum, cached speech chunks, or word timings. No speech
is re-synthesised, so no free-tier quota is spent. Nothing in the chain can
change timing, and a test asserts that.

If the measurement fails, the render uses the previous filter graph unchanged
and prints `[voice-master] loudness could not be measured`. Otherwise the leg
log carries one line, for example
`[voice-master] voice-master-v2: narration -19.7 LUFS, peak -7.4 dBTP -> -15 LUFS (+4.7 dB), music +3 dB and ducked under speech`.

Limits: this is level, clarity and balance. It cannot make the synthesis itself
more human; that is decided by the model, the voice and the delivery style. The
delivery style is still one string for all channels
(`calm, reverent, peaceful devotional tone`). A per-channel style is the next
voice step and needs a human ear on one channel first, because every earlier
change to what reaches the speech model put a defect into a published video.

## 3. Viewer-first standard: `pipeline/brain_equipment/viewer_first_standard.md`

One file, appended last to every channel's authoring prompt by
`compile_full_brain_prompt`. It replaces the title template in the front-matter
skeleton and adds rules the prompt never stated:

- Title: the listener's need or the promise first, then the scripture reference,
  still ending with the channel name, still 35 to 70 characters. No promised
  outcomes, no shouting, no emoji.
- `thumb_text` completes the title instead of repeating it.
- `seo_hook` says who the prayer is for and what it asks.
- Opening: no greeting, no channel name in the first three sentences, the
  listener's situation in sentence one, scripture read within 45 seconds, and no
  opening reused between scripts.
- Retention: a natural handover at the end of each scene, varied texture, one
  concrete human moment, speakable sentences, benediction before the single
  call-to-action, and an invitation to write a first name or a need in the
  comments.

`strip_channel_greeting` in `pipeline/gemini_brain.py` backs the opening rule at
authoring time: a first sentence that only greets and says "welcome to
`<this channel>`" is removed, and the next sentence opens the video. It is
skipped when the cut would take the script under the 850-word floor.

Scope: newly authored scripts only. The 49 long-forms already queued keep their
titles and openings; at the 17 to 30 a day actually delivered the queue turns over in two to three days.
Published videos are not retitled.

Why this and not more volume: the reasoning that need-led titles draw more
search and browse clicks than verse references is general practice, not
something measured on these channels. The loop that could measure it
(`pipeline/performance_feedback.py`) has no data yet because no long-form has
reached 25 views.

## 4. Thumbnail line: a fault found on the way

`_clamp_thumb_text_for_ctr` (`pipeline/gemini_brain.py`) checks that the
thumbnail line fits its box. When the first three words were too wide it
replaced the whole line with a fixed phrase. On 13 November two Family Altar
jobs authored in one pass, `0309` (Job 1:10) and `0310` (Proverbs 3:33), both
came out as `PERFECT PEACE`: a line about neither passage, identical on two
thumbnails, and `tests/test_supply_never_stalls.py` fails on `main` because of
it (queue commits skip CI, so nothing flagged it).

Now the line is kept whole when it fits, including four words; otherwise it is
cut to a contiguous part of the model's own words that fits and does not end on
"THE", "OF", "OUR" and the like. The fixed phrase remains only when nothing of
the line can fit. The two queued jobs now read `A HEDGE AROUND US` and
`A BLESSED HOME`, with their fact-check worksheets rebuilt.

## 5. Found, not fixable in code: seven channels cannot set thumbnails

65 of 169 receipts on `main` carry `thumbnail_pending: true`. For 53 of them
(31 long-forms, 22 Shorts) YouTube's answer is "The authenticated user doesn't
have permissions to upload and set custom video thumbnails". They are on seven
channels:

| Channel | Long-forms | Shorts |
|---|---|---|
| The Midnight Watch (prayer4) | 10 | 8 |
| The Family Altar (prayer5) | 7 | 5 |
| The Healing Balm (prayer6) | 3 | 3 |
| The Provision Table (prayer7) | 3 | 2 |
| The Covenant Shield (prayer8) | 3 | 2 |
| The Praise Garden (prayer9) | 3 | 1 |
| The Guided Path (prayer10) | 2 | 1 |

Those videos are public with a frame YouTube picked instead of the designed
thumbnail. YouTube grants custom thumbnails once a channel is verified by phone
(`youtube.com/verify`, signed in as that channel). No code change can do it.
The monitor already retries pending thumbnails, so they fill in after
verification. Six more receipts failed on "uploaded too many thumbnails
recently", which is a rate limit and clears by itself.

## 6. What this cannot do

Monetisation needs 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 public watch hours in 12 months
per channel (or 10 million Shorts views in 90 days). Long-forms currently get 1
to 11 views. No change to audio or titles closes that gap in a month, and
nothing here is a promise of views. Thirty near-identical uploads a day across
ten channels also carries a review risk under YouTube's inauthentic-content
policy; fewer, more distinct videos per channel is the safer direction and is a
decision for the owner.

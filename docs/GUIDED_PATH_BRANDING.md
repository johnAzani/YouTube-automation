# The Guided Path — tenth prayer channel

**Status:** registered and active since 2026-11-12 on the owner's instruction; it runs
on the same terms as the other channels. See [TENTH_CHANNEL_SETUP.md](TENTH_CHANNEL_SETUP.md)
for what is wired and what has not yet been exercised with real uploads.

## Identity

- **Name:** The Guided Path
- **Handle shown in the owner's screenshot:** `@TheGuidedPathPrayer`
- **Focus:** Christian prayers for wisdom, discernment and guidance through
  decisions and uncertain seasons, grounded in Scripture. Unlike The Provision
  Table, its main subject is discernment rather than material provision; unlike
  the time-of-day channels, it is not tied to a daily watch.
- **Scripture anchor:** Proverbs 3:5–6 (KJV).
- **Palette:** deep teal, soft cream, sage and warm gold.
- **Voice:** reassuring, thoughtful and honest; avoid claiming to know God's
  specific plan, guaranteeing outcomes, or replacing professional advice.

## Editorial direction

1. Proverbs 3:5–6 — trusting God when a decision feels unclear.
2. James 1:5 — asking God for wisdom with humility.
3. Psalm 25:4–5 — learning and waiting for guidance.
4. Proverbs 15:22 — seeking wise counsel, not treating prayer as a substitute
   for medical, legal, financial or other professional advice.

The first two topics are seeded in the launch queue; the roadmap in

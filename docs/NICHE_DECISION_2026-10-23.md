# The decision: one niche, one number, one deadline

**Date:** 23 October 2026  
**Status:** decision record. This supersedes the niche discussion in all earlier finance memos. One niche is chosen; everything else is deleted.

---

## 1. The 2-to-3-week question, answered with arithmetic

You asked to monetize in 2–3 weeks. Here is exactly what that requires, and why it does not happen from a standing start.

**The bar today:** 1,000 subscribers **plus** 4,000 public watch hours in 12 months (or 10M Shorts views in 90 days).

4,000 hours = **240,000 minutes** of watch time.

| Your format | AVD | Views needed for 4,000 h |
|---|---:|---:|
| 9 min at 50% | 4.5 min | 53,333 |
| 20 min at 40% | 8 min | **30,000** |
| 30 min at 35% | 10.5 min | 22,857 |

So in 2–3 weeks you would need roughly **30,000 views and 1,000 subscribers on a brand-new channel**, and then:

**The review alone takes time.** YouTube's own help page says a decision typically takes **about one month**. AIR Media-Tech, a YouTube partner that processes these, says **most of their channels hear back within a week**, slowest about a month. So even in the best case, the review can consume the entire 2–3 weeks on its own.

**What the fastest real cases look like:**

| Source | Fastest observed |
|---|---|
| BecomeViral case set | **30 days** (Shehzad, verified), 50 days (Robert); most common 60–120 days |
| r/NewTubers report | 10 days to meet the threshold; 1,000 subs in under 48 hours; 4,000 hours from two long videos |
| vidIQ, 9,414 channels created after 2023 | **median ~16 months** to 1,000 subscribers |
| Influencer Marketing Hub 2026 | median **11 months** to monetization |

**The honest verdict:** 2–3 weeks is not a plan. It is the tail of the distribution — a viral hit you cannot schedule, plus a fast review. Anyone telling you otherwise is selling a course. The **fastest credible window from zero is about 6 weeks**, and that requires a video that breaks out immediately.

**But there is a real deadline that gives you a genuine advantage, and it is 14 weeks away, not 3.**

YouTube doubles the entry bar on **1 February 2027**: 8,000 hours, or 20M Shorts views. Crucially, **the application date sets the bar**. An application submitted by 23:59 UTC on **31 January 2027** is reviewed against 4,000 hours — even if the decision arrives in March.

That gives you **100 days** to reach 4,000 hours at half the future requirement. That is the single most valuable deadline in this entire project, and it is the one to plan around.

## 2. The niche — one only

> ### Money-collapse documentaries, categorized as **Education**, 18–22 minutes, Tier-1 English audience, built from primary documents.

That is the whole thing. One sentence, no alternatives.

**The promise on screen:** every claim traced to a filing, a court order, an examiner's report or a central-bank statement.

**The shape of every video:** one financial machine, one moment it broke, 18–22 minutes, 2,600–3,000 words, documents visible.

## 4. Why this one, in numbers

**a) Education pays more than Business & Finance — by a lot.**

AIR Media-Tech, 300 channels, 3,595 monetized channel-months, May 2025–May 2026, read directly from Studio:

| Niche | Median RPM | P25–P75 | Studio CPM | Views for $1,000 |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|
| **Education & Science** | **$10.22** | $2.31–$19.50 | $5.91 | ~98,000 |
| Transport | $5.69 | $3.17–$9.81 | $5.88 | ~176,000 |
| Business & Finance\* | $2.01 | $0.73–$5.17 | $4.84 | ~498,000 |
| All niches | $2.30 | — | — | ~435,000 |

\*Business & Finance had only three channels in the sample — directional. But the direction is consistent with everything else: **the "finance pays the most" claim is a CPM story, not an RPM story.**

**b) Education has the best ad fill on YouTube.** 77% of Education views show an ad, versus 56% for Business & Finance and 53% platform-wide. It also carries 1.84 ads per monetized view. A view in Education is simply more likely to be paid for.

**c) The channel is already categorised correctly.** Every script in `content/queue/` carries `category: education`. That is not a detail to change — it is the single highest-leverage setting you already have right, and it is worth roughly **5× the RPM** of the finance label.

**d) Documentary length is the watch-hour engine.** At 20 minutes and 8 minutes average view duration, six videos averaging 5,000 views each produce **4,000 hours exactly**. At 9 minutes, the same six videos produce 2,250 hours — you would need twelve.

**e) The moat is the paperwork.** Every competitor I audited is narrating the same public record. Almost none show it. A channel where the examiner's report is on screen while the claim is made is the one thing a 58-day-old AI channel cannot copy cheaply.

| $250,000 | 24,461,840 | 2,038,487 | ~271,800 |
| $500,000 | 48,923,679 | 4,076,973 | ~543,600 |

For context: **1.3% of all YouTube channels have 100,000 subscribers.** 815,000 views a month, sustained for a year, is a genuine channel — not a side project. That is a 2–4 year build at the median, or a fast one if a story breaks out.

**This is the number you asked for, and it is reachable. It is not reachable in three weeks.**

## 6. The 100-day plan

The target is not "go viral." The target is **six videos averaging 5,000 views at 20 minutes** — which produces exactly 4,000 hours, and at a 5% subscribe rate on 30,000 views, about 1,500 subscribers.

| Window | What | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–10 | Pick the first story. Build the source pack. Write 2,700 words. Record. | 1 video |
| Days 11–14 | Publish. Watch retention and CTR like a hawk. | Data |
| Days 15–100 | One video every 12–14 days, same format | 6–7 videos |
| By 31 Jan 2027 | **Apply to YPP — this locks the 4,000-hour bar** | Application |
| Feb–Mar 2027 | Review, approval, first AdSense | Revenue starts |

**At ~9 hours per video, that is 54–63 hours across 14 weeks — about 4 hours a week.** It fits a real life. That is the point of the six-video plan: it is sized to what one person can actually sustain.

**The one number that decides everything: average views per video.** Not subscribers, not watch time, not uploads. If six videos average 5,000, you are monetized. If they average 2,000, you are not — you are at 1,600 hours and you need three more videos.

**The first three stories:**

| # | Story | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The 2022 nickel squeeze** — the LME cancelled $12bn of trades in a single day | Pure mechanism: margin calls, off-book shorts, an exchange with the power to press undo. Under-covered: top explainers sit at 222K–227K, not millions |
| 2 | **The LDI gilt crisis** — UK pension funds forced the Bank of England to buy bonds | The least-told systemic story of the decade; near-zero explainer competition; Tier-1 UK audience |
| 3 | **Greensill** — supply-chain finance, insurance and access | Documents-rich, mechanism-rich, still resolving. Best existing explainers: 129K–222K |

Not Barings. Not Lehman. Not LTCM — those are owned by people with 1–4M views on the same story.

## 7. The honest odds

If I had to bet on **six videos from a standing start clearing 4,000 hours by 31 January 2027**, I would put it at roughly **one in five**.

That is my judgement, not a measurement. It is not zero — the arithmetic is genuinely achievable, and 5,000 average views is modest in a niche where the mid-tier gets 17,000–129,000. But most new channels do not average 5,000 in their first six uploads. That is the base rate, and it is the reason this plan is sized to fail cheaply: six videos, 60 hours, no money spent.

**What the one-in-five buys:** if it works, you are monetized for the rest of the channel's life at the easier threshold, and you are one breakout away from real money. If it fails, you have six documentaries and a repeatable method, and you try again with the 8,000-hour bar.

---

## 8. What would make me change this

- **If the first video gets under 500 views**, the problem is packaging, not topic. Stop producing and fix thumbnails, titles and the first 30 seconds before video two.
- **If retention at 20 minutes is under 30%**, the writing cannot carry the length. Cut to 12–14 minutes and accept needing more views.
- **If two videos in a row get under 1,000 views**, the format is not working. Rebuild the hook before spending another 9 hours.
- **If a competitor with a real brand covers the same mechanism story**, pick a different mechanism. Never be the lesser version of an existing video.

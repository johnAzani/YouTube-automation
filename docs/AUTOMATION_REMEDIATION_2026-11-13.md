# Automation remediation — 13 November 2026

This is branch work, not a deployed change to the production schedule. Do not dispatch or cancel live jobs to test it. At 06:27 UTC the current production run had nine successful channel jobs and prayer10 in progress; the next run was pending. A read-only fetch at 06:21 UTC found that live main had moved the 0404, 0504 and 0601 long-form jobs into published receipts and added two Short receipts since the branch base; this is repository evidence, not an independent YouTube verification. The running matrix must be reconciled with YouTube and its receipts before any manual recovery.

## Implemented on this branch

- **Production watchdog:** only the production workflow is eligible for automatic cancellation under this policy. Fetch all job pages; require at least one active job and require every active job to have a valid start time more than 250 minutes old. The production job cap is 240 minutes. A fresh leg, queued-only matrix, missing/invalid job timestamps or failed API lookup cannot justify cancellation. Other workflows are deliberately not auto-cancelled until their own timeout/matrix policies are designed.
- **Voice lock:** long-form and authored prayer Shorts use Gemini Charon or fail closed. Audio caches require voice/model/text and audio-integrity provenance. Legacy prayer Shorts without verified provenance and pre-rendered prayer Shorts cannot be published through this path. New receipts record the verified voice/model; recovery receipts do not invent provenance. The first run after this change may regenerate old unlabelled cached audio and spend free quota. When quota or Gemini is unavailable, the affected release can pause or fail instead of publishing a different voice.
- **Future prayer Shorts:** the central karaoke-style cards are generated from every narration word rather than abbreviated kinetic phrases, with timing aligned to the matching audio. Production refuses missing or incomplete local alignment rather than publishing guessed timings; dry-run preview may still use estimated timings. Stock searches rotate candidates per edition/scene, avoid already selected footage and fingerprints from the last three recorded Shorts on that channel, and production requires at least two distinct licensed video clips both before and after rendering. Older receipts have no fingerprints, so earlier footage cannot be excluded from this new history. Unavailable alignment or sufficiently varied footage leaves the Short queued; it does not change past uploads.
- **Thumbnail, recovery and replacement safety:** new long-form receipts record the uploader's actual thumbnail result, and Short receipts assess the thumbnail for their own video ID only. Long-form recovery verifies channel ownership, title, privacy, timestamp and upload status before changing queue state; it records remote facts without claiming unverified narration, metadata or thumbnails. A matching title and owner still do not prove exactly-once identity. Short replacements no longer automatically delete legacy videos before confirming a new upload and writing a receipt; rejected uploads keep the old videos and queue state for review. Dashboard cards show receipt-based pending counts, not verified current live state. No thumbnail API calls, video deletions or permission changes were made by this branch work.
- **Commit race guard:** before each production commit attempt, compare each touched/deleted content path at the run's starting commit and newly fetched main. Conflicting changes abort instead of being overwritten; identical existing state is allowed. Pending content is copied into the short-lived run report for reconciliation. A conflict after an upload must be reconciled before retrying; this guard is not an idempotency key and does not authorize parallelism.
- **Observability:** monitor issue heading no longer implies every channel is blocked. Dashboard shows the monitor snapshot timestamp in Nigeria time, omits the invented zero-missed-slots claim, labels receipt counts and editorial Short targets honestly, and treats missing monitoring state as unhealthy. Neither a failed metrics snapshot nor a failed feedback computation can commit an old checkout's report over newer feedback. Metrics snapshot verifies an expected channel ID when configured and warns when the identity cannot be verified.
- **Feedback quality:** aggregate-only diagnostics distinguish snapshot IDs, receipt IDs, matches and below-threshold / missing-view-count / too-young videos. New snapshots preserve a missing view count as unknown rather than zero. Only long-form videos at least three days old at the snapshot date with at least 25 recorded views contribute to measured scores or guidance; unmeasured themes cannot be called the best or worst measured theme. This is a confidence gate, not a promise of growth or YouTube analytics verification.
- **Queue safety:** expired quota pauses still pass duplicate and minimum-length checks; a published job ID blocks reuse even with a different slug or unreadable receipt. Explicit `--all` processes its full selection, and `--next` stays limited to one new release while continuing past verified duplicate recovery. The YouTube title check now scans the uploads playlist through at most 20 pages (up to 1,000 entries), checks the configured channel identity, and fails closed on lookup errors, malformed entries, pagination loops, incomplete scans, or multiple matching videos. Long-form prechecks also fail before narration unless the explicit duplicate override is enabled; that override remains available for deliberate replacements and bypasses the title guard. Each scan costs one channel request plus up to 20 playlist requests, and production can perform more than one scan. This can delay releases during API outages or for larger channels and is not exactly-once delivery. Guided Path's time values are quoted and dashboard parsing rejects invalid time values. Three planned Morning Altar roadmap entries restore branch headroom from 14 to 17; they are not approved or published scripts.

## Not yet complete

1. **Capacity:** production remains a serial matrix. Do not set `max-parallel` above one while each leg's commit step can stage shared `content/` state. The current changes make cancellation safer but do not fix throughput or guarantee slots.
2. **Parallelism design:** the new path-level commit guard is a first step, not the entire design. Give each channel an isolated workspace and stage only its own receipt, queue files and approved generated artifacts; handle compare-and-swap retries and prove that retries cannot restore deleted queue files or overwrite another leg's receipt. Upload idempotency must survive a process dying after YouTube accepts an upload but before the receipt is committed. Use mocked concurrent channel tests, then a non-uploading rehearsal before considering bounded production parallelism. In particular, the bounded title check is not an idempotency key: simultaneous runs can both observe no match, and titles can change or collide. It cannot alone resolve an upload accepted before its receipt was committed.
3. **Live reconciliation and thumbnails:** inspect the actual YouTube state and current run outcomes channel by channel. Permission errors, rate limits and invalid posters need different remedies. Respect existing backoffs and never assume a 403 proves a particular verification or OAuth issue.
4. **Feedback diagnosis:** under the previously deployed code, the eight checked-in 12 November reports had 1–17 snapshot/receipt-matched long-form videos per channel, but zero with a recorded snapshot view count of at least 25. Thus the neutral result is consistent with the existing threshold, not an absence of joins. The collector also defaults a missing view count to zero, so these reports alone cannot prove actual live views. All eight historical snapshot and feedback steps reported success. The artifact download redirected to a host inaccessible from this environment, so the raw snapshots could not be independently verified. Inspect the short-lived artifacts and current YouTube analytics when available; do not fabricate engagement or watch-hour numbers.
5. **Verification and rollout:** the complete dependency-enabled suite including the Short caption and footage changes passed: 1,421 tests and 4,022 subtests (630 warnings, predominantly Pillow deprecations), in 730 seconds. Focused queue, supervisor, voice, dashboard, monitor, feedback, duplicate lookup, recovery, commit-race and thumbnail tests also pass; the Morning Altar headroom test now passes. Review against the latest main and reconcile its live content changes, then decide whether to merge/deploy. Observe one safe cycle and verify each channel's outcomes; rollback must not delete receipts or republish content.

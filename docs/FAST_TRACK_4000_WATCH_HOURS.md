## 2. YouTube Partner Program (YPP) Watch-Hour Policy Rules (2026 Audit)

To guarantee that every second of accumulated watch time counts toward your monetization application, the content must strictly adhere to YouTube's 2026 eligibility criteria:

### 2.1 What COUNTS Toward 4,000 Watch Hours:
1. **Public Long-Form Videos:** Any standard horizontal (16:9 widescreen) public video on YouTube.
2. **Public Live Streams:** Watch time generated by viewers during public live streams and subsequent live replay VODs.
3. **Embedded Players:** Watch time from viewers watching embedded videos on external websites (such as our GitHub Pages review portal) counts as long as the YouTube video is set to **Public**.

### 2.2 What DOES NOT Count (Critical Pitfalls to Avoid):
1. **YouTube Shorts:** Watch hours generated from views in the **Shorts Feed DO NOT count** toward the 4,000-hour bar. (Shorts only count toward the alternate 10M views in 90 days threshold).
2. **Unlisted & Private Videos:** Videos set to Unlisted or Private generate zero qualifying watch time.
3. **Deleted Videos:** If a video that earned 1,000 watch hours is deleted, YouTube automatically deducts those 1,000 hours from your YPP total.
4. **Ad Campaigns (Google Ads):** Views bought via Google Ads (Discovery or In-Stream) do NOT count toward the 4,000 organic hours.

## 2. Step-by-Step Account Setup (3 Minutes)

### Step 1: Create Your Free Creator Account
1. Visit **[selar.com](https://selar.com)** or **[selar.co](https://selar.co)**.
2. Click **"Start Selling"** (free tier).
3. Set your store username to: `thesecretplace`
   * Your Show Love link is: `https://selar.com/showlove/thesecretplace`
   * Your store URL is: `https://selar.co/m/thesecretplace`

### Step 2: Configure Your Payout Account (First Bank Domiciliary)
1. In your Selar Dashboard, navigate to **Settings $\rightarrow$ Bank Details / Payout Settings**.
2. Select **Nigeria**.
3. Choose your preferred payout option:
   * **Option A (USD Direct):** Add your **First Bank USD Domiciliary Account** Number.
   * **Option B (Instant Local Naira):** You can also add your regular Nigerian Naira bank account (First Bank, GTB, Zenith, etc.) if you want instant local Naira settlements at prevailing rates.
4. Verify your account name (must match your BVN/NIN for automated regulatory clearance).

#### How to Add Each Product in Selar:
1. Click **Add Product $\rightarrow$ Digital Product**.
2. Copy the Title, Subtitle, and Description from `content/products/<product_id>.json`.
3. Upload the corresponding square cover image (`content/products/cover_*.png`).
4. Under **Pricing**:
   * Check **"Pay What You Want"** or **"Show Strike-through Price"**.
   * Set Minimum Price to **$0** (Free access for any believer in financial distress).
   * Set Suggested Price to **$5.00 USD**.
5. Upload the audio or PDF file.
6. Click **Save Product**.

# The Praise Garden — ninth prayer channel brand kit

**Status:** registered and active since 2026-11-12 on the owner's instruction; it runs
on the same terms as the other channels. See [NINTH_CHANNEL_SETUP.md](NINTH_CHANNEL_SETUP.md)
for what is wired and what has not yet been exercised with real uploads.

## Identity

- **Name:** The Praise Garden
- **Handle shown in the owner's screenshot:** `@ThePraiseGardenDaily`
- **Distinct focus:** Christian prayers of thanksgiving, praise and worship,
  including gratitude in ordinary life and in difficult seasons. Unlike the
  Morning Altar, it is not tied to a time of day; unlike the Provision Table, it
  is not primarily about receiving material provision.
- **Scripture anchor:** Psalm 100:4 (KJV).
- **Palette:** terracotta `#A95836`, sage `#77866A`, cream `#FFF8E9`, dark garden `#1B271A`.
- **Visual motif:** a brass lamp and garden foliage; warm natural light, calm
  and hopeful, never a promise of guaranteed outcomes.
- **Voice:** reverent, gentle, emotionally honest, varied and welcoming.

## Editorial direction

Each episode should make a distinct contribution and use careful context for the
scripture, not interchangeable scripts. Draft topics for later human review:

1. Psalm 100:4 — entering God's presence with thanksgiving.
2. Psalm 103:1–5 — remembering God's compassion and benefits.
3. Habakkuk 3:17–19 — praise amid hardship without denying grief.
4. Philippians 4:6–7 — thanksgiving in prayer amid anxiety, without treating
   prayer as a replacement for medical or mental-health care.

The first two topics are seeded in the launch queue; the roadmap in
`scripts/continuous_research.py` carries all thirty. Scripts, quotations and
visual/audio rights still deserve human review.
AI-generated assets and automated workflows do not by themselves establish
originality or guarantee monetization. See [the monetization action plan](MONETIZATION_ACTION_PLAN.md).

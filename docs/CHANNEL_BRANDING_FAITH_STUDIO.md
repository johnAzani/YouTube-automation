## 5. Editorial Video Formulas & Studio Scripts

### A. The 60-Second Short Prayer Formula (Viral Growth & Subscriber Engine)
* **0:00 – 0:03 (The Scroll-Stopping Hook):**  
  *"Stop scrolling for 30 seconds. God wants you to hear this message today."* or  
  *"Before you sleep tonight, let's place your family under Psalm 91 protection."*
* **0:04 – 0:35 (The Word & Declaration):**  
  Speak directly to a universal human struggle (anxiety, loneliness, financial fear, health). Recite a powerful scripture verse with on-screen kinetic golden typography.
* **0:36 – 0:52 (The Pastoral Prayer):**  
  A warm, personal prayer inviting God's peace, protection, and breakthrough.
* **0:53 – 0:60 (The Engagement CTA):**  
  *"If you receive this blessing, type AMEN in the comments and share this with someone who needs God's peace today. Subscribe to pray with us tomorrow."*

### B. The 10–15 Minute Morning Devotional & Prayer (AdSense & Daily Habit Engine)
* **Part 1 (The Exposition — 2 to 3 mins):**  
  Theological and historical context. Why this scripture matters today.
* **Part 2 (The Guided Prayer — 5 to 8 mins):**  
  Slow, reverent, pastoral prayer covering family, protection, peace, and health.
* **Part 3 (The Scripture Affirmation — 2 to 4 mins):**  
  Calm repetition of key verses with gentle musical pads.

### C. The 1–3 Hour Sleep & Meditation Stream (Watch-Hour Engine)
* Calm, low-register narration paced with natural pauses.
* Ambient **432Hz/528Hz peaceful synthesized audio beds** (generated natively in `tools/score.py` with zero Content ID exposure).
* Repeating cycles of Psalms (Psalm 91, Psalm 23, Psalm 121, Psalm 46, Isaiah 40).
* Generates **30 to 50+ minutes average view duration per viewer**.

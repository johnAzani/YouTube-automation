## D. Unresolved questions are bounded, not hidden

- **Rescue counterfactual:** primary testimony/commission accounts disagree. Full legal/valuation reconstruction is not completed; revised script acknowledges dispute.
- **Technical accounting adjudication:** the inspected examiner summary explicitly avoids resolving SFAS 140 technical compliance. This draft does not decide it either.
- **Legal cases:** no blanket claim about all criminal/civil actions. Detailed case outcomes require final case documents; the NYAG settlement is not a universal judgment.
- **Current estate totals:** the script uses explicitly dated 2022/2025 entity outcomes. No 2026 all-estate total is asserted.
- **Visual evidence:** historical query coverage is not verification of the final assets or the final cut. Human review must check captions, identities and potentially misleading illustrative images.
- **Source access:** partial reads and failed direct fetches are listed in the dossier; a search excerpt is never described as an independently audited full document.

A stronger script does not close the human gate. The narration remains a draft, all worksheet certifications remain blank, and no upload/publication is authorized by these files.

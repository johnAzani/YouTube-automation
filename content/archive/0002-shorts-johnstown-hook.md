## Scene 1 — Hook
In 1889, a dam collapsed and killed two thousand two hundred and nine people in under
an hour. The rain that night was ordinary.

## Scene 2 — The twist
Rich men had rebuilt the dam's overflow so their private lake wouldn't drain. When the
storm came, the water had nowhere to go.

## Scene 3 — The verdict
The courts ruled it an "Act of God." Nobody went to prison. Full story on the channel.

## Scene 1 — The First Light and the Cry for Guidance

The room is quiet around you at this early hour, before the demands of the day have begun to crowd your mind and pull your thoughts in a dozen different directions. You wake up carrying questions about the hours ahead — decisions that need to be made, crossroads where the path is not clear, and a quiet longing to know that you are walking in the right direction. This morning is an altar of beginning, a place to lay down the anxiety of choosing wrongly and to ask the One who holds the morning to take your hand. The light is rising over the hills, piercing the dawn shadows, reminding us that God is already at work before we take our first step.

In the book of the prophet Isaiah, chapter thirty, verse twenty-one, the Word of God gives us this profound promise for every journey: And thine ears shall hear a word behind thee, saying, This is the way, walk ye in it, when ye turn to the right hand, and when ye turn to the left. 

Let us ponder this marvellous image together. Matthew Henry, in his commentary on these prophetic writings, notes that God's grace does not leave His people to wander in confusion, but provides both an inward persuasion and an outward providence to guide our steps. When we are uncertain whether to turn right or left in our work, our relationships, or our daily responsibilities, the promise is not that we will see the entire map of the year ahead, but that we will hear a voice behind us at the exact moment of decision. That is the comfort of the morning altar. We do not need to figure everything out before we get out of bed; we only need to commit our steps to the Lord who promises to guide us in real time.

There is one more thing this opening movement asks of us before we look closer at our daily walk, and that is to quiet the noise that so often drowns out the gentle promptings of the Spirit. Stay with this thought as we move into the second moment of our morning prayer.

## Scene 2 — Mercies That Meet Us at the Dawn

Yesterday brought its own set of burdens, and perhaps some of those choices left you wondering if you missed a turn along the way. But the mercy of God is renewed with every sunrise, offering a clean slate and a fresh start. As the dawn breaks across the open sky, we are reminded that His faithfulness is not dependent upon our flawless navigation, but upon His steadfast love. When we turn to Him in the early hours, we acknowledge that we are dependent creatures who need divine wisdom to guide our minds and steady our hearts.

Let us return to the scripture of this morning, holding fast to the promise given through the prophet: And thine ears shall hear a word behind thee, saying, This is the way, walk ye in it, when ye turn to the right hand, and when ye turn to the left.

Think of what it means for a voice to speak from behind. It is the voice of a faithful shepherd who follows close behind His flock, gently correcting our course when we begin to drift off the narrow path. We often wish God would show us ten miles down the road so we could plan our security and avoid every discomfort. Yet His design is far more tender: He gives us grace for the step we are taking right now. As Charles Spurgeon often observed in his meditations on divine guidance, the Lord leads His people step by step so that they may learn to trust Him continually rather than trusting in their own foresight.

Let this truth sink deep into your spirit as the morning light fills your room. You are not walking into this day alone, and you are not expected to manufacture your own wisdom. 

## Scene 3 — Trusting God with the Decisions of Today

The hours ahead hold meetings, conversations, financial choices, and interactions with people whose responses you cannot control. It is easy for the human heart to grip these responsibilities with white-knuckled anxiety, trying to orchestrate every outcome. But the invitation of the morning is to unclench our fists and place our schedule into the hands of the Maker of heaven and earth. To acknowledge Him in all our ways means bringing every small detail of the morning and the afternoon into His presence before we ever step out the door.

Let us ponder again the words written for our instruction: And thine ears shall hear a word behind thee, saying, This is the way, walk ye in it, when ye turn to the right hand, and when ye turn to the left.

Notice the gentle realism in that verse. It speaks of turning to the right hand and to the left — acknowledging that we live in a world of choices, alternatives, and potential missteps. God does not condemn us for facing decisions; rather, He provides the internal compass of His Spirit and the external counsel of His Word to keep us anchored. When a difficult choice arises today — whether in a workspace, a family conversation, or a moment of sudden pressure — pause for thirty seconds. Let your spirit quiet down, and listen for that still, small voice that whispers the direction of peace and truth.

There is a profound protection in obeying that whisper, and it leads us directly into the fourth movement of our morning prayer.

## Scene 4 — Favour, Shield, and Protection for the Hours Ahead

As we step out into the rushing current of the day, we need more than good intentions; we need the protective shield of God's presence surrounding our lives. The world outside our doors can be loud, demanding, and contrary to the peace of Christ. Yet the believer who begins the morning at the altar carries an invisible armour — the assurance that the Lord goes before us to prepare the way and stands behind us as a guard. His favour is not a badge of our own worthiness, but the gracious canopy under which we live and move and have our being.

Let us anchor our hearts once more in the scripture: And thine ears shall hear a word behind thee, saying, This is the way, walk ye in it, when ye turn to the right hand, and when ye turn to the left.

Matthew Henry reminds us in his exposition that divine guidance and divine protection go hand in hand. The God who directs our path is also the God who defends our soul from the snares set by our own impatience or the malice of the enemy. When you feel pressured to rush into a decision or compromise your integrity for the sake of speed, let this verse rise in your memory. Let it be the gentle check in your spirit that says: stop, wait, listen, and follow the Master's pace. You do not have to run anxiously when the Creator of the universe is leading your steps.

Let us now turn our focus toward the people and the labour that fill our daily lives.

## Scene 5 — Blessing Over Work, Family, and Home

Every listener waking up to this prayer carries a circle of love — a family, a home, colleagues, neighbours, and friends whose names are written on your heart. The blessing of a new morning is never meant to be kept to ourselves; it overflows from the altar into our homes and workplaces. When we ask God to establish the work of our hands today, we are inviting His kingdom to touch the ordinary tasks before us — the emails, the conversations, the meals prepared, the care given to children or aging parents.

Let us speak the promise over our homes and our labour today: And thine ears shall hear a word behind thee, saying, This is the way, walk ye in it, when ye turn to the right hand, and when ye turn to the left.

Imagine the peace that would fill our homes if every member walked in conscious dependence upon this divine guidance. Families are held together not by human perfection, but by the daily grace of forgiving one another, listening to one another, and seeking God's wisdom together. As you go out into your community today, let your presence be marked by a quiet steadiness that comes from having spent this time at the morning altar. You carry the fragrance of Christ into rooms that are desperate for His peace.

We are now arriving at the final movement of our morning journey, where we receive our commission for the day.

## Scene 6 — The Sending Benediction and Sunrise Commission

The morning sun has now fully risen, casting its golden light across the landscape and marking the official beginning of the active day. You are fully awake, surrounded by the mercies of God, equipped with His promise, and covered by His protection. There is no need to fear what the hours hold, because the One who called you to this prayer is already walking ahead of you into every room you will enter. Take a deep breath, release any remaining tension in your shoulders, and receive this blessing as you cross the threshold of your day.

Let us seal our hearts with the final repetition of our morning scripture: And thine ears shall hear a word behind thee, saying, This is the way, walk ye in it, when ye turn to the right hand, and when ye turn to the left.

Heavenly Father, we thank You for the gift of this new sunrise and for the unfailing promise of Your guidance. As we step out into the duties, decisions, and encounters of this day, grant us attentive ears to hear Your voice behind us. When we face crossroads where we do not know whether to turn to the right hand or the left, let Your Spirit whisper clarity and peace to our souls. Protect our minds from anxiety, shield our families from harm, and establish the work of our hands for Your glory. May Your grace go before us, beside us, and behind us today and always.

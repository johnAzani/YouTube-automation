## Scene 1 — Bringing the Decision to the Lord

There are days when the road ahead splits into three directions, and none of them show a sign. You sit with papers on a table, or stare through a rain-streaked window, turning the same question over in your mind until the very facts begin to blur. It is the quiet ache of standing at a crossroad without a map, wanting only to move in the right direction, yet afraid of taking a false step that might cost you years. We come to God in these moments not with a polished speech or a list of demands, but with the simple weariness of those who have reached the limit of their own understanding. We bring our decisions, our confusion, and our hesitation straight to the throne of grace, acknowledging that we do not know how to steer our own course. The Apostle Paul writes in the eighth chapter of Romans, reminding us of the deep relationship that sustains the believer in every season of uncertainty. For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, they are the sons of God. Lord God, You see the crossroads before us tonight. You know the weight of this choice and the limits of our foresight. We lay down our striving and our anxious calculations, asking simply for Your wisdom. When our own thoughts are troubled and the counsel of friends leaves us still divided, grant us the quiet assurance of Your presence. Teach us to look away from the noise of our own fears and to wait upon Your gentle leading, trusting that You care for the path of Your children.

## Scene 2 — Asking God for Wisdom

When we lack discernment, James tells us that we may ask of God, who gives to all men liberally and upbraideth not. Yet asking often feels like speaking into an empty room until we learn the posture of true reception. Matthew Henry, in his commentary on the Word, notes that divine guidance is not granted to feed our curiosity or to spare us from the discipline of faith, but to guide our steps in paths of righteousness for His name's sake. To be led by the Spirit is not to possess an exhaustive blueprint of tomorrow; it is to possess a living guide for today. It is the steady cultivation of a teachable heart that listens for the whisper behind us saying, This is the way, walk ye in it. Father, we confess how quickly we lean toward our own habits, choosing the path of least resistance or the one that flatters our desires. Forgive us for treating Your wisdom as a last resort rather than our first shelter. Grant us the humility to receive instruction, even when Your direction runs counter to our plans. We ask not for wealth or ease, but for an understanding heart that can discern between good and evil, truth and illusion. As we open Your Word, steady our restless thoughts and tune our ears to the quiet voice of Your Spirit, that we may walk without stumbling through the fog of our present trials.

## Scene 3 — The Lamp of the Word

The Scripture does not always illuminate the entire valley; most often, it provides a light bright enough for the next single step. When the path is shrouded in mist, we are tempted to demand a sunrise before we will move our feet. Yet the Psalmist declares that Your word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path — a small, steady flame held close to the ground, showing us only what we need to see right now. To be led by the Spirit is to trust that the light will not fail as we advance in obedience. It requires us to leave behind the demand for guarantees and to accept the companionship of God in the dark. Lord, when the darkness around our circumstances makes us hesitant to move, remind us of the sufficiency of Your Word. Let Your truth cut through the confusion of conflicting voices and steady our trembling resolve. We do not ask to see the end from the beginning, but simply for the grace to be faithful in this present hour. Keep our feet from slipping, and when we are tempted to rush ahead of Your timing or lag behind in fear, draw us back into step with Your grace.

## Scene 4 — Wise Counsel and Tested Motives

The search for direction is rarely a solitary endeavor. Scripture tells us that in the multitude of counsellors there is safety, yet it also warns us to examine the motives of our own hearts before we lean upon human advice. James reminds us that the wisdom that is from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, and easy to be entreated. When we bring our decisions into the light of God's presence, He exposes the hidden corners of ambition, pride, or fear that so often distort our judgment. We remember that prayer walks alongside the good counsel of mature believers, the sober facts of our circumstances, and the ordinary responsibilities we carry. Father, search our hearts today and try our thoughts. If there is a selfish motive hiding beneath our desire for change, or a stubborn pride that refuses to submit to godly advice, lay it bare before us. Deliver us from the counsel of expediency and self-interest. Surround us with those who speak truth in love, and give us the discernment to weigh every opinion against the standard of Your holy Word. We ask that our decisions may honor Your name and bring peace to our households, free from the turbulence of hurried choices.

## Scene 5 — Waiting With Patience and Ordered Steps

There are seasons when the hardest part of guidance is not moving forward, but standing still while the Lord works out His purposes in the quiet. The steps of a good man are ordered by the Lord, and He delighteth in his way. Yet ordering steps often involves delays, closed doors, and long afternoons of waiting where nothing seems to change. It is in these quiet intervals that our impatience rises, whispering that God has forgotten our address or grown indifferent to our plight. But the Spirit who leads us is also the One who teaches us patience, cultivating a quiet resilience that outlasts our doubts. Lord, we bring our impatience to You. When the waiting stretches longer than we hoped, and the silence from heaven feels heavy, keep our hearts from despair. Teach us the quiet art of abiding in Christ, knowing that delays are not denials and that Your timing is perfect. If we must wait, grant us grace to wait without bitterness. If we must move, grant us courage to move without hesitation. Hold us steady in the palm of Your hand when our own strength is spent, and let Your presence be our sufficient reward.

## Scene 6 — Committing the Way and Closing Benediction

Commit thy way unto the Lord; trust also in him; and he shall bring it to pass. We come to the end of our asking with our hands open and our plans surrendered. Whatever lies on the other side of this prayer — whether a sudden clarity or a long season of quiet faithfulness — we leave the outcome in Your hands, knowing that those who are led by the Spirit of God are kept by the power of God. Father, we commit our families, our work, and our future into Your care. Go before us into tomorrow, smoothing the rough places and guarding us against our own errors. Let Your peace, which passes all human understanding, garrison our hearts and minds as we rest in Your sovereign goodness.

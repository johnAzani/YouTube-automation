## Scene 1 — Peace I Leave with You (Verse 27a)

Jesus leaves a legacy: not wealth, not fame, but peace.

Peace I leave with you, my peace I give unto you.

This is not temporary peace. It is peace that outlasts every crisis.

Receive His peace as your inheritance tonight.
Heavenly Father, as I prepare to rest, I gratefully receive the incredible inheritance of peace that Jesus, Your Son, has left for me. I acknowledge that this peace is not merely a fleeting feeling, but a deep, abiding tranquility that transcends all earthly understanding and circumstances. Tonight, I open my heart to this divine legacy, allowing it to settle deeply within my spirit, knowing it is a gift that will endure beyond any present or future challenge. May this peace guard my thoughts and quiet my soul, assuring me of Your constant presence and unwavering care throughout the night. I pray that this divine peace will permeate every fiber of my being, calming my restless spirit and silencing the anxious voices that may linger from the day. Let it be a deep wellspring of quiet confidence, sustaining me through the hours of darkness and preparing my heart to greet the new day with renewed strength and spiritual serenity, fully trusting in Your benevolent watch over me.

## Scene 2 — My Peace I Give Unto You (Verse 27b)

His peace is different from the world's peace.

My peace I give unto you: not as the world giveth, give I unto you.

The world's peace depends on circumstances. His peace depends on Him.

World peace disappears when trouble comes. His peace grows stronger in trouble.
Lord, I recognize the distinct nature of Your peace, a profound contrast to the conditional calm offered by the world. While the world's peace is fragile, easily shattered by external pressures and unforeseen trials, Your peace originates from an unshakeable source: Your eternal presence and unfailing love. As I lie down, I choose to surrender my anxieties, trusting that this divine peace will not only sustain me through any difficulty but will actually deepen and strengthen within me, empowering me to face tomorrow with quiet confidence, regardless of what it may hold. I ask that this extraordinary peace would settle every unsettled thought, quieting the clamor of the world and attuning my spirit to Your gentle whispers. May it be a powerful shield against all worldly distractions and anxieties, allowing me to rest profoundly in Your unwavering faithfulness. Let this truth deepen my sense of security, knowing Your perfect peace is my constant companion.

## Scene 3 — Not as the World Giveth, Give I Unto You (Verse 27c)

Compare and contrast: the world gives conditionally; God gives unconditionally.

The world gives what can be taken away. God gives what cannot be taken.

His peace is permanent. His peace is sufficient. His peace is yours.

Sleep in peace that the world cannot give and cannot take away.
Gracious God, I reflect on the incomparable gift of Your peace, given without condition, unlike anything the temporal world can offer. The world's offerings are always subject to change, loss, or destruction, but Your peace, dear Lord, is an eternal promise, a spiritual anchor that remains steadfast. Tonight, I fully embrace this permanent, sufficient peace that is unequivocally mine through Christ. I pray that this profound assurance will envelop me completely, safeguarding my sleep from any external disturbance or internal worry, allowing me to rest securely in the knowledge that Your gift is forever beyond the reach of any worldly force. I thank You, Father, that Your gift of peace is not a fleeting comfort, but an eternal promise sealed by Your Son's sacrifice, freely bestowed upon me. Let this understanding deepen within me, allowing me to release all attempts to control or worry, as I fully trust in the sufficiency of Your peace. May this divine calm transform my sleeping hours into a true sanctuary of Your unwavering love.

## Scene 4 — Let Not Your Heart Be Troubled (Verse 27d)

A troubled heart is a restless heart. A restless heart cannot sleep.

Let not your heart be troubled.

This is a command backed by a promise: because He gives peace, your heart does not have to be troubled.

Choose untroubled rest tonight.
Merciful Father, I hear the loving command to let not my heart be troubled, and I accept it as a divine invitation to tranquility. I confess that sometimes my heart feels restless, burdened by concerns of the day or worries about the future. But tonight, I lean into the certainty of Your promise: because You have given Your perfect peace, I do not have to carry these burdens into my sleep. I deliberately choose to release every anxious thought, every unsettling emotion, and every lingering fear into Your capable hands, trusting Your boundless power to transform my restless heart into a haven of deep, undisturbed peace for the night. I surrender every burden, every concern, every weight that seeks to trouble my inner being. Let Your perfect peace, which surpasses all understanding, stand guard over my mind and heart in Christ Jesus. I choose to trust implicitly in Your sovereignty, knowing that You are orchestrating all things for my good, even as I sleep. Grant me the sweet repose that only Your divine tranquility can provide.

## Scene 5 — Neither Let It Be Afraid (Verse 27e)

Fear and peace cannot occupy the same heart.

Neither let it be afraid.

When fear knocks, answer with the peace of Christ.

Your heart is a sanctuary. Keep it free from fear.
Almighty God, I stand firm on the truth that fear and Your peace cannot coexist within the same heart. I reject the whispers of anxiety and apprehension that try to disturb my serenity. Tonight, as I close my eyes, I declare that my heart is a sanctuary consecrated by Your presence, a sacred space where fear has no dwelling. Should any shadow of dread approach, I will not open the door, but instead, I will answer with the unwavering, perfect peace of Christ, knowing that Your calming presence casts out all fear. I yield my spirit to Your protection, trusting You to keep my mind quiet and my body at rest, free from all alarms. I declare that no anxious thought, no fearful image, no lingering shadow of apprehension will disturb my sleep. Your presence is my ultimate protection, a fortress around my soul. I rest in the absolute assurance that Your love casts out all fear, enveloping me in a cocoon of safety. May I awaken refreshed and renewed, my heart still filled with the profound and unshakable peace of Christ.

## Scene 1 — The Sunrise Hook and Directing the Prayer

The coffee is poured, the windowpane holds the first grey light of dawn, and before the noise of the street begins to rise, you find yourself sitting in the quiet of an early room. There are years behind you that have tested your faith, and there are hours ahead that will demand your strength, your patience, and your focus. In this quiet hour of morning worship, we turn our faces toward the One who holds the keys of the morning. We do not rush into the traffic or the tasks of the day without first directing our voice to the Creator of the day, looking up with an honest heart and an open spirit. 

The psalmist knew this posture well, writing from a season of life where every day was a gift from a faithful God. Let us hear the ancient promise recorded in the seventy-first Psalm, verse fifteen:

My mouth shall show forth thy righteousness and thy salvation all the day; for I know not the numbers thereof.

Here is the anchor for our day with God. Not our own strength, not our own clever plans, but His righteousness declared from the very first light of dawn. Matthew Henry reminds us in his exposition that those who have been upheld by God through many years have the greatest reason to speak of His faithfulness. When the morning arrives, we are invited to set the tone of our hearts not by our anxieties, but by His unchanging character. Lord God, as the sun breaks over the horizon this morning, we bring our voices to You. We acknowledge that every breath we draw today is a gift from Your hand. Before we look at a screen, before we open an email, before we speak to another human being, we declare that Your righteousness is our shelter and Your salvation is our song. Direct our steps in this hour.

## Scene 2 — New Mercies and Faithfulness at Dawn

The night has passed, and with it, the failures and weariness of yesterday. The sun rises on a fresh page written by the hand of grace. Every morning brings a renewal of God's covenant mercies, stretching out wider than our mistakes and deeper than our doubts. When we begin our day with God, we are stepping into a stream of faithfulness that has flowed since the beginning of time. We do not come to Him this morning because our record is spotless; we come because His mercy is new.

The words of the scripture echo in our ears, calling us to a daily rhythm of trust:

My mouth shall show forth thy righteousness and thy salvation all the day; for I know not the numbers thereof.

Think of the phrase all the day. Not just in the quiet sanctuary of the early morning hours, but when the phone rings with difficult news, when the schedule gets crowded, when the unexpected friction of human life enters our workspace. Charles Spurgeon notes that the believer's tongue should be a ready writer, quick to magnify the Lord when the path is smooth and equally ready to trust Him when the shadows lengthen. We do not know the number of our days, nor do we know what hours of testing or rejoicing lie between sunrise and sunset. But we know the One who numbers them. 

Heavenly Father, we confess that our minds often drift toward worry before they drift toward worship. Forgive us for taking the weight of today upon our own shoulders. Today, we choose to rest in Your unfailing kindness. Let our mouths be filled with Your praise. When frustration rises in our hearts during the middle hours of the day, bring these words back to our memory. Remind us that Your salvation is complete and Your righteousness does not depend on our performance.

## Scene 3 — Trust and Guidance for the Decisions Ahead

The hours ahead hold decisions we have not yet made, conversations we cannot yet predict, and moments where we will have to choose between our own impulses and the gentle guidance of the Spirit. It is easy to step out into the morning confident in our own experience, leaning on our own understanding. Yet the wisdom of the scripture points us away from self-reliance and toward the continuous, living guidance of the Almighty.

Listen again to the testimony of the psalmist:

My mouth shall show forth thy righteousness and thy salvation all the day; for I know not the numbers thereof.

To speak of His righteousness all the day is to walk through our daily work with an inner awareness of His presence. It means that when a difficult choice presents itself at the desk or on the road, our first instinct is to look upward. We do not know what the afternoon will bring, but we know the path of righteousness where He leads us. Matthew Henry observes that those who make God's righteousness their daily study will never lack for direction when the crossroads appear. 

Lord, we commit our schedule into Your hands today. Every meeting, every task, every quiet moment in between — we ask that Your wisdom govern our thoughts. Keep our feet from wandering into paths of bitterness or haste. When we do not know which way to turn, let Your voice be heard behind us, saying, This is the way, walk ye in it. Give us the grace to listen, the courage to obey, and the humility to yield our own agenda to Your perfect will.

## Scene 4 — Favour, Shield and Protection for the Day

There are unseen battles in every ordinary day — pressures that test our patience, words spoken in haste by others, and the quiet weariness that creeps in when the load is heavy. We do not walk through these hours undefended. The favour of God is a shield about us, and His righteousness goes before us to make our way straight. Morning worship is the place where we put on this spiritual armor before we ever step out the door.

The verse remains our steady guide:

My mouth shall show forth thy righteousness and thy salvation all the day; for I know not the numbers thereof.

Spurgeon reminds us that the man whose mouth is occupied with the praises of God has little time for murmuring and no room for fear. When we declare His salvation, we are reminding ourselves that we have already been rescued from the ultimate darkness. Whatever trouble visits us today is temporary; whatever grace sustains us is eternal. God is our refuge and our fortress, a very present help in every morning trouble.

Father, we ask for Your divine protection over our minds, our bodies, and our spirits today. Shield us from the arrows of discouragement and the temptation to rely on our own strength when pressure mounts. Surround our families, our homes, and our workplaces with Your favour as with a shield. Let Your peace, which passes all human understanding, guard our hearts through every hour of this day. When we feel weak, be our strength. When we feel exposed, be our hiding place.

## Scene 5 — Blessing Over Work, Family and Home

Every sunrise is an invitation to extend the blessing of God into the spaces where we live and labor. The work of our hands — whether it is tending to a home, serving in a workplace, caring for children, or creating things of value — takes on eternal meaning when it is offered to God. Our homes are meant to be little sanctuaries where His righteousness is spoken of and His salvation is remembered around the table and in the hallway.

Let the scripture settle deep into your heart once more:

My mouth shall show forth thy righteousness and thy salvation all the day; for I know not the numbers thereof.

All the day means in the kitchen, in the car, across the desk, and in the quiet evening hours when the work is done. It transforms our daily labor from a burden into an act of worship. Henry notes that a life spent in praising God is the closest earthly approximation to the work of heaven. We do not have to wait until Sunday or until eternity to experience the joy of His presence; it is available right here, in the middle of a Tuesday morning, amid ordinary duties.

Lord God, we speak Your blessing over our homes and families this morning. May Your love rest upon every room and every relationship. Bless the work of our hands today; establish it, Lord, and let it bear fruit that remains. Where there is tension in a relationship, bring Your healing grace. Where there is weariness in a parent or a worker, pour out fresh energy from on high. Let our words today be seasoning with grace, building others up rather than tearing them down, reflecting the righteousness we have received from You.

## Scene 6 — The Sending Benediction

The morning light is now fully broken across the sky, and the day calls us forward. We leave this quiet moment of prayer not with anxiety about what may happen, but with a settled trust in the One who holds every numbered hour in His hands. Our mouths are set to declare His praise, our hearts are anchored in His salvation, and our steps are guided by His unfailing righteousness.

Hear the promise one last time, carried with you into the hours ahead:

My mouth shall show forth thy righteousness and thy salvation all the day; for I know not the numbers thereof.

May the Lord bless you and keep you as you step into the world today. May His face shine upon you and be gracious unto you in every conversation and every challenge. May He lift up His countenance upon you and give you deep, unshakeable peace, this morning and all the days of your life.

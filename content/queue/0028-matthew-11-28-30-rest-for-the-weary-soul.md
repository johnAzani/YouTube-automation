## Scene 1 — Come Unto Me, All Ye That Labour and Are Heavy Laden (Verse 28a)

The invitation is open to every weary soul.

Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.

You do not have to clean yourself up before coming. You do not have to fix your life first.

Just come. As you are. Tired. Burdened. Weary.

Heavenly Father, as this day closes, I feel the weight of its demands upon me. My spirit is tired, my mind is full of anxieties, and my body aches from the burdens I have carried. I hear Your gentle call, "Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden," and I respond to it now, just as I am. I lay down my pretense of strength and my attempts to manage everything myself, trusting that You truly invite me in my brokenness and fatigue. I come to You tonight, Lord, seeking the comfort only Your presence can provide.

## Scene 2 — And I Will Give You Rest (Verse 28b)

Rest is not something you earn; it is something He gives.

And I will give you rest.

Not rest from work, but rest in work. Not rest from trouble, but rest in trouble.

Receive rest tonight as a free gift.

Lord, I receive this promise of rest not as something I have earned through my efforts, but as a gracious gift from Your loving heart. I pray for a deeper kind of rest tonight, one that permeates my soul beyond mere sleep. Grant me the ability to find rest within the ongoing challenges of my life, not just from them. Let this rest be a spiritual renewal, an inner peace that calms my racing thoughts and eases my burdened spirit, reminding me that You are in control of all things. Thank You for this unmerited favor.

## Scene 3 — Take My Yoke Upon You, and Learn of Me (Verse 29a)

A yoke is shared burden. When you take His yoke, He carries the weight with you.

Take my yoke upon you, and learn of me.

Learn from the One who is meek and lowly in heart. Learn His way of gentle strength.

His yoke does not crush; it enables.

Father, I confess that I often try to carry my burdens alone, stubbornly holding onto my own heavy yoke. Tonight, I desire to release my grip on self-reliance and instead take Your yoke upon me. Teach me, Lord, how to truly share my burdens with You, to walk in step with Your divine rhythm. Guide my thoughts and actions so that I may learn from Your example of gentle strength and humility. Help me understand that true freedom comes from aligning myself with Your will, allowing You to carry the heavier portion of my load.

## Scene 4 — For I Am Meek and Lowly in Heart (Verse 29b)

The One who offers you rest is not harsh or demanding.

For I am meek and lowly in heart.

He is gentle. He is humble. He is safe to approach.

You can come to Him without fear of rejection.

Gracious God, it is comforting to know that the One who calls me is not a harsh taskmaster, but one who is meek and lowly in heart. I find reassurance in Your gentle nature, knowing that I can approach Your throne with confidence, free from the fear of judgment or rejection. Thank You for being so approachable, so humble in Your immense power. Help me to truly internalize this truth, allowing it to dissolve any apprehension I might feel in bringing my deepest struggles and imperfections before You tonight. Let Your humility draw me closer.

## Scene 5 — And Ye Shall Find Rest Unto Your Souls (Verse 29c)

Soul-rest is deeper than physical rest.

And ye shall find rest unto your souls.

Your soul can rest even when your body is tired. Your spirit can be still even when your mind is racing.

Find soul-rest tonight in His presence.

Oh Lord, I long for the profound rest You promise for my soul. Beyond the exhaustion of my body, my soul often feels restless, agitated by worldly concerns and endless thoughts. I pray tonight for that deep, inner stillness that only Your presence can bring. Quiet the anxieties that clamor for attention in my mind, and bring peace to my spirit. May my soul truly anchor itself in Your unwavering love and sovereign control, allowing me to experience Your promised rest, regardless of external circumstances. Let my spirit be calm in You.

## Scene 6 — For My Yoke Is Easy, and My Burden Is Light (Verse 30)

His yoke is easy. His burden is light.

For my yoke is easy, and my burden is light.

Whatever you are carrying, He makes it lighter. Whatever you are facing, He makes it easier.

Lay down your heavy load. Take His light yoke instead.

Merciful Father, Your word assures me that Your yoke is easy and Your burden is light. I pray for the faith and strength to fully surrender every heavy load I am carrying into Your capable hands tonight. Help me to truly believe that with You, nothing is too difficult or too overwhelming. Empower me to exchange my self-imposed pressures and anxieties for the lightness of Your grace and guidance. May I awaken refreshed, not just physically, but spiritually, having embraced Your easy yoke and light burden, knowing You are with me.

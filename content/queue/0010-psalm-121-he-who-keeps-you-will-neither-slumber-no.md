## Scene 1 — Lifting Eyes to the Hills (Verses 1–2)
Before you close your eyes tonight, take a deep breath and release the weight of everything you carried today.
I will lift up mine eyes unto the hills, from whence cometh my help.
My help cometh from the Lord, which made heaven and earth.
When you look at your circumstances, worry feels overwhelming; but when you look up to the Creator, peace enters your room.
Rest tonight in the sovereign care of the Maker of the universe.
Father, when the day has left us feeling small, remind us that the hills are not higher than Your compassion. We do not have to find strength in ourselves before we come to You. We can bring our questions, our tired minds, and the things we cannot yet understand. You made the heavens and the earth, and You are near enough to hear this quiet prayer. Let our attention turn from the size of our worries to the faithfulness of our Helper.

## Scene 2 — The Foot That Shall Not Slip (Verse 3)
He will not suffer thy foot to be moved: He that keepeth thee will not slumber.
Notice the deep gentleness of this promise. God does not grow weary.
He does not turn His eyes away when the world goes dark.
Even while you rest in quiet sleep, His heavenly hand is steadying your steps and ordering your tomorrow.
Breathe out the tension in your shoulders and receive His sleepless watch.
Lord, some of us are thinking about decisions that still lie ahead. We wonder whether we will take the right step, say the right words, or have enough strength for the work before us. Guide us with wisdom when morning comes. Help us to walk patiently rather than rush in fear. When the path feels uncertain, keep us close to Your voice, and teach us to trust Your care one step at a time.

## Scene 3 — The Sleepless Watchman of Israel (Verse 4)
Behold, He that keepeth Israel shall neither slumber nor sleep.
Throughout this entire night, you do not have to stay awake guarding your worries.
God is on duty. He is the eternal Watchman who never grows drowsy and never abandons His post.
Surrender your home, your children, and your future into His faithful keeping.
He has you safe in His everlasting arms.
Tonight we entrust to You the people we love. Remember those who are far from home, those who work through the night, and those who are caring for someone who is unwell. Give comfort to the lonely and patient strength to the weary. We cannot watch over every person or control every outcome, but we can place them into Your hands. Let Your peace settle over each home represented in this prayer.

## Scene 4 — The Shade at Your Right Hand (Verses 5–6)
The Lord is thy keeper: the Lord is thy shade upon thy right hand.
The sun shall not smite thee by day, nor the moon by night.
Whatever heat or burden pressed against your spirit during the daylight hours, the shadow of the Almighty is cooling your soul.
His presence surrounds you like a divine shield against every unseen danger.
You are fully covered by His grace.
God, be our shade when the demands of life feel relentless. Make room in our hearts for quiet, for gratitude, and for the courage to ask for help when we need it. We do not pretend that every difficulty has disappeared. We rest in the promise that Your presence remains with us through the heat of the day and the silence of the night. Let Your kindness be a shelter as we breathe slowly and release the burdens we cannot carry alone.

## Scene 5 — Preserved from All Evil (Verse 7)
The Lord shall preserve thee from all evil: He shall preserve thy soul.
Let this eternal promise quiet every fearful thought whispering in the quiet of this room.
God preserves your heart from bitterness, your mind from anxiety, and your spirit from defeat.
He guards what matters most: your eternal soul.
Sleep in freedom knowing that no weapon formed against you will prevail.
Merciful Father, guard our inner lives as well as the paths before us. Where resentment has taken root, begin the gentle work of forgiveness. Where fear has made our thoughts restless, lead us back to what is true and good. Give us grace to seek wise counsel and practical support when we need it. Keep our hearts tender, our hope alive, and our trust anchored in Your love, even when tomorrow is not yet clear.

## Scene 6 — Preserving Your Coming and Going (Verse 8)
The Lord shall preserve thy going out and thy coming in from this time forth, and even for evermore.
As you close your eyes, receive this final benediction of peace.
Tomorrow's journeys are already covered by God's faithfulness.
Sleep deeply, sleep peacefully, and rest in the sanctuary of His presence.
As this day ends, we thank You for every mercy we noticed and every mercy we missed. Thank You for the people who showed kindness, for strength that carried us, and for the gift of another opportunity to begin again. Bless our going out and our coming in. When morning arrives, meet us with fresh wisdom for its responsibilities and compassion for the people we encounter. For now, let our minds grow quiet, our breathing soften, and our bodies receive the rest they need. In Your faithful care we pray. Amen.

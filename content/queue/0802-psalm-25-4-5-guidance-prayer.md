## Scene 1 — Bringing the Decision to the Lord

Shew me thy ways, O LORD; teach me thy paths. Lead me in thy truth, and teach me: for thou art the God of my salvation; on thee do I wait all the day. These words from the twenty-fifth Psalm open our hearts before the living God today, bringing every quiet burden, every unresolved question, and every crossroads of life directly into His holy presence. Lord God of heaven and earth, we come before Thee not with our own map already drawn, but with open hands and yielded wills. We acknowledge that human understanding is so often clouded by haste, by fear, and by the shifting shadows of this world. In Proverbs we are commanded to trust in the LORD with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding. In all thy ways acknowledge him, and he shall direct thy paths. Father, right now, we bring this season of uncertainty, this heavy decision, and this need for direction before Your throne of grace. We lay down our impatience. We lay down our desire to force an outcome or to rush ahead of Your cloud and Your fire. We ask for the grace to stand still in Your presence, recognizing that true wisdom begins the moment we confess our utter dependence upon Your holiness. As Charles Spurgeon reminds us in his reflection on these verses, the posture of a believer seeking guidance is not that of a master demanding answers, but of a scholar sitting humbly at the feet of the divine Teacher, willing to learn the alphabet of heaven. Lord, quiet our anxieties. Still the competing voices within our minds that pull us toward self-reliance, fear, and hasty compromise. We choose this day to acknowledge Thee in our ways, believing that Your ear is open to the prayer of the upright and that Your hand is mighty to guide those who look to Thee alone.

## Scene 2 — Asking God for Wisdom

If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not; and it shall be given him. Heavenly Father, Your Word encourages us with this magnificent promise of divine generosity. We do not come to a reluctant God who hides His will from those who seek Him with sincere hearts; rather, we come to the Father of lights, who delights to bestow discernment upon His children when they ask in faith. Matthew Henry, in his commentary on this passage, observes that divine wisdom is not a secret kept under lock and key from those who fear the Lord, but a well-spring opened for all who hunger and thirst after righteousness. Lord, we confess that in our current juncture, we lack the foresight needed for tomorrow. We cannot see the end from the beginning. We do not know what a single day may bring forth, nor can we discern the full fruit of the paths that stretch before us. Therefore, we ask You for wisdom — not merely information, but spiritual discernment, the heavenly skill to distinguish between what is merely good and what is holy, between the voice of self and the whisper of Your Spirit. Grant us a teachable heart, O Lord. Remove from us any stubbornness of will that resists correction or resents closed doors. When You close a door, give us the grace to accept Your boundary. When You open a path, give us the courage to walk through it without hesitation. Let Your Spirit search our motives, purify our intentions, and align our desires with Your eternal purposes. We ask not for wealth, nor for worldly ease, but for that wisdom which is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, and easy to be entreated, full of mercy and good fruits.

## Scene 3 — The Lamp of the Word

Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path. Lord God, as we seek Your guidance through this decision, we anchor our souls immovably in the truth of Your Holy Word. We thank You that You have not left us in darkness, nor have You abandoned us to the uncertain philosophies of a changing culture. Your Scripture stands forever settled in the heavens, shining like a steady beacon across every dark valley and every mountain pass of human experience. Yet we recognize, Lord, that Your Word ordinarily operates as a lamp to our feet — illuminating just the next step we must take, rather than casting a brilliant floodlight across the entire horizon of our future. Teach us to be content with this daily dependence. Forgive us when we murmur because we cannot see five years, or five months, or even five weeks down the road. Remind us that daily bread requires daily trust, and daily guidance requires daily walking with You. As the psalmist declared, Your word gives light; it gives understanding unto the simple. We pray that Your precepts would direct our affections, that Your statutes would be our counsellors, and that every decision we face would be weighed upon the righteous scales of Scripture. If fear whispers that we are moving too slowly, let Your promises steady us. If impatience urges us to leap without looking, let Your commands restrain us. We rest in the assurance that You do not misguide those who commit their way unto You, and that Your light never fails the soul that trusts in Your unfailing faithfulness.

## Scene 4 — Wise Counsel and Tested Motives

Where no counsel is, the fall is: but in the multitude of counsellors there is safety. O Lord, our God, You have placed us not in isolation, but within the body of Christ and under the order of Your divine providence. As we seek discernment for the path ahead, we pray for humility to listen to godly counsel, to receive the loving rebukes of mature believers, and to weigh our choices against the wisdom of those who have walked faithfully before us. We recognize that prayer and the reading of Scripture do not stand apart from the ordinary means of grace You have ordained, including the counsel of righteous men and women whose lives bear the fruit of the Spirit. Furthermore, Lord, we acknowledge that prayer walks hand in hand with human responsibility. We commend this decision into Your hands while also honoring the practical duties You have placed upon us, knowing that seeking God does not replace the diligent exercise of sound judgment, nor does it substitute for qualified professional counsel in matters of health, law, or stewardship where earthly expertise is required. Search us, O God, and know our hearts; try us, and know our thoughts: and see if there be any wicked way in us, and lead us in the way everlasting. Expose any hidden selfishness, any pride that refuses advice, or any secret fear that seeks to manipulate circumstances. Grant us discernment to recognize godly counsel and the wisdom to reject ungodly compromise. Establish our steps, Lord, not in our own strength, but in the collective wisdom of Your truth and the fellowship of Your saints.

## Scene 5 — Waiting With Patience and Ordered Steps

The steps of a good man are ordered by the LORD: and he delighteth in his way. Though he fall, he shall not be utterly cast down: for the LORD upholdeth him with his hand. Heavenly Father, there are moments in every believer's pilgrimage when waiting upon the Lord is the hardest and yet the most necessary labor of all. To wait is not inactivity; it is active trust, the steady anchor of the soul that refuses to panic while the clouds gather overhead. We lift up every listener right now who is weary of waiting, whose heart is heavy with the tension of an unanswered prayer, or who fears making a misstep in a season of transition. Remind them, Lord, of Your promise in Psalm 37. Even when we stumble, even when our weakness causes us to falter, Your hand is underneath us. You do not let us fall away from Your grace. Teach us to wait upon You without growing faint. When doors remain closed and the silence seems long, let Your peace — which passes all human understanding — guard our hearts and minds in Christ Jesus. We confess that Your timing is perfect, even when our impatience calls it delayed. You know the exact moment when the path will open, and You know the exact preparation our hearts require before we step into the next season of service. Quiet our restless spirits. Remove the frantic striving that exhausts our faith. Fill us with the quiet confidence that the God who ordered the stars in their courses is fully able to order our steps today, tomorrow, and unto the end of our days.

## Scene 6 — Committing the Way and Closing Benediction

Commit thy way unto the LORD; trust also in him; and he shall bring it to pass. And the LORD shall guide thee continually, and satisfy thy soul in drought, and make fat thy bones: and thou shalt be like a watered garden, and like a spring of water, whose waters fail not. O Lord our God, having brought our petition before Your throne, having searched Your Word, having examined our motives, and having surrendered our impatience, we now commit our entire way into Your hands. We leave the outcome with You. We release our grip on the steering wheel of our lives and place our hands completely into Yours, knowing that Your right hand shall hold us fast. Be unto us a pillar of cloud by day and a pillar of fire by night. When the road ahead forks, make our ears sensitive to the gentle voice behind us saying, This is the way, walk ye in it. We ask for no other security than Your presence, no other compass than Your truth, and no other reward than Your fellowship. Strengthen every listener to take the very next faithful step — no matter how small — with courage, peace, and unwavering trust in Your character. We give You all the glory, all the honor, and all the praise, now and forevermore.

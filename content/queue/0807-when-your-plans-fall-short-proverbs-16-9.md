## Scene 1 — Bringing the Decision to the Lord

You sat with the notepad late into the evening, listing the options, weighing the cost, trying to make the pieces fit into a future that makes sense. But the more you map it out, the more the road seems to twist away from what you anticipated, leaving you with a quiet sense of uncertainty in your chest. Proverbs chapter sixteen, verse nine tells us this truth: A man's heart deviseth his way: but the LORD directeth his steps. Lord God, we come before You tonight acknowledging that our minds are finite and our vision is remarkably short. We make our plans, we calculate our steps, and we set our hearts upon outcomes we cannot guarantee. In this season of decision, when the road ahead branches off into directions we did not expect, we choose to stop leaning upon our own understanding. We bring our careful planning, our anxieties, and our confusion right to Your feet. You are the God who sees the end from the beginning, and nothing in our lives takes You by surprise. Grant us a teachable heart, O Lord, that we might seek Your guidance above all else. As we navigate this crossroads, steady our minds against the panic of rushing ahead or the paralysis of standing still. We desire Your direction far more than our own preferred outcome. In every thought and every deliberation, we acknowledge You as sovereign over our days, asking simply that You lead us in the way everlasting according to Your holy word.

## Scene 2 — Asking God for Wisdom

When we realize how little we truly control, the temptation is to grasp tighter at the wheel, forcing doors open that perhaps were never meant to turn. Yet James reminds us that if any of us lack wisdom, we are to ask of God, who gives to all men liberally and upbraideth not. Matthew Henry, in his commentary on the wisdom of Proverbs, reminds us that human wisdom is mere folly when divorced from divine dependence, for man proposes but God disposes. Heavenly Father, we confess that we often lack the discernment needed for the hours before us. We ask You now for that heavenly wisdom which is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, and easy to be entreated. Do not upbraid us for our hesitant faith or our many missteps in the past, but pour out Your generous grace upon our minds today. Cleanse our motives from selfish ambition, pride, or the fear of what others might think. When we are pulled in opposite directions by circumstance or advice, grant us the quiet clarity that only Your Spirit can provide. We do not ask for a map of the next ten years, Lord; we ask for the lamp of Your wisdom for the very next step. Help us to recognize Your voice when it whispers behind us, saying, This is the way, walk ye in it, even when the path requires humility and patience. We lean upon Your promise that those who seek You early shall find You.

## Scene 3 — The Lamp of the Word

We live in a culture that demands immediate certainty, flashing signs, and guaranteed results before we are willing to move an inch. But Scripture does not promise a floodlight over the entire horizon; it promises a lamp unto our feet and a light unto our path. Lord, teach us to trust the daily measure of Your word rather than demanding a panoramic view of tomorrow. Your word cuts through the noise of our own desires and reveals the true motives of our hearts. When we read the proverbs and the psalms, we find a steady anchor that refuses to shift with every changing wind of circumstance. Let Your truth govern our expectations today. If Your word tells us to wait, give us the grace to be still. If Your word tells us to move, give us the courage to step out without looking back in terror. We rest in the reality that Your direction never contradicts Your written truth. As we search the Scriptures for guidance in this current decision, let every anxious thought be brought into captivity to the obedience of Christ. We do not look for fleece-laying or signs of our own invention; we look to the unchanging standard of Your righteousness. Illuminate the immediate path before us, Lord, and let Your word be the final arbiter in every choice we make.

## Scene 4 — Wise Counsel and Tested Motives

The road of discernment is never meant to be walked in proud isolation, for Proverbs also tells us that in the multitude of counsellors there is safety. Yet even among our closest friends and advisors, our hearts can remain deceitful, masking our own preferences as the will of Heaven. Sovereign Lord, search us and try our thoughts today. If there is any hidden stubbornness within us — any desire to use prayer as a way to baptize our own pre-determined choices — expose it by Your light. Bring godly, discerning men and women across our path who will speak truth in love, holding us accountable to Scripture rather than flattering our fears. We remember that wise counsel walks hand in hand with professional diligence, sound judgment, and honest evaluation of the facts before us. We do not treat prayer as a shortcut that replaces the hard work of wisdom, nor do we lean merely on human counsel as if You were absent from the room. Balance our hearts, O God. Give us ears that are open to correction and a spirit that refuses to be swayed by unbiblical counsel. May every advisor we consult point us back to the sufficiency of Christ and the wisdom of walking in integrity.

## Scene 5 — Waiting With Patience and Ordered Steps

There are moments in every life when the hardest command of all is simply to wait upon the Lord while the silence stretches on. David wrote in the psalms that the steps of a good man are ordered by the Lord, and He delighteth in his way, yet even David had to wait in caves and wildernesses before the promise came to pass. Lord, we confess that waiting makes us restless. We want to force outcomes, to finish the process, and to be done with the discomfort of uncertainty. Yet we know that a step taken outside of Your timing is a step into needless sorrow. If You have called us to wait in this valley, then grant us the patience to pitch our tent here until Your pillar of cloud moves once more. Uphold us with Your right hand when our footing slips and discouragement whispers that we have been forgotten. Remind us that even in the waiting, You are working behind the scenes, ordering the circumstances, preparing the hearts of others, and shaping our own character for the task ahead. We surrender our timetables to You. Whether the answer comes today, next month, or a year from now, our times are in Your hands. Keep us from taking matters into our own hands out of panic or weariness.

## Scene 6 — Committing the Way and Closing Benediction

Commit thy works unto the LORD, and thy thoughts shall be established, says Proverbs sixteen, verse three. And Isaiah reminds us that the LORD shall guide thee continually, and satisfy thy soul in drought. Heavenly Father, we take our tangled thoughts, our unresolved decisions, and our future days, and we commit them entirely into Your keeping. We do not know what tomorrow brings, but we know the One who holds tomorrow, and that is enough for our weary souls. Go before us into the decisions of this week, smoothing the rough places and shutting the doors that are not of Your appointing. If we must walk through a narrow pass or an unfamiliar valley, let us sense Your presence walking right beside us every step of the way. We lay down our anxieties, our need to control, and our fear of making a mistake, trusting in Your great mercy to redirect us if we wander. May our lives bring glory to Your name as we walk humbly in Your counsel. Type Amen to agree with this prayer, and subscribe to The Guided Path to seek God's wisdom and direction for every step.

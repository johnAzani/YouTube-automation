## Scene 1 — Bringing the Decision to the Lord

There are hours when the road ahead divides in three directions, and every signpost seems weathered and blank. You have weighed the choices, you have asked for the opinions of those around you, and still the quiet center of your mind finds no rest. It is in this exact place of human uncertainty that we bring our petitions to the Lord. We begin by acknowledging that our own understanding is limited, and that we cannot see the turning point that waits just beyond the next rise. The psalmist Asaph knew what it was to struggle with the complexities of this life, to look at the crooked paths of the world and feel his own foothold slipping. Yet in the middle of his weariness, he looked upward and found an anchor that did not move. He wrote in the seventy-third psalm, verse twenty-three, saying, Nevertheless I am continually with thee: thou hast holden me by my right hand. Let these words settle over your mind right now. You are not navigating this decision alone, and your confusion has not slipped past the notice of your Creator. He holds you by the right hand, steadying your steps even when the ground feels uncertain beneath your feet. As we begin this time of prayer, bring your specific question, your tangled thoughts, and your quiet worries directly into His presence. Acknowledge that He is God, and that you are leaning, not on your own imperfect wisdom, but upon His unfailing grace.

## Scene 2 — Asking God for Wisdom

When we face a decision that carries heavy consequences for our work, our families, or our future, the temptation is to rush forward, driven by anxiety or the pressure of a ticking clock. But Scripture instructs us differently. James reminds us that if any man lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not. Matthew Henry, in his commentary on the Scriptures, notes that God's door is never closed to those who come confessing their ignorance and seeking His direction with an honest heart. He does not scold us for our lack of foresight; rather, He invites us to draw near and receive the discernment that only He can supply. Think of the quiet places where you have tried to manufacture your own answers, running down blind alleys of worry. Lay those attempts aside today. Ask the Lord to grant you a teachable heart — a mind that is willing to be corrected, and a spirit that is ready to wait for His timing rather than forcing an open door that was never meant to be crossed. Wisdom from above is pure and peaceable, gentle and easy to be entreated. When God gives counsel, it brings an inner settledness that the loudest human advice can never replicate. Ask Him to clear away the static of your own desires so that you can hear the quiet voice of His truth speaking into your circumstance today.

## Scene 3 — The Lamp of the Word

There is a common desire in every human heart to see the entire map laid out before our feet — to know the outcome of every choice five years down the road before we take a single step today. But the Lord does not often give us the map of tomorrow. Instead, He gives us a lamp for the step we are on right now. As the psalmist reminds us elsewhere, God's word is a lamp unto our feet, and a light unto our path. It illuminates the immediate ground directly in front of us, giving us just enough light to make the next faithful choice in obedience and faith. This is why we return again and again to the Scriptures when we are seeking guidance. We do not look for magical signs or secret handwriting on the wall; we look to the established truth of God's holy word, which trains our conscience and tests our motives. When you read the Scriptures in a season of decision, you find that the principles of God cut through the fog of your immediate anxieties. They remind you of what is honorable, what is just, and what honors His name. Trust that the light you have right now is sufficient for the choice you need to make today. You do not need to figure out next year this afternoon. You only need to walk in the light that God is providing this very moment, resting in the assurance that His word never fails and His instructions never lead us astray.

## Scene 4 — Wise Counsel and Tested Motives

Seeking God's guidance is never an excuse to isolate ourselves from the community of faith He has placed around us. The book of Proverbs reminds us that in the multitude of counsellors there is safety, and that plans are established when we take counsel with those who walk closely with the Lord. When you are facing a difficult crossroads, it is wise to seek out mature, godly individuals whose lives bear the fruit of the Spirit — those who will listen without judgment and speak truth in love. At the same time, this spiritual journey must always be accompanied by a rigorous and honest examination of our own heart's motives before God. We must ask the Lord to search us and try our thoughts, to reveal any hidden ambition, fear, or stubbornness that might be dressing itself up as spiritual discernment. It must also be said with all humility that prayer and spiritual counsel walk hand in hand with the practical responsibilities of life. If your decision involves medical, legal, financial, or professional matters, seek out qualified counsel, study the facts with diligence, and apply the best wisdom available. God uses the ordinary means of wise advisors, diligent research, and careful thought to shape our decisions, holding our human efforts within the larger compass of His sovereign care.

## Scene 5 — Waiting With Patience and Ordered Steps

There are few disciplines more difficult for the human spirit than the discipline of waiting on God when a decision is pressing and the silence from heaven seems long. We want quick resolutions; we want the door to fly open or shut immediately so we can stop feeling the tension of the unknown. Yet the psalmists constantly return to the theme of waiting upon the Lord, reminding us that those who wait upon Him shall renew their strength. Psalm thirty-seven tells us that the steps of a good man are ordered by the Lord, and He delighteth in his way. Notice that the text speaks of steps, one at a time, ordered by His hand. Even if you stumble, even if you take a hesitant turn, the promise of God is that He upholds you with His hand, and you shall not be utterly cast down. In your season of waiting, let your impatience be quieted by His presence. Do not mistake delay for denial, and do not mistake silence for absence. The God who holds the stars in their courses is carefully ordering your path, aligning circumstances, softening hearts, and preparing the way before you. Trust His timing above your own urgency, and let your spirit find its rest in the quiet certainty that He is working on your behalf even when you cannot see the movement of His hands.

## Scene 6 — Committing the Way and Closing Benediction

We come now to the center of our petition, resting our entire situation into the hands of the One who knows the end from the beginning. Let us hold fast to the enduring promise found in the seventy-third psalm, verse twenty-four: Thou shalt guide me with thy counsel, and afterward receive me to glory. This is the great confidence of the believer: that the guidance we receive in this temporary life is tethered to an eternal destination. He is not merely directing your career, your finances, or your immediate choices; He is shepherding your soul toward an eternal home in His presence. Commit your way completely unto Him today. Release the fear of making a wrong turn into the hands of a God whose wisdom is infinite and whose mercy is without end.

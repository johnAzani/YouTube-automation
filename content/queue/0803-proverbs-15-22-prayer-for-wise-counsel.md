## Scene 1 — Bringing the Decision to the Lord

Father in Heaven, we come before Your throne today with hearts that seek Your face, acknowledging that apart from You we can do nothing. In moments of great decision, when the road forks and the fog of uncertainty rests heavy upon our eyes, we do not lean unto our own understanding. We bow before Your wisdom, asking for the grace to trust You with all our heart. Proverbs 15:22 reminds us: "Without counsel purposes are disappointed: but in the multitude of counsellors they are established." Lord, we bring our tangled thoughts, our heavy dilemmas, and our whispered questions into Your holy presence. We confess that our own minds are easily blinded by our desires, our fears, and our impatience. We lay every option, every choice, and every unresolved question upon Your altar. Teach us to wait upon You, to quiet our hurried spirits, and to seek Your direction before we take a single step. As we open Your Word today, grant us a teachable heart. Guard us against hasty choices born of anxiety, and give us the patience to listen when You speak. We acknowledge that You are the source of all true wisdom, and we ask that Your Holy Spirit would illuminate our minds, steady our nerves, and direct our paths in righteousness, for Your name's sake. Amen.

## Scene 2 — Asking God for Wisdom

Almighty God, You have promised in James chapter one that if any of us lacks wisdom, we may ask of You — You who give to all men liberally, and upbraid not. We claim that promise right now in this season of decision. We confess our lack. We do not know what tomorrow holds, nor do we possess the foresight to discern the hidden snares that lie along life's pathway. Therefore, we look away from our own limited capacity and look up to You. Matthew Henry, in his commentary on the Scriptures, reminds us that human wisdom left to itself is frail and unstable, but the counsel of the Lord stands forever. Lord, grant us discernment that is pure, peaceable, gentle, and easy to be entreated. When we are torn between conflicting paths, give us the inner stillness to know Your peace which passes all understanding. Keep us from the pride that thinks it knows the way, and from the stubbornness that refuses correction. If there are blind spots in our judgment, reveal them by Your light. If our motives are mixed with self-seeking or fear, purify them in the fire of Your truth. We ask not for our own will to be rubber-stamped, but for Your holy, righteous, and perfect will to be established in our lives. Incline our hearts unto Your testimonies, and let Your wisdom be our shield and our counselor today. Amen.

## Scene 3 — The Lamp of the Word

Merciful Father, Your Word declares in Psalm 119:105 that Your word is a lamp unto our feet, and a light unto our path. We thank You that You do not leave us in total darkness, fumbling blindly through the maze of this world. You have given us the Holy Scriptures as a sure guide, shining upon the exact spot where we must place our next footstep. We do not ask You to unveil the entire horizon all at once, for we know our minds could not bear it; instead, we thank You for the sufficient light for today. We confess that sometimes we grow impatient with Your pacing, wishing for a flash of lightning that reveals every mile of the journey. Forgive us, Lord, and grant us the grace to walk step by step in obedience to what You have already made clear in Your Word. We remember the promise of Psalm 32:8: "I will instruct thee and teach thee in the way which thou shalt go: I will guide thee with mine eye." Keep us sensitive to the gentle guidance of Your Spirit, attuned to the quiet whisper behind us saying, "This is the way, walk ye in it." As we search the Scriptures, let every anxious thought subside, and let our confidence rest securely upon the unchanging truth of Your holy promises. Amen.

## Scene 4 — Wise Counsel and Tested Motives

Righteous God, You have ordained that we should not walk this pilgrimage alone. Proverbs 15:22 reminds us that in the multitude of counsellors, purposes are established. We ask You to place godly, mature, and discerning mentors, pastors, and believers in our path — people who fear Your name and love Your truth. Give us the humility to seek their counsel and the discernment to weigh their words against the standard of Scripture. We recognize that while prayer and godly counsel are vital components of seeking Your will, they walk alongside and never replace the practical wisdom of qualified professional advice where matters of health, law, or finance are concerned. Grant us the sense to use the gifts of wise counsel You have placed in our world, while keeping our ultimate trust anchored in You alone. Search our hearts, O God, as David prayed in Psalm 139. Know our thoughts, test us, and see if there be any wicked way in us. If we are seeking counsel merely to find someone who will agree with our pre-formed desires, correct our course. Break down our self-will, expose our hidden biases, and give us ears to hear even the hard truths that lead to life. We commit our relationships, our advisors, and our decisions into Your hands, praying that Your wisdom would be the referee in every discussion we undertake. Amen.

## Scene 5 — Waiting With Patience and Ordered Steps

Sovereign Lord, You are the God who orders the steps of a righteous person, and You delight in their way. Even when our feet stumble, You do not let us utterly fall, for You uphold us with Your hand. We bring before You the weariness that so often accompanies prolonged waiting. There are decisions before us that cannot be rushed, and seasons of uncertainty that test the very fabric of our faith. Teach us how to wait upon the Lord and be of good courage. Remind us that waiting is not wasted time; it is the crucible where our impatience is burned away and our trust in Your timing is refined. We cast off the pressure to force doors open that You have closed, and we release the anxiety of trying to manufacture our own security. Father, if You are bidding us to wait, grant us supernatural peace while we hold our position. If You are opening a door, give us the courage to step through it with faith, resting entirely upon Your grace. We rest in the assurance of Psalm 27:14: "Wait on the LORD: be of good courage, and he shall strengthen thine heart." Strengthen our hearts this day, Lord, and keep our feet firmly planted on the Rock of Ages. Amen.

## Scene 6 — Committing the Way and Closing Benediction

Gracious and eternal God, we now commit our way entirely unto You, trusting that You shall bring it to pass. We lay down our burdens of doubt, our fears of making a wrong turn, and our need to control the outcome of our decisions. We step forward into the remainder of our days clothed in Your armor and guided by Your counsel. May Your presence go with us wherever we turn, and may Your peace guard our hearts and our minds in Christ Jesus. We thank You that You are not a God who hides Your face from those who seek You with a whole heart. You have heard our prayer, You have measured our tears, and You are even now preparing the way before us. We ask all these things in the precious, holy, and matchless name of our Lord and Savior, Jesus Christ. Type Amen to agree with this prayer, and subscribe to The Guided Path to seek God's wisdom and direction for every step. Amen.

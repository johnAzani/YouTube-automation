## Scene 1 — The Table Prepared in Daily Labour

The early hours often bring a familiar weight before the workday even begins, as tasks pile high and the demands of career, trade, or household responsibility stretch out before you. There is a temptation in the middle of routine labour to work only for the approval of men, to measure your worth by shifting metrics, or to grow weary when effort seems hidden from notice. Yet the Scriptures direct our eyes away from human sight and toward the living God, who sets a table before us in the presence of every daily burden. In Colossians chapter three, the Apostle Paul speaks directly to the daily toil of our hands, lifting our work out of the realm of mere survival and placing it into the sanctuary of faithful service to Christ. Let us quiet our hearts and bring our daily labour before Jehovah-Jireh, the God who sees every honest effort and rewards the quiet diligence of His people.

## Scene 2 — Working Heartily as Unto the Lord

When the tasks of the day feel mundane or heavy, it is easy to let our diligence slip and our spirit grow indifferent to the work set before us. Yet the word of truth calls us to an entirely different standard of stewardship, one that transforms ordinary duties into acts of devotion. Let us hear the instruction preserved in Scripture, written down for every worker, builder, and servant of God: And whatsoever ye do, do it heartily, as to the Lord, and not unto men. Lord God of heaven, we bring our daily work to You. Forgive us for the times we have laboured with complaining hearts or sought only the praise of human supervisors. Grant us the grace today to approach our desks, our tools, our homes, and our vocations with a renewed spirit. May every task we set our hands to be done with sincerity and vigour, knowing that we serve not merely an earthly employer, but the King of glory. When fatigue presses in and the hours grow long, infuse our labour with Your strength, that our work might reflect the character of our Redeemer.

## Scene 3 — The Reward of the Inheritance

Human systems often fail to notice honest labour, and diligent hands can sometimes go unrewarded in a world driven by self-interest and haste. But the Lord does not overlook the faithful stewardship of His servants. Let us ponder the promise that follows our daily diligence, recorded by the same apostle: Knowing that of the Lord ye shall receive the reward of the inheritance: for ye serve the Lord Christ. Heavenly Father, we thank You that our toil is never wasted in Your sight. When doors of recognition remain closed and earthly compensation falls short, anchor our hope in Your unfailing justice and grace. We do not look to temporal measures alone for our security, but we trust in the eternal inheritance purchased by Christ. Establish the work of our hands, Lord, and let Your favour rest upon our daily endeavours. Guard our hearts against bitterness when our labour goes unpraised, and remind us continually that our ultimate Master is in heaven, keeping watch over every honest deed.

## Scene 4 — Wisdom and Discernment in Daily Decisions

Every working day brings crossroads, decisions, and moments where wisdom is needed to navigate complex choices with integrity. Whether we are managing budgets, negotiating agreements, leading teams, or simply tending to the daily duties of the household, we need the counsel of the Lord to guide our steps. Father, grant us discernment in every professional and vocational choice we face. Where there is confusion, give clarity; where there is pressure to compromise, grant unwavering moral courage. Let Your Holy Spirit direct our words in meetings, our actions in difficult tasks, and our patience in times of stress. Remind us that integrity in small matters prepares us for greater responsibilities. Keep us from dishonest gain and hasty shortcuts, anchoring our feet firmly upon the path of righteousness and truth.

## Scene 5 — Favour and Open Doors of Opportunity

As we labour faithfully day by day, we lift our eyes to ask for the open doors that only Your providence can provide. You are the God who prepares a table before us and opens the windows of heaven to bless the work of our hands. Lord, we pray for divine favour in our workplaces, our businesses, and our communities. Open doors of opportunity that no man can shut, connecting us with tasks and callings where our gifts can serve Your purposes and bless our households. Grant us favour with those we work alongside, that our conduct may be a silent witness to Your grace. Yet, even as we pray for provision and advancement, guard us against anxious striving. Teach us to rest in the assurance that our times are in Your hands, and that You know precisely what we need before we ask.

## Scene 6 — The Overflowing Cup of Covenant Provision

As this day continues, we rest our confidence in the unfailing goodness of our Shepherd, whose grace sustains us through every season of labour and rest. Our cup runneth over, not because of our own unassisted strength, but because Your mercy follows us all the days of our life. Heavenly Father, we commit our careers, our families, and our daily bread into Your keeping. Fill our hearts with quiet contentment and our minds with steady peace, knowing that You are Jehovah-Jireh, the God who provides.

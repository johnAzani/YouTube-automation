## Scene 1 — Bringing the Decision to the Lord

There are times when the choices before us are tangled with so many conflicting opinions, our own hurried thoughts, and the pressure to have every answer right now, that we find ourselves standing still. It is the quiet moment at the desk when the paperwork lies unfinished, or the late afternoon when the mind simply refuses to settle on one direction. In those moments, when human reasoning has reached its limit and the next step is hidden in shadow, we are invited to bring our need for understanding to the one true source of light. We do not lean upon our own understanding, because we know how easily our own desires can dress themselves up as wisdom. Instead, we come acknowledging Him in this very decision, asking that He direct our paths according to His unchanging truth. Let us turn our hearts toward His presence right now, laying down the burden of having to figure everything out on our own, and asking Him to quiet the noise within us so we can hear His voice. Heavenly Father, You know the exact decision that weighs on our minds today, and You see the limits of our own understanding. We confess that we often rely on our own quick judgments and anxious calculations, trying to force doors open or shut them before we are ready. Grant us the grace to stop striving and to bring this uncertainty directly to Your throne. When our thoughts are divided and the path ahead is unclear, teach us to wait on You. As we open Your Word, remove every distraction from our minds, and grant us a humble spirit that is willing to receive Your instruction above all else. In Jesus' name, we pray. Amen.

## Scene 2 — Asking God for Wisdom

When we search our hearts honestly, we realize how often we lack the discernment needed for the tests of each day. We want to do what is right, yet we stumble over our own impatience or the fear of making a costly misstep. James reminds us in the first chapter of his letter that if any of us lacks wisdom, we should ask of God, who gives to all men liberally and does not upbraid. Matthew Henry notes in his commentary on these passages that divine wisdom is not a native product of the human heart; it is a treasure sent down from above, graciously bestowed upon those who acknowledge their poverty and ask in faith without wavering. This heavenly wisdom brings an order to our thoughts that earth-bound cleverness can never manufacture. It calms the restless urge to manipulate outcomes and replaces it with a steady, quiet trust in the character of God. Lord God, we come asking for that wisdom which only You can supply, knowing that You give liberally to those who ask in humility. We do not come with proud pretensions or demands for our own way, but with open hands and dependent hearts. When we face choices that require a discernment deeper than our own experience, let Your Spirit impart the mind of Christ to us. Cleanse our motives from self-seeking, ambition, and the fear of man. Give us the discernment to distinguish between what looks expedient in the eyes of the world and what is truly pleasing in Your sight, so that our lives may honor You in every private thought and public action. In Jesus' name, we pray. Amen.

## Scene 3 — The Lamp of the Word

The psalmist declared that Your word is a lamp unto our feet and a light unto our path, yet how often do we demand a floodlight for the entire horizon when God has only promised enough light for the next single step? We grow anxious because we cannot see five years, or even five months, down the road. But the heavenly wisdom described in Scripture does not overwhelm us with distant maps; it illuminates the immediate obedience required right now. In the third chapter of James, verse seventeen, the apostle lays out the exact character of this divine guidance, setting a standard that cuts through every counterfeit of human cleverness. Let us hear these words as they are written in the King James Version: But the wisdom that is from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, and easy to be intreated, full of mercy and good fruits, without partiality, and without hypocrisy. These words are not merely an abstract ideal; they are the very DNA of the guidance God offers to His children. Heavenly Father, let this verse search our hearts and guide our steps today. We thank You that Your wisdom begins with purity, calling us away from hidden motives and divided loyalties. When we are tempted to take shortcuts or compromise our integrity for the sake of an easy way out, bring the purity of Your truth to our remembrance. Let Your Word shine directly upon our present situation, lighting our feet so we do not stumble into self-will. Keep us tethered to Your truth, and let Your precepts govern our choices when the world pulls us in a dozen different directions. In Jesus' name, we pray. Amen.

## Scene 4 — Wise Counsel & Tested Motives

As we seek to walk in this heavenly discernment, we must also examine the quality of our relationships and the influences we allow into our deliberations. Scripture reminds us that in the multitude of counsellors there is safety, and that godly wisdom is ever ready to listen. It is not stubborn, nor does it close its ears to correction. The text tells us that the wisdom from above is gentle and easy to be intreated, meaning it possesses a teachable spirit that is approachable and willing to weigh counsel with humility. At the same time, this discernment requires us to test our motives before the Lord, asking Him to search us and try our thoughts. We acknowledge that prayer walks alongside the practical wisdom of wise counsel, and that we must never use spiritual language to bypass the honest, grounded advice of those who walk closely with God. Lord, grant us the teachable spirit described in Your Word. Save us from the stubborn pride that refuses correction or insists on its own infallibility. When we seek advice from others, give us the discernment to weigh their words against the unchanging standard of Scripture. If our own hearts are harboring secret biases, stubborn preferences, or an unwillingness to hear what is right, expose those motives by Your Spirit. Make us gentle and approachable, ready to yield to sound counsel and peaceable instruction, so that our steps may be established in righteousness and truth. In Jesus' name, we pray. Amen.

## Scene 5 — Waiting With Patience & Ordered Steps

There are seasons when the hardest part of seeking guidance is simply waiting for the Lord's timing without forcing our own solutions. We grow restless in the quiet intervals between decisions, wondering if our stillness is being wasted. Yet Psalm thirty-seven assures us that the steps of a good man are ordered by the Lord, and that He delights in his way. Even if we stumble, we shall not be utterly cast down, for the Lord upholds us with His hand. This heavenly wisdom is full of mercy and good fruits, reminding us that God deals patiently with our frailties and works quietly in our waiting. He does not rush our growth, nor does He abandon us when we feel uncertain about how to proceed. Heavenly Father, teach us the holy art of waiting upon You without losing heart. When the temptation arises to rush ahead of Your cloud and make decisions out of panic or exhaustion, grant us the grace to be still. Remind us that Your mercy covers our mistakes and that Your hand is strong enough to uphold us when our footing feels unsure. Order our steps according to Your holy will, and keep us from wandering into paths of our own making. Fill our days with the good fruits of patience, kindness, and steadfast trust, knowing that You are faithful to complete the work You have begun in us. In Jesus' name, we pray. Amen.

## Scene 6 — Committing the Way & Closing Benediction

We come now to the threshold of the rest of our day and the decisions still waiting for us, committing our work and our ways completely into the hands of our faithful Creator. We do not ask for a guarantee of an easy road, but for the presence of the Guide who has promised never to leave us nor forsake us. The wisdom from above is without partiality and without hypocrisy; it is transparent, steady, and anchored in the unchanging love of God. We leave our confusions, our questions, and our plans at His feet, trusting that He will provide the exact measure of discernment we need when the moment for action arrives. May the Lord bless you and keep you. May He make His face shine upon you and be gracious unto you. May He lift up the light of His countenance upon you, and give you that pure, peaceable wisdom which guides every step in His holy fear.

## Scene 1 — The Sunrise Hook and Directing the Prayer

The morning light reaches across the floor before the emails start or the demands of the day take hold. This is the quiet hour when you step into the day with God, bringing the weights and uncertainties of the hours ahead. There are decisions waiting for you that you do not yet know how to solve, and paths that look tangled before you have even taken your first step. In moments when the mind feels crowded and your own wisdom falls short, the morning invitation is to turn upward and lift your voice to the One who sees the entire road from its beginning to its end. You do not have to walk into this day leaning on your own understanding or guessing at the right turn. Let us bring our need for direction to His throne right now, as we read the promise recorded in the seventy-third Psalm. Thou shalt guide me with thy counsel, and afterward receive me to glory.

## Scene 2 — New Mercies and Counsel at Dawn

The dawn arrives without asking for our credentials or demanding that we have yesterday completely figured out. Every morning brings a fresh measure of grace, reminding us that we are not left to wander through life on our own strength. When the Asaph of old looked at the confusion of the world and his own failing heart, he remembered that God was his portion forever, holding him by his right hand. Matthew Henry notes in his commentary on these verses that those who have God for their guide have the best of counsel, for His wisdom never fails and His eye is upon the righteous. You may be facing a choice today where two paths look equally difficult, or where the next correct step is entirely hidden from your view. That is precisely where the counsel of the Lord meets you. He does not give direction in a hurried whisper that gets lost in the noise of the day; He offers steady, unfailing guidance to the soul that waits before Him. Let us ask Him to make His way plain before our face this morning, laying down our own anxiety and receiving His quiet instruction for the decisions we must make.

## Scene 3 — Trust and Guidance for the Decisions Ahead

Every workday brings its own intersections, meetings, conversations, and moments where a hasty word or a wrong turn could cost peace of mind. We bring those specific hours into the light of this prayer. Lord, we confess that we often lean too quickly on our own judgment, moving fast because the world demands speed, only to find ourselves entangled in things we never intended to enter. We ask You to grant us the discernment that comes from Your Spirit. When we do not know whether to speak or remain silent, whether to press forward or wait patiently, let Your counsel be the voice behind us saying, this is the way, walk ye in it. Direct our thoughts when we plan our schedules. Overrule our stubbornness when we want our own way rather than Your best way. Keep our feet from slipping on the paths of hurry and worry. We commit every responsibility of this morning and afternoon into Your keeping, trusting that the God who orders the sun to rise also orders the steps of those who look to Him for guidance.

## Scene 4 — Favour, Shield, and Protection for the Day

The road through the day is not always smooth, and unexpected friction often meets us before the afternoon arrives. We need more than good organization; we need the protecting presence of God around our lives, our families, and our work. In the words of the psalmist, we rest in the truth that God is the strength of our heart and our portion forever. Let His favour be a shield about you as you step out today, guarding your mind from discouragement and your spirit from fear. Where opposition arises or patience is tested by difficult circumstances, let the quiet assurance of His presence steady your nerves. You are not walking into an unguarded space. The same God who holds the universe together has promised to hold your hand through the ordinary, demanding moments of this day. Let us give thanks that His protection does not depend on our perfection, but on His steadfast covenant love that never lets go of His own.

## Scene 5 — Blessing Over Work, Family, and Home

The grace we receive in the morning is meant to overflow into every place our feet will tread today. We speak a blessing now over the work of your hands, whether it is labour at a desk, service to others, care for a home, or tasks that feel unseen and repetitive. Lord, establish the work of our hands upon us; yea, the work of our hands establish thou it. Bless our families, our households, and every person we will encounter in the hours ahead. Where there is weariness in a home, bring renewal. Where there is tension in a relationship, bring a spirit of grace and understanding. Let the peace that guards our hearts in this morning prayer go with us into conversations, into traffic, into quiet rooms, and into crowded places. May we carry the fragrance of Christ's patience and kindness into every interaction, leaving every space a little more anchored in His love than we found it.

## Scene 6 — The Sending Benediction

As this morning hour draws to a close, we look forward not just to the tasks of today, but to the great horizon that awaits every believer at the end of our earthly days. The verse that guides us today holds both a present promise and an eternal hope: Thou shalt guide me with thy counsel, and afterward receive me to glory. The guidance we receive now in our daily walk is the very path that leads us safely home at last. Let your heart be at rest, knowing that the God who directs your steps this morning will also keep you until the very end.

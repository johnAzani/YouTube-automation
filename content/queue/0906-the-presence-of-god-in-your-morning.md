## Scene 1 — The Sunrise Hook & Directing the Prayer

The coffee is already brewing, or perhaps the house is still quiet while the first light of dawn begins to trace the edges of your windows. It is the moment before the emails, the commute, or the demands of the family begin to pull your focus in a dozen different directions. You have come here to steady your spirit, to turn your heart toward the light of the morning, and to acknowledge that you are not walking into this day alone. Even when the morning feels heavy or uncertain, there is a promise that reaches across the centuries to find you exactly where you are. In the book of Genesis, Jacob had spent the night in a desolate place, resting his head on a stone, yet he awoke to a profound realization.

And Jacob awaked out of his sleep, and he said, Surely the LORD is in this place; and I knew it not.

These words capture the shift from confusion to clarity. Matthew Henry reminds us that God is often present with His people even when they are not aware of it. You may feel like you are standing in a wilderness of tasks or waiting on a decision that seems impossible to navigate. But God is there. Directing your prayer toward Him this morning is the act of acknowledging His presence before you face the first challenge of the day. You are not asking God to come into your day; you are asking for the eyes to see that He is already there, working in the corners of your life, standing in the doorway of your home, and walking the path that lies ahead.

## Scene 2 — New Mercies & Faithfulness at Dawn

Every sunrise is a testimony to the faithfulness of the Creator. We often look for God in the spectacular or the miraculous, forgetting that He is most surely found in the consistency of the morning light. As the sun rises over the horizon, it clears away the shadows of the night, just as God’s mercies are new for you today. You do not carry the burdens of yesterday into this sunrise. The grace that sustained you through the seasons of your past is the same grace that meets you at this altar this morning.

It is easy to focus on what we lack or what we failed to accomplish yesterday. But the covenant mercy of the Lord does not rely on our performance. It is founded on His character. When Jacob realized the Lord was in his presence, he was forced to drop his defenses and accept that he was standing on holy ground. You are on holy ground today. Your kitchen, your office, your car, and your quiet study are all places where the Lord has placed His feet. He is not distant, and He is not unconcerned with the small details of your commute or the quiet fears you harbor about the afternoon. His faithfulness is like the dawn — it is inevitable, it is bright, and it is a gift that you did not earn, yet you are invited to walk in it.

## Scene 3 — Trust & Guidance for the Decisions Ahead

There is a natural tendency to want to map out the entire day, to anticipate every conversation, and to steel ourselves against potential conflict. We look for patterns and rely on our own understanding to ensure our day goes according to plan. But the journey of faith is built on the opposite posture — the posture of the traveler who recognizes he is being led by a greater hand. Jacob, having caught a glimpse of the reality of heaven’s gate, had to move forward into the unknown, trusting that the God he encountered would guide his steps.

As you consider the hours ahead, invite the Lord into the decision-making process. If there is a meeting that weighs on you, or a conversation you are hesitant to have, release the outcome into His hands. You are not steering this ship alone. The straight path is promised to those who acknowledge Him in all their ways, and this morning is your chance to do just that. When you feel the pressure to figure it all out, stop and remember that the Lord is already in the space of your upcoming afternoon. He is in the boardrooms, He is in the schools, and He is in the silence between your own anxious thoughts. Lean into the truth that He is the architect of your day and the navigator of your soul.

## Scene 4 — Favour, Shield & Protection for the Day

There is a sense of awe that comes with knowing the Creator of the universe is walking with you. It is a protective, shielding favour. When Jacob called the place the house of God and the gate of heaven, he was acknowledging that he was under divine jurisdiction. You, too, are under that same covering. The favour of the Lord is a shield that goes before you to prepare the way and stands behind you to guard your steps. This is not a promise that you will face no difficulty; it is a promise that you will face no difficulty alone.

And he was afraid, and said, How dreadful is this place! this is none other but the house of God, and this is the gate of heaven.

The fear Jacob felt was not the fear of terror, but the fear of reverence — a profound recognition of the greatness of God. When you recognize that your daily life is being held in the palm of His hand, your anxieties begin to lose their edge. Let this morning prayer be your shield. As you step out, declare that your workplace is a place where God is at work. Declare that your commute is a place where His presence resides. You are protected not because you are perfect, but because you belong to the One who owns the gate of heaven. His favour is your atmosphere. Breathe it in, and stand in the confidence that no situation today is outside the reach of His sovereign grace.

## Scene 5 — Blessing Over Work, Family & Home

Now, let us speak a blessing over the work of your hands and the people who fill your life. The same God who met Jacob in the wilderness is the God of your home and your household. Whether you are caring for children, providing for your family, or serving others through your profession, ask that the Lord would establish the work of your hands today. May He bless you as you go out and as you come in. May He shine His face upon your relationships, bringing peace where there has been tension and clarity where there has been confusion.

Jacob rose up early in the morning, and took the stone that he had put for his pillows, and set it up for a pillar, and poured oil upon the top of it.

This act of setting up a pillar was an act of dedication. He was marking the place where he met God so he would not forget. Today, let your life be a pillar. Let your actions, your kindness, and your patience be the oil poured out, marking this day as one dedicated to the Lord. Bless your family with words of grace. Bless your colleagues with the spirit of integrity. Whatever tasks require your attention, perform them as though you are working directly for the One who walked with Jacob. You are a vessel of His presence, and through your interactions today, you can reveal that the Lord is indeed in this place, and in this day.

## Scene 6 — The Sending Benediction

As you prepare to turn from this screen and enter the fullness of your day, carry this truth with you like a compass: you are walking in the presence of the Almighty. The stone that Jacob used as a pillow became a monument to God’s faithfulness, and your morning today can be the foundation for everything that follows. You are sent forth not in your own strength, but in the power of His Spirit, refreshed and ready to witness His hand in the ordinary moments of the hours ahead.

May the Lord bless you and keep you. May the light of His countenance shine upon your path, illuminating the way forward and softening the rugged places. May you walk with the quiet assurance that the gate of heaven is always open to you, and that no matter where you find yourself, the Lord is there.

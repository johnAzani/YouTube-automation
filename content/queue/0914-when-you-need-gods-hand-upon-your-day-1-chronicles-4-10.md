## Scene 1 — The Sunrise Hook & Directing the Prayer

The house is quiet, and before the first email lands or the traffic begins to stir, there is a quiet space to lift your voice upward. This is the hour to direct your prayer to the Lord and look up, laying out the weight of the hours ahead upon His mercy. You wake up with tasks to manage, decisions to weigh, and a quiet desire that this day might not be spent in vain strength, but touched by something higher. Jabez knew this posture well when he cried out in the ancient days, not asking for mere earthly display, but for the substance of God's own hand in his life. 

Let us hear the scripture as it was written in the book of First Chronicles, chapter four, verse ten. And Jabez called on the God of Israel, saying, Oh that thou wouldest bless me indeed, and enlarge my coast, and that thine hand might be with me, and that thou wouldest keep me from evil, that it may not grieve me! And God granted him that which he requested. 

Lord God of Israel, we come to You at the break of this new morning because we know that every good gift comes down from Your throne. You are the author of this day, and You have measured out its hours before we even stepped into them. As we begin our morning worship, we ask not for our own self-willed ambitions, but for the genuine, unmixed blessing that only You can give. Strip away the distractions that clamour for our attention before our feet even hit the floor, and anchor our hearts in Your steadfast grace. Direct our thoughts away from anxiety and toward Your great faithfulness, which is new every single morning. We look to You because our help comes from no other source, and we place the unfolding hours of this day entirely into Your keeping.

## Scene 2 — New Mercies & Faithfulness at Dawn

The morning light creeps across the floorboards, bringing with it a reminder that yesterday's failures do not set the terms for today. Whatever weariness you carried to bed last night, and whatever unfinished trouble you woke up with, God's mercies have arrived fresh with the sunrise. Matthew Henry reminds us that Jabez prayed with a holy earnestness, setting his faith upon the covenant God who hears and answers. He did not trust in his own pedigree or his own strength, but cast himself wholly upon the mercy of the Almighty. 

Father, we thank You that Your compassions fail not, but are renewed with every dawn. We confess that we often carry the heavy baggage of yesterday into the fresh light of a new day, letting old worries dictate our present joy. Wash our minds clean with Your grace this morning. Grant us the quiet assurance that Your covenant faithfulness is deeper than our frailties and wider than our shortcomings. When we look at the blank page of today, let us see it not as a test of our own endurance, but as an arena for Your grace to be displayed. Let Your mercy meet every point of our uncertainty, smoothing the rough edges of our anxiety and filling our chests with the steady, calm breath of Your peace.

## Scene 3 — Trust & Guidance for the Decisions Ahead

There are roads to travel today, conversations that require wisdom, and responsibilities that may stretch beyond our natural capacity to manage. It is easy in the quiet of the morning to feel ready for anything, but we know how quickly the afternoon can bring complications we did not foresee. This is why we pray the ancient petition: enlarge our coast, grant us the room to walk in Your purpose, and keep our steps from straying into paths of our own making. We do not lean on our own understanding as we face the calendar of this day.

Lord of all wisdom, we commit every meeting, every decision, and every interaction of this day into Your hands. Enlarge our capacity to love, to serve, and to endure for Your name's sake. Where doors need to be opened according to Your will, open them wide; where doors need to stay shut, grant us the discernment not to force our way through. We ask that You order our steps so that we do not stumble into foolish choices or react out of impatience. Give us clarity when things become muddy, and give us a quiet spirit when the pace of the day quickens around us. Let our work today be established by Your hands, so that whatever we touch might bear the mark of Your integrity and truth.

## Scene 4 — Favour, Shield & Protection for the Day

We live in a world that presses hard against the soul, where sharp words, unexpected friction, and hidden traps can derail our peace before noon. We need more than good intentions to carry us through; we need the tangible presence of God going before us and standing as a rear guard behind us. Jabez understood this danger when he prayed that God's hand might be with him and that he would be kept from evil so that it might not grieve him. That prayer is just as fitting for your drive to work, your desk, your kitchen table, and your interactions today.

O God, we pray for Your protecting hand to rest heavily upon us throughout these coming hours. Be a shield around our minds when discouragement whispers, and be a guard over our lips when provocation rises. Keep us from the evil that lurks in our own shortcuts, in our pride, and in our hurried reactions. When unexpected trials or harsh words come our way, let Your grace be the cushion that absorbs the blow so that our hearts are not embittered or overcome with grief. Let Your favour surround us like a shield, not so that we might boast in ourselves, but so that others might see Your goodness shining through our preservation.

## Scene 5 — Blessing Over Work, Family & Home

The morning prayer does not stay locked inside the walls of our own personal devotions; it reaches out to touch our homes, our families, and everyone we will encounter before the sun goes down. The people we love are facing their own burdens today, carrying their own silent worries out into the world. We lift them up now, asking that the same blessing we seek for ourselves would overflow onto our households, our communities, and our workplaces. 

Heavenly Father, we pour out our intercession upon our families, our friends, and our homes this morning. Where there is strain, bring Your healing; where there is worry, bring Your quiet assurance. Bless our work, whether it is done in an office, in a vehicle, or within the walls of our homes. Let the labour of our hands be fruitful and honest, bringing no shame to Your name. We pray for the people we will meet today — the cashier, the colleague, the stranger on the street, the neighbour whose burdens we do not know. Let a measure of Your grace touch them through us. Make us instruments of Your peace and quiet bearers of Your light in a weary world, that Your name might be praised in our going out and our coming in.

## Scene 6 — The Sending Benediction

As the morning deepens and the world around us starts to move at full speed, we do not leave this place of prayer unprotected or unprovided for. We stand in the light of Your countenance, ready to step into the day because we know who goes with us. The prayer of Jabez was not left unanswered in the ancient text, and Your ear is no less open to the cry of Your children today. 

And God granted him that which he requested. 

Lord, as we step out into the noise and the tasks of this new day, let Your face shine upon us. Grant us strength that matches the hours, grace that covers our mistakes, and a deep, unshakeable awareness that we are never walking alone. May Your hand direct our steps, may Your blessing rest upon our labour, and may Your peace keep our hearts steady from morning until night.

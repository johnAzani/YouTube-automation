## Scene 1 — Gathering Around the Family Altar

Welcome, beloved brothers and sisters, husbands, wives, parents, and children, to this gathering around the family altar. As we quiet our hearts and center our minds upon the unchanging truth of God's Holy Word, let us take our stand upon that great declaration of Joshua, that as for me and my house, we will serve the Lord. In every generation, the home that makes God its refuge finds a peace that the shifting economies and troubled currents of this world can never dismantle. Today, we bring every corner of our domestic life, every room under our roof, every financial care, and every unspoken burden before the throne of heavenly grace. We gather not in our own strength or self-sufficiency, but anchored entirely in the boundless mercy of our Creator, who formed the families of the earth and covenanted to be a shield and an exceeding great reward to those who walk uprightly before Him. Let us settle our spirits in deep reverence, knowing that where two or three are gathered in His name, He is in the midst of them, bending His ear to hear the intercessions of a believing household.

## Scene 2 — Building the House Upon God's Wisdom

Except the Lord build the house, they labour in vain that build it. These profound words from the Psalms remind us that every true foundation must be laid with reverence, obedience, and total dependence upon divine wisdom. As Matthew Henry observed in his rich commentary on Christian households, a well-ordered family is a little church under its own roof, where the daily worship of Almighty God sanctifies the labors of the week and guards the threshold against the restless anxieties of this life. When we build upon God's wisdom, our daily toil is transformed from mere survival into an act of faithful stewardship. We do not lean on our own understanding or fret over tomorrow's provisions, for we know that the God who commands the sun to rise and feeds the fowls of the air is intimately acquainted with the exact needs of our table. Let us pray this day that our home may be garrisoned by His counsel, that every decision made within these walls honors His name, and that our children witness a living, breathing testimony of trust in a faithful God.

## Scene 3 — Covering Children, Sons, Daughters & Generations

And all thy children shall be taught of the Lord; and great shall be the peace of thy children. This magnificent promise from Isaiah, echoing the imagery of Psalm 128 where the children are like olive plants round about the table, calls us to fervent intercession for the rising generation. Our children are not given to us merely for a season of earthly rearing; they are arrows in the hand of a mighty warrior, entrusted to our care to be sharpened in the ways of righteousness, truth, and grace. Let us lift up our sons and our daughters this hour — whether they are resting under our roof as little ones or walking out into the wider world as young adults. We pray that the hand of the Lord may hedge them in from every snare of the enemy, preserving their minds from confusion, guarding their footsteps in times of temptation, and planting deep within their hearts an unshakeable love for the Saviour. May their lives bear the rich, oil-producing fruit of the Holy Spirit, reflecting integrity, kindness, and devotion to Christ Jesus all the days of their lives.

## Scene 4 — Unity, Peace & Grace Within Marriage and Relationships

Behold, how good and how pleasant it is for brethren to dwell together in unity. Within the walls of our homes, the adversary seeks to sow discord, impatience, and weariness, but the grace of God is infinitely greater than every domestic friction. As we gather at this altar, we invite the reconciling, healing peace of Jesus Christ to flow freely through every marriage and every relationship under this roof. Let husbands love their wives as Christ loved the church, and let wives reverence their husbands, walking together as heirs of the grace of life. Let patience have her perfect work when words are hasty, and let quick forgiveness and tender mercy replace pride and offense. As Isaiah prophesied, our people shall dwell in a peaceable habitation, and in sure dwellings, and in quiet resting places. Let the atmosphere of our home be characterized by gentleness, mutual respect, and unfeigned love, so that anyone who crosses our threshold immediately senses the sweet, calming presence of the Holy Spirit ruling in our midst.

## Scene 5 — Honest Provision, Health & Protection Over Every Threshold

But my God shall supply all your need according to his riches in glory by Christ Jesus. This glorious verse from Philippians is the bedrock of our confidence for daily provision and temporal preservation. We do not come to God with anxious greed or demands for earthly luxury, but with honest, grateful hearts for our daily bread, for the strength of our hands to labor, and for the wisdom to steward every resource He places in our possession. Heavenly Father, we acknowledge that You are Jehovah-Jireh, the God who sees ahead and provides abundantly for every sparrow of the field and every member of Your covenant family. We ask that You bless the work of our hands, grant health and vitality to every member of this household, and provide for every financial obligation, mortgage, utility, and sustenance needed for our daily walk. Set a fiery hedge of protection around our property, our incoming, and our outgoing, preserving us from pestilence, sudden terror, and every device of darkness, that we may serve You without fear in holiness and righteousness all our days.

## Scene 6 — Aaronic Household Blessing & Covenant Benediction

And now, as we seal this time of household prayer, let us lift our hearts to receive the ancient blessing that God commanded Moses to place upon His people, knowing that His name is recorded over this home for evermore. The Lord bless thee, and keep thee. The Lord make his face shine upon thee, and be gracious unto thee. The Lord lift up his countenance upon thee, and give thee peace. May the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, the love of God our Heavenly Father, and the sweet, comforting fellowship of the Holy Spirit abide, rest, and remain with each one of you, and with all your loved ones, both now and forevermore.

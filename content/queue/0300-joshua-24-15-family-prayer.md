## Scene 1 — Gathering Around the Family Altar

Grace and peace to you and to every loved one under your roof, and welcome to The Family Altar. Today we pause together around the unchanging Word of God to build a quiet altar of thanksgiving, covering, and consecration over your household. Hear the enduring declaration of King James Scripture in Joshua chapter twenty-four, verse fifteen: And if it seem evil unto you to serve the LORD, choose you this day whom ye will serve; whether the gods which your fathers served that were on the other side of the flood, or the gods of the Amorites, in whose land ye dwell: but as for me and my house, we will serve the LORD. Notice that Joshua did not wait to see which way the surrounding culture drifted before setting the direction of his home. Standing as an elder statesman who had walked with God through wilderness and promise alike, he drove a covenant stake deep into the ground of his dwelling. Right now, in calm and reverent faith, we lift your home, your spouse, your children, and everyone dear to you before the throne of grace.

## Scene 2 — Building the House Upon God's Wisdom

Look back one verse earlier to Joshua chapter twenty-four, verse fourteen: Now therefore fear the LORD, and serve him in sincerity and in truth. Matthew Henry, in his Commentary on the Whole Bible, writes with warm pastoral clarity on Joshua's resolve that those who lead a household are entrusted with the care of souls under their roof, and that true family religion turns an ordinary dwelling into a little sanctuary where God is honoured daily. Charles Spurgeon likewise observed in his sermons on household piety that a home without prayer is like a house without a roof, exposed to every passing storm, whereas a home where Scripture and supplication rise together stands sheltered beneath the wings of the Almighty. Heavenly Father, we invite Thy wisdom and reverent truth to govern our household today. We renounce haste, strife, and worldly anxiety, and we gladly declare over every room of our dwelling that Jesus Christ is Lord of this house.

## Scene 3 — Covering Children, Sons, Daughters & Generations

When Joshua spoke of his house, he spoke with generational vision — covering sons, daughters, grandchildren, and everyone abiding within his gates. Later in verse thirty-one of this same chapter, Scripture records the fruit of such faithful leadership: And Israel served the LORD all the days of Joshua, and all the days of the elders that overlived Joshua, and which had known all the works of the LORD, that he had done for Israel. Faithful prayer planted inside a home bears fruit long after the moment it is spoken. Merciful Father, we lift up every child, young person, son, daughter, and grandchild connected to each family praying right now. Guard their minds in pure truth, surround them with wise and upright companions, protect their steps in school, work, and travel, and draw each of their hearts into a living, personal love for our Saviour. Where any loved one has wandered or grown weary in faith, let Thy gentle mercy bring them safely home.

## Scene 4 — Unity, Peace & Grace Within Marriage and Relationships

Joshua's words, as for me and my house, remind us that spiritual strength begins with unity of heart inside the home. Where patience, humility, and quick forgiveness dwell, contention loses its foothold. As Matthew Henry notes, serving the Lord together sweetens every domestic relationship and binds hearts in a loyalty deeper than fleeting emotion. Gracious Lord, pour out fresh tenderness, understanding, and peace across every marriage and family bond represented at this altar today. Heal strained conversations, soften sharp words before they are spoken, and clothe us with kindness, meekness, and forbearance toward one another. Let the peace of God rule in our hearts so that our homes become quiet resting places of encouragement, honesty, and unfeigned Christlike love.

Remember also Joshua twenty-four verse twenty-six and verse twenty-seven, where Joshua wrote these words in the book of the law of God and set up a great stone under an oak by the sanctuary of the Lord as a witness unto the people. Every daily prayer offered inside your living room or beside your kitchen table stands as a spiritual memorial stone testifying that your family belongs unto the Most High. Even when ordinary weekdays feel busy or unseen, heaven records every faithful petition whispered over the ones you love.

## Scene 5 — Honest Provision, Health & Protection Over Every Threshold

Throughout Joshua chapter twenty-four, the Lord reminded His people how He had guided their steps, preserved their lives along the way, and given them fruitful land and vineyards by grace. Thou art still Jehovah-Jireh, the Lord who sees ahead and provides for His covenant households. Father in heaven, we ask Thee to bless the honest labour of our hands and grant sound wisdom in every financial, family, and vocational decision before us. Supply every needful provision according to Thy riches in glory by Christ Jesus. Set a hedge of peaceable protection around our doors, our windows, our health, and our daily going out and coming in, preserving our households from unseen harm and anxious care.

## Scene 6 — Aaronic Household Blessing & Covenant Benediction

As we seal this time around The Family Altar, we stand anew upon Joshua's unshakeable confession: But as for me and my house, we will serve the LORD. Father, thank Thee for hearing our household petitions and writing Thy faithfulness upon the doorposts of our homes today. Now may the LORD bless thee, and keep thee; the LORD make his face shine upon thee, and be gracious unto thee; the LORD lift up his countenance upon thee, and give thee and thy household peace, both today and forevermore.

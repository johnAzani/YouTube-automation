## Scene 1 — Fear Thou Not; For I Am with Thee (Verse 10a)

The most repeated command in Scripture is: fear not.

Fear thou not; for I am with thee.

You are not alone. The Creator of the universe is with you in your room tonight.

His presence eliminates the need for fear.

Heavenly Father, as the darkness gathers around me this night, and my mind races with anxieties of tomorrow or regrets of today, I often find myself overwhelmed by a quiet dread. I pray that Your promise, "Fear thou not; for I am with thee," would settle deeply within my spirit. Remind me, O God, that Your divine presence is not merely a concept but a palpable reality that fills this very room, pushing back every shadow of doubt and apprehension. Let me rest in the assurance that I am never truly alone, for You, the sovereign Lord, are intimately near, a faithful companion through the watch hours.

## Scene 2 — Be Not Dismayed; For I Am Thy God (Verse 10b)

Dismay comes from looking at your circumstances instead of your God.

Be not dismayed; for I am thy God.

He is not a distant deity. He is your God — personal, near, and involved.

Look to Him, not to the storm.

Lord, my circumstances often loom large in my sight, threatening to engulf my peace and fill me with dismay. Tonight, I lift my gaze from the turbulent waters of my situation and fix it upon You, my unfailing God. Let the truth resonate within me that You are not a distant, uninterested power, but my personal, intimate God who cares deeply for every detail of my life. Strengthen my resolve to trust in Your divine wisdom and sovereign control, knowing that no challenge is too great for You, my ever-present help. May Your faithful nature overshadow all my worries.

## Scene 3 — I Will Strengthen Thee (Verse 10c)

When you feel weak, He strengthens you.

I will strengthen thee.

Not gradually over months, but now — in this moment, in this night.

Receive divine strength for every weakness you feel.

Almighty God, I confess my own limitations and the weariness that often settles upon my soul at the close of the day. There are moments when my spirit feels utterly depleted, and I yearn for an infusion of Your divine might. I claim Your powerful promise, "I will strengthen thee," believing that Your supernatural vigor is available to me right now, not dependent on my own diminishing reserves. Pour into me a fresh surge of endurance and fortitude, enabling me to face tomorrow's demands and to rest peacefully tonight, held firm by Your inexhaustible strength.

## Scene 4 — Yea, I Will Help Thee (Verse 10d)

Help is not a maybe; it is a definite promise.

Yea, I will help thee.

God's help is immediate, sufficient, and perfectly timed.

You do not have to figure it out alone. He will help you.

Father, I often carry the burden of trying to solve every problem on my own, feeling the weight of responsibility pressing down. Tonight, I lay down my striving and wholeheartedly embrace Your unwavering declaration, "Yea, I will help thee." I trust that Your assistance is not a mere suggestion but a guaranteed reality that will manifest precisely when and how it is most needed. Guide my steps, illuminate my path, and intervene in the areas where I am powerless, knowing that Your divine aid is perfectly tailored and always sufficient to navigate the complexities before me.

## Scene 5 — Yea, I Will Uphold Thee with the Right Hand of My Righteousness (Verse 10e)

The right hand of righteousness is the hand of justice, power, and faithfulness.

Yea, I will uphold thee with the right hand of my righteousness.

He holds you up. He sustains you. He carries you.

You will not fall, because His righteous hand is beneath you.

Righteous God, there are times when I feel as if I am teetering on the edge, vulnerable to falling and uncertain of my footing in a chaotic world. I find immense comfort and unwavering security in Your promise, "Yea, I will uphold thee with the right hand of my righteousness." Let me feel the firm, unyielding grip of Your mighty hand, knowing that Your power, justice, and faithfulness are the very foundations upon which I stand. Sustain me through every trial, steady me in every tremor, and ensure that I remain steadfast, completely secured by Your perfect righteousness.

## Scene 6 — Sleep Upheld by God (Verse 10 Synthesis)

From fear not, to I am with you, to I am your God, to I will strengthen, to I will help, to I will uphold — every promise removes a reason to be anxious.

Tonight, fear not. You are with God. You are strengthened. You are helped. You are upheld.

Sleep in the grip of His righteous right hand.

Heavenly Father, as I prepare to close my eyes this night, I gather all the magnificent assurances from Your word, knowing that each one is a pillar of truth for my soul. From Your abiding presence that dispels all fear, to Your personal identity as my God, to Your strengthening hand, Your definite help, and Your righteous upholding, my heart finds rest. Grant me a deep and tranquil sleep, free from the burdens and anxieties of the day, knowing that I am cradled safely in the palm of Your hand. May Your peace, which surpasses all understanding, guard my heart and mind through Christ Jesus, until I awaken refreshed and ready to honor You anew.

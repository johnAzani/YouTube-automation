## Scene 1 — My Soul Shall Be Satisfied as with Marrow and Fatness (Verse 5a)

The deepest hunger of your soul is not for food, but for God.

My soul shall be satisfied as with marrow and fatness.

Nothing in this world can satisfy the spiritual hunger within you — only God can.

Come to bed tonight hungry for Him, and leave satisfied.

Oh Lord, as I lie down tonight, I confess that my soul often seeks satisfaction in fleeting comforts and earthly desires, yet remains perpetually thirsty. I pray that You would draw my heart away from the emptiness of worldly pursuits and truly fill me with Your divine presence. Let Your spirit nourish me more deeply than any physical meal, satisfying the profound longing within me completely, so that I may rest in the fullness of Your incomparable grace, knowing my spirit is entirely content and deeply satiated only in You, my God.

## Scene 2 — And My Mouth Shall Praise Thee with Joyful Lips (Verse 5b)

Satisfaction naturally produces praise.

And my mouth shall praise thee with joyful lips.

Before you sleep, let your last words be words of thanksgiving.

Joyful lips quiet a troubled heart.

Father, even when anxieties swirl and my mind is tempted to dwell on worries, I choose to offer You praise. Let my final thoughts and unspoken words before sleep be a joyful chorus of gratitude for Your goodness and unwavering faithfulness throughout this day. Inspire my heart to bless Your holy name, not because circumstances are perfect, but because You are perfect and worthy of all adoration. May my lips speak forth a genuine, heartfelt thanksgiving that silences every fear and brings a profound peace to my spirit as I surrender this night to You.

## Scene 3 — When I Remember Thee Upon My Bed (Verse 6a)

The bed is not just a place for sleep; it is a place for remembrance.

When I remember thee upon my bed, and meditate on thee in the night watches.

In the quiet watches of the night, when the world is silent, remember God.

Let your thoughts drift toward Him, not toward your problems.

Almighty God, as I settle into my bed, I acknowledge how easily my mind can become a battlefield for worries and regrets that steal my peace. I deliberately turn my gaze upon You now, inviting Your presence into this quiet space, allowing Your tranquility to settle over me. Help me to remember Your faithfulness from days past, Your constant care that has brought me through every challenge. May my mind not replay the day's troubles, but instead meditate on Your unchanging character and Your boundless lovingkindness, allowing Your peace to fill every corner of my thoughts as I rest.

## Scene 4 — And Meditate on Thee in the Night Watches (Verse 6b)

The night watches — those hours when sleep is elusive — become sacred when filled with meditation on God.

Turn your insomnia into intercession. Turn your restlessness into reverence.

God is present in every watch of the night.

You are never alone in the darkness.

Heavenly Father, when sleep eludes me and the night stretches long, I commit these restless hours into Your hands, refusing to let them be moments of anxiety. I pray that You would transform any discomfort or wakefulness into an opportunity for deeper communion with You. Rather than tossing and turning in frustration, help me to consciously turn my thoughts to Your Word and Your magnificent promises. Let this quiet darkness become a sacred chamber where I feel Your comforting presence near, knowing that even in the deepest watches of the night, You are ever-present and attentive to my heart.

## Scene 5 — Because Thou Hast Been My Help (Verse 7a)

Your confidence for tonight is built on His help in the past.

Because thou hast been my help.

He helped you yesterday. He helped you last week. He helped you through every crisis you thought would break you.

The same help is available tonight.

Gracious God, as I prepare to close my eyes, I lean on the unwavering truth that You have always been my helper, my steadfast support through every season of life. I recall with profound gratitude how You have guided me, protected me, and sustained me through countless trials and uncertainties, never once abandoning me. Tonight, I cast all my burdens upon You, trusting that Your strength is made perfect in my weakness. Your past faithfulness is my anchor and my unwavering assurance; therefore, I can lie down in peace, knowing that Your divine assistance is just as real and potent for me in this moment as it has ever been.

## Scene 6 — Therefore in the Shadow of Thy Wings Will I Rejoice (Verse 7b)

The shadow of His wings is the safest place in the universe.

Therefore in the shadow of thy wings will I rejoice.

Rejoice not because your circumstances are perfect, but because your Protector is perfect.

Sleep under the shadow of His wings, and rejoice.

Merciful Father, it is in the blessed shadow of Your mighty wings that I find my truest refuge and deepest joy, a sanctuary secure from all harm. Tonight, I choose to rejoice, not because everything in my life is perfect or without challenge, but because You, my sovereign Protector, are always perfect in Your unfailing love and infinite power. Cover me with Your comforting presence, guard me from all harm both seen and unseen, and fill my heart with a quiet confidence that transcends any earthly concern. Let this assurance of Your constant care bring a profound sense of gladness and security as I rest securely within Your tender embrace throughout the night.

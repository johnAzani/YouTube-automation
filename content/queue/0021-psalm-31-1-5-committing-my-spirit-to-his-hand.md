## Scene 1 — In Thee, O LORD, Do I Put My Trust (Verse 1a)

Before sleep comes, make the ancient declaration: I put my trust in You, LORD.

In thee, O LORD, do I put my trust; let me never be ashamed.

Trust is the opposite of anxiety. Where trust is present, fear has no room to stand.

Tonight, place every unresolved worry into the hands of the One who cannot be ashamed.
O LORD, as darkness falls and my mind begins to wander, I consciously choose to anchor my soul in Your steadfastness. I bring before You every lingering concern, every unanswered question from the day, and every apprehension about tomorrow. Let my trust in You be a firm foundation, unshaken by the anxieties that often seek to steal my peace. When fears whisper doubts, remind me of Your unchanging nature and Your perfect faithfulness. Prevent any feeling of shame from overwhelming me, for in You alone do I find true security and acceptance, knowing You will never abandon those who seek Your refuge.

## Scene 2 — Deliver Me in Thy Righteousness (Verse 1b)

Deliverance does not come from your own righteousness, but from His.

Deliver me in thy righteousness.

You are rescued not because you have earned it, but because He is righteous.

Rest in the deliverance that comes from His character, not your performance.
Almighty God, I humbly ask for Your deliverance, not based on my own merits or anything I have accomplished, but solely on Your perfect righteousness. Deliver me from the weight of unfulfilled tasks, from the sting of harsh words, and from the quiet anxieties that cling to my spirit before sleep. Rescue my thoughts from circles of worry and my heart from any lingering despair. Let Your holy character be the shield against all that assails me, ensuring my rest is deep and undisturbed, knowing I am safe in Your just and loving hands through the night.

## Scene 3 — Into Thine Hand I Commit My Spirit (Verse 5a)

This is the ultimate bedtime prayer: into thine hand I commit my spirit.

You are not holding yourself together tonight. God is holding you.

Your spirit, your soul, your breath — all committed into His faithful keeping.

Sleep knowing that the One who holds the universe is holding you.
Loving Father, with intentional surrender, I commit my very spirit into Your omnipotent hand tonight. Every breath I take, every thought that forms, every emotion that stirs within me — I place them all under Your divine guardianship. Guard my soul from disturbance, my mind from restless thoughts, and my body from discomfort. Watch over me as I sleep, protecting me from all harm and ill. Let my surrender be complete, trusting that You will keep my spirit safe, renew my strength, and grant me the profound peace that only comes from being entirely within Your faithful keeping.

## Scene 4 — Thou Hast Redeemed Me, O LORD God of Truth (Verse 5b)

You are not your own. You have been bought with a price, redeemed by the God of truth.

Thou hast redeemed me, O LORD God of truth.

Truth is the foundation of everything God says and does. He cannot lie, He cannot fail, He cannot abandon.

Sleep in the certainty of His redeeming truth.
O LORD God of Truth, I cling to the profound reality that You have redeemed me. In Your unfailing truth, I find my security and my identity, knowing I am no longer defined by my past errors or the world's shifting standards. Dispel any lies that might try to creep into my mind as I drift to sleep, reminding me of the precious price paid for my freedom. Let the unwavering certainty of my redemption calm every fear and quiet every doubt, allowing me to rest assured in the absolute truth of Your saving grace and Your eternal commitment to me.

## Scene 5 — Sleep Committed to God (Verses 1, 5 Synthesis)

From trust, to deliverance, to commitment, to redemption — every clause is an act of surrender.

Tonight, commit your spirit into His hand. Let Him keep what you cannot keep yourself.
Gracious God, as I prepare to close my eyes, I reaffirm this complete act of surrender: my trust is in You alone, my deliverance is through Your righteousness, my spirit is committed into Your hands, and my redemption is by Your unfailing truth. I release all control, all striving, and all pretense, resting solely in Your sovereignty over this night and my life. May my sleep be a testament to the peace found in perfect surrender, a deep repose granted by the knowledge that I am utterly kept, watched over, and loved by You, my faithful Keeper.

You are redeemed. You are delivered. You are safe.

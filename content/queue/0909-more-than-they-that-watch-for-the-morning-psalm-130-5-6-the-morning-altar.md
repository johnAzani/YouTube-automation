## Scene 1 — The Sunrise Hook & Directing the Prayer

The room is still, and the first pale line of light is beginning to show at the edge of the window while the house is quiet before the day begins to stir. In these early moments, before the first message arrives or the mind rushes ahead to the tasks waiting on the desk, there is a choice about where to place the weight of your attention. This is the hour to direct your prayer toward heaven and look up, bringing the quiet expectation of a new day into the presence of the Lord. Psalm 130 opens with the cry of a soul looking out from the depths, turning away from every earthly distraction to find its anchor in God alone. As the morning breaks, we turn our hearts to His unchanging word and the quiet assurance of His presence. I wait for the LORD, my soul doth wait, and in his word do I hope. These are not hasty words spoken in passing, but the settled posture of a heart that has learned to lean entirely upon the character of God. Lord God, as this morning dawns, we quiet our spirits before Thee. We acknowledge that our help comes not from our own planning, but from Thy steadfast hand. Thou hast brought us safely through the watches of the night, and now we open our hands to receive the hours of this new day. Direct our steps, steady our thoughts, and keep our eyes fixed upon Thee when the noise of the world begins to rise.

## Scene 2 — New Mercies & Faithfulness at Dawn

Morning arrives with a quiet mercy that asks nothing of yesterday's failures, offering instead a clean page and the fresh breath of life. Charles Spurgeon, writing on these Psalms of ascent, notes that the waiting soul is never forgotten by the God who hears the cry from the depths. The night may have felt long, and the questions that lingered in the dark may still be resting in the corners of the mind, but the faithfulness of God does not slumber. His mercies are new every morning, matching the exact measure of our need before we even have words to ask. We rest today not on our own strength, but on the enduring promise of His word. Father, we thank Thee that Thy faithfulness is wider than our fears and deeper than our uncertainty. When our souls are restless and inclined to look everywhere for relief except to Thee, draw us back by Thy grace. Teach us what it means to wait patiently, knowing that Thou art never late in Thy coming. Grant us this morning a deep sense of Thy peace, washing away the residue of yesterday and filling our hearts with the quiet confidence of Thy love. Let Thy word be the lamp that lights our path through the hours ahead, steadying our feet when the road is uneven and keeping our minds anchored in Thy truth.

## Scene 3 — Trust & Guidance for the Decisions Ahead

The hours ahead hold decisions, conversations, and tasks that we cannot fully see or control from where we stand right now. It is easy to lean upon our own understanding, to calculate every outcome and try to secure our own path before the sun is even fully up. Yet the posture of the waiting soul is one of surrender, relinquishing the need to manage everything in our own strength. In his commentary on this psalm, Matthew Henry reminds us that true hope is placed not in outward circumstances, but solely in the promise and power of God. When we wait for the Lord, our steps are ordered by His wisdom rather than our anxiety. Lord, we commit every meeting, every responsibility, and every quiet moment of this day into Thy hands. We do not know what the afternoon may bring, but we know Who goes before us. Give us wisdom when choices must be made, patience when progress is slow, and grace in every interaction with the people around us. Keep us from striving in our own strength. Remind us that our work is done under Thine eye and for Thy glory. When the pressure of the day begins to press in upon us, let the memory of this morning stillness return to calm our spirits and direct our paths.

## Scene 4 — Favour, Shield & Protection for the Day

The day brings its own friction, weariness, and unexpected demands, requiring a shield greater than our own resolve. To wait upon the Lord is to find refuge under the shadow of His wings, where no trouble can ultimately separate us from His care. His favour surrounds the righteous as a shield, going before us into every room we enter and standing behind us to guard our steps. We do not walk out into this morning unprotected or forgotten. Heavenly Father, we ask for Thy divine protection over our lives, our families, and our work today. Shield our minds from discouragement and our hearts from bitterness when things do not unfold as we planned. Let Thy presence be a tangible reality to us, calming the sudden storms of worry and replacing them with the quiet strength that comes from knowing Thou art God. Grant us favour in our labor and peace within our homes. Where there is weariness, pour out renewal. Where there is confusion, grant clarity. May the light of Thy countenance shine upon us as we move through the day, making our service acceptable and our witness clear to those who cross our path.

## Scene 5 — Blessing Over Work, Family & Home

Every morning is a gift given not just for ourselves, but to be shared in the ordinary places where we live and serve. The work of our hands, the conversations at the breakfast table, the quiet hours spent in diligence — all of these are brought before the altar to be sanctified by His blessing. As we wait for the Lord more eagerly than watchmen wait for the morning light, our homes and our daily labors are held in His keeping. Lord, we speak a blessing over our families, our workplaces, and our communities this morning. Keep those we love under the shadow of Thy wings, whether they are near to us or far away. Establish the work of our hands and make our daily efforts fruitful for Thy kingdom. Help us to carry the peace of this morning hour into the rush of the afternoon, extending grace, patience, and kindness to everyone we meet. May our homes be places of rest and refuges of Thy love. We trust Thee with our health, our provisions, and our future, knowing that Thou dost care for us with an everlasting affection that never fails.

## Scene 6 — The Sending Benediction

The morning light is now fully risen, and the day calls us forth from this quiet place of prayer into the world. We do not go alone, nor do we go in our own strength. Our souls have waited for the Lord, and in His word we have found our hope, enduring and sure as the dawn. My soul waiteth for the Lord more than they that watch for the morning: I say, more than they that watch for the morning. May the Lord bless thee and keep thee. May the Lord make His face shine upon thee and be gracious unto thee. May the Lord lift up His countenance upon thee and give thee peace, this day and forevermore.

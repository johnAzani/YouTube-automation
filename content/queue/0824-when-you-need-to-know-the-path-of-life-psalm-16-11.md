## Scene 1 — Bringing the Decision to the Lord

You might be standing at a fork in the road tonight, looking down two different paths and feeling entirely unable to see where either one leads. Perhaps a choice has been placed before you that you never asked to make, or a door has closed, leaving you wondering which way you are supposed to turn now. When the landscape of your life feels uncertain and every option carries its own weight of consequence, it is easy to let anxiety crowd out the quiet assurance of God's presence. You do not need to have all the answers tonight. You do not need to force a conclusion before you are ready. Instead, you can bring this exact confusion to the One who sees the end from the beginning. In Proverbs 3, we are reminded to trust in the LORD with all our hearts and lean not unto our own understanding. That instruction meets you right in this room, right in the middle of your hesitation. Let us bring this moment before Him in prayer, asking simply for the grace to yield our own calculations and let Him direct our steps.

Lord God, Maker of heaven and earth, You know the exact dilemma that rests upon my mind tonight. You see the questions I have not spoken aloud to anyone, and You know the weight of the decision that lies ahead of me. Forgive me, Lord, for the times I rely entirely on my own reasoning, spinning every possible outcome in my head until I am exhausted. Tonight, I choose to let go of that frantic need to figure everything out by myself. I bring my uncertainty and lay it before Your throne. I ask You to quiet the noise of my own fears and anchor my heart in Your unchanging truth. When my vision is limited and the road ahead is obscured by mist, grant me the courage to trust Your wisdom above my own understanding. Teach me to acknowledge You in every detail of this choice, knowing that You are able to make my paths straight. In Jesus name, amen.

## Scene 2 — Asking God for Wisdom

When we face crossroads that require genuine discernment, the apostle James tells us that if any of us lack wisdom, we should ask of God, who gives to all men liberally and upbraideth not. That promise is extraordinarily gentle. It means that God does not scold you for not knowing what to do. He does not grow impatient with your hesitation or your repeated questions. Matthew Henry, in his commentary on the Scriptures, notes that divine wisdom is readily supplied to those who come with a simple desire to know and do the will of God, rather than merely seeking a confirmation of their own pre-arranged plans. Asking for wisdom is an act of humility. It acknowledges that your own insight has reached its limit and that you are entirely dependent on the counsel of the Almighty. You do not have to muster up artificial certainty. You can come to Him empty-handed, asking for the light that only He can provide for the next step of the journey.

Father of lights, You have promised that if I lack wisdom, I may ask of You and receive it without reproach. Lord, I am asking. I confess that I do not possess the foresight needed for the days and months ahead. I cannot see what fruit will grow from the choices I make right now. But I know that Your wisdom is pure, peaceable, and full of mercy. Grant me a discerning heart that can distinguish between my own desires and Your holy direction. Remove any stubbornness from my spirit that wants my own way above Yours. If there are motives within me that are clouded by fear or ambition, expose them gently by Your Spirit and realign my heart with Your kingdom. I receive Your generous promise of wisdom tonight, trusting that You will supply precisely what I need when I need it. In Jesus name, amen.

## Scene 3 — The Path of Life Revealed

In the sixteenth psalm, David declares a profound truth that cuts through all our temporal anxieties and directional confusion. He looks past the immediate struggles of his day and anchors his soul in the certainty of God's leading. The King James Version preserves his words with timeless clarity in Psalm 16:11: Thou wilt shew me the path of life: in thy presence is fulness of joy; at thy right hand there are pleasures for evermore. Notice that David does not say he has mapped out the journey himself, nor does he claim to see every mile of the road ahead. Instead, his confidence rests entirely on the character of God. Thou wilt shew me. It is an act of divine revelation. God is the One who illuminates the path, step by step, unfolding His will as we walk in fellowship with Him. When your path feels narrow or blocked, remember that the Lord delights in guiding His children. He is not hiding His will from you; He is inviting you to walk closely enough with Him to see the lamp when it shines on your feet.

Almighty God, Your word tells me that You are the One who shows the path of life. When I look at my circumstances and feel lost, I lean heavily upon that promise tonight. You are the faithful Shepherd who leads His sheep beside still waters and restores their souls. Show me the way I should walk, Lord. Do not let me wander off into paths of my own making, but guide me gently by Your counsel. Where the road divides and I am afraid of making a misstep, let Your voice be heard behind me, saying, This is the way, walk ye in it. I do not ask for a map of the entire future; I only ask for the grace to see and obey the next instruction You give. Fill my heart with the quiet confidence that comes from knowing my times are in Your hands. In Jesus name, amen.

## Scene 4 — Wise Counsel and Tested Motives

Seeking God's direction in a decision rarely happens in complete isolation. The scriptures remind us in the book of Proverbs that in the multitude of counsellors there is safety, and that plans are established when we seek advice. When you are trying to discern the path of life, the Lord often speaks through the stable, tested wisdom of godly men and women who love Him and know His word. At the same time, we must examine our own motives before the searchlight of God's holiness. Psalm 139 asks God to search us and know our hearts, to try us and know our thoughts, and see if there be any wicked way in us. True spiritual discernment requires us to lay down our stubborn preferences and ask God to purify our intentions. If you are seeking guidance simply to escape discomfort or to secure your own ease, the Lord may use this very season of waiting to refine your character, teaching you that His fellowship is far more valuable than a frictionless life.

Lord, search my heart tonight and test my thoughts. If I am clinging to an outcome simply because it flatters my pride or spares me from difficulty, please loosen my grip upon it. Give me the humility to listen to the wise counsel You place around me, and grant me discernment to weigh advice against the truth of Your Holy Scripture. Protect me from the deceitfulness of my own desires. I know that true spiritual guidance is never at odds with Your character or Your word. Keep my feet firmly planted in paths of righteousness for Your name's sake. When my own heart is divided, unite my heart to fear Your name and follow Your leading without reservation. In Jesus name, amen.

## Scene 5 — Waiting With Patience and Ordered Steps

There are seasons when the hardest part of seeking guidance is simply waiting for the fog to lift. We want immediate answers, rapid confirmation, and a clear view of the destination. Yet the scriptures constantly remind us that the steps of a good man are ordered by the LORD, and He delights in his way. Notice the scale of that promise: it is the individual step that is ordered, not the panoramic view of the entire landscape. God gives us grace for the present moment, sustaining us while we wait upon Him. If you are in a season where the answer has not yet appeared, do not mistake God's silence for abandonment. He is working quietly beneath the surface of your life, aligning circumstances, preparing your heart, and making ready the way before you. Take courage in His steadfast love. He who began a good work in you is faithful to complete it, and His timing is always perfect, even when it tests our human impatience.

Father, waiting is often the crucible where my trust is truly tested. When the days pass and the direction I prayed for remains unclear, quiet my restless spirit. Help me to rest in the truth that my steps are ordered by You. When I am tempted to force a door open or rush ahead of Your timing, grant me the patience to stand still and see Your salvation. Strengthen my hands when they grow weary and my knees when they feel weak. Remind me that even in the waiting, I am walking in Your presence, and nothing can separate me from Your love. I commit the timing of this decision entirely into Your hands, knowing that You make all things beautiful in its time. In Jesus name, amen.

## Scene 6 — Committing the Way and Closing Benediction

We return now to the quiet assurance of the scripture that started this journey. The psalmist found that true contentment is not found in the absence of trials, but in the overwhelming reality of God's presence. In thy presence is fulness of joy; at thy right hand there are pleasures for evermore. Whatever decision you face tomorrow, whatever uncertainty greets you when the sun rises, you do not walk into it alone. The Lord who shows you the path of life is the same God who fills your heart with a joy that circumstances cannot touch. Commit your way unto Him, trust also in Him, and He shall bring it to pass. As you close this time of prayer, let every burden of self-reliance slip from your shoulders. Rest in His care, knowing that He has gone before you into the very hours you are dreading, and He is holding you securely in the palm of His hand.

Lord God of all grace, I commit my way, my work, and my future completely to You tonight. Thank You for meeting me in this place of quiet prayer, and for reminding me that Your presence is my true home. As I rest my mind and heart in You, pour out Your peace that passes all understanding. Guide my thoughts, guard my steps, and let Your unfailing love sustain me through every upcoming choice. May my life bring honor to Your name as I walk in the light of Your countenance.

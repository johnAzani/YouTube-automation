## Scene 1 — Bringing the Decision to the Lord

You might be standing right now at a fork in the road, looking at options that all look heavy, wondering if you are about to make a misstep that will cost you months of peace. When a choice is too big for your own understanding, the quietest room can feel very lonely. We often lean so hard on our own reasoning, turning every angle over in our minds until exhaustion sets in and clarity slips further away. But the ancient wisdom of Scripture reminds us that we were never meant to carry the weight of our decisions in isolation. Proverbs chapter eleven, verse fourteen declares: Where no counsel is, the people fall: but in the multitude of counselors there is safety. Lord, You see the uncertainty that has settled over this mind today. You know the exact decision that needs to be made, and You know how easily we stumble when we rely entirely on our own strength and perspective. Father, grant us the humility to pause, to breathe, and to acknowledge that we do not have all the answers. Teach us to bring our questions out of the dark corners of our worry and lay them openly before Your throne.

## Scene 2 — Asking God for Wisdom

As Matthew Henry notes in his commentary on the ways of wisdom, those who walk humbly with God are never left entirely without a guide, for He delights to direct the steps of the teachable. We often ask God for a lightning flash of direction, wanting the entire road illuminated from start to finish so we never have to feel the discomfort of not knowing. Yet Scripture consistently points us toward a quieter, more deliberate way of learning. James reminds us that if any of us lacks wisdom, we are invited to ask of God, who gives to all men liberally and upbraideth not. Lord, we ask You for that generous wisdom right now. Not a wisdom built on pride or the desire for immediate comfort, but a spiritual discernment that can separate what is merely urgent from what is truly good. Guard our hearts against hasty commitments and impulsive reactions. When our emotions urge us to rush ahead, grant us the inward quietness to wait upon Your voice and to test every impulse against the steady truth of Your Word.

## Scene 3 — The Lamp of the Word

Your Word is described in the Psalms as a lamp unto our feet and a light unto our path, casting just enough illumination for the step we are taking this very moment. We do not need to see the final destination to take a faithful step forward; we only need to trust the One who holds the lamp. Lord, steady our steps in Your truth when the noise of conflicting opinions threatens to drown out Your promptings. Help us to value the quiet consistency of Scripture above the shifting opinions of the world. When we are tempted to force a door open simply because we are tired of waiting, remind us of Your promise to guide the meek in judgment and to teach the humble Your way. Let Your Word be the final court of appeal in every decision we face, cutting through our rationalizations and pointing us toward the narrow path of righteousness and peace.

## Scene 4 — Wise Counsel and Tested Motives

The search for direction is not meant to be a solitary endeavor. Proverbs reminds us that in the multitude of counsellors there is safety, pointing us toward the wise, seasoned voices that God places in our lives to steady our footing. Yet even as we seek the advice of others, we recognize that human counsel must always be weighed against the unchanging standard of Your truth, and our own motives must be laid bare before You. Search our hearts, Lord, and reveal any hidden desires, stubborn pride, or secret fears that might be clouding our judgment. We acknowledge that prayer walks hand in hand with wise counsel, and we thank You for the godly men and women You have given us who speak truth in love. Protect us from isolation, and give us the discernment to recognize true, biblical wisdom when we hear it, even when it challenges what we wanted to do.

## Scene 5 — Waiting With Patience and Ordered Steps

Waiting is often the hardest part of discernment. When time feels short and pressure mounts from every side, the temptation to force an outcome grows strong. Yet Scripture assures us that the steps of a good man are ordered by the Lord, and He delighteth in his way. Lord, grant us the grace to wait when waiting is required. Keep us from panicking into a bad decision or leaning on the opinions of those who do not fear Your name. When we feel the anxiety of an unresolved choice pressing down on our spirits, wrap us in Your peace that passes understanding. Remind us that You are not hurried by our deadlines, and Your wisdom never fails. Even if we have stumbled in the past or made choices out of our own haste, Your mercy is wide enough to catch us and set our feet back upon solid ground.

## Scene 6 — Committing the Way and Closing Benediction

Father, we commit this entire situation, with all its uncertain details and unanswered questions, entirely into Your hands. We release our grip on the outcome and ask that Your will be done in our lives, above our own preferences and our own fears. May Your presence go with us into the days ahead, guiding our thoughts, guarding our relationships, and directing our labor. We rest in the assurance that You are faithful to lead those who trust in You.

## Scene 1 — Gathering Around the Family Altar

There are quiet evenings when you look across the room at the people living under your roof — your spouse, your children, those whose names are woven into your daily life — and a sudden, heavy weight settles over your chest. You see the pressures of the world pressing in against them, the subtle ways hearts can drift, the slippery paths where even those with good intentions can stumble. You cannot watch every step they take, nor can you guard every door they pass through. That realization is precisely where the family altar begins. It is the moment we stop leaning on our own vigilance and turn toward the only One whose hand is mighty enough to hold a household steady. 

As it is written in the epistle of Jude, verse twenty-four: Now unto him that is able to keep you from falling, and to present you faultless before the presence of his glory with exceeding joy. 

Let us bring our household before Him now in prayer.

Heavenly Father, we gather our home in Your presence this hour, acknowledging that except You keep this house, our labor to guard it is entirely in vain. We confess that our own strength is small, our wisdom is limited, and our eyes cannot see the dangers that lie ahead on tomorrow's road. But You see all things. You know the paths our children will walk, the decisions our spouses will face, and the quiet temptations that try to enter our dwelling. We place every member of this family into Your keeping. Where our grip is weak, let Your mighty hand take hold. Where our vigilance fails, let Your unwearied eye watch over us. Teach us to build this home upon Your truth, so that when the winds blow and the rains descend against our walls, our foundation remains unshaken, rooted not in our own goodness, but in Your sovereign and preserving grace.

## Scene 2 — Building the House Upon God's Wisdom

A house is made of walls and a roof, but a home is built of patience, forbearance, and daily obedience to God's voice. When misunderstandings arise over the kitchen table, or when the cares of the day leave everyone tired and short of temper, we need more than good intentions to keep the peace. We need the wisdom that comes down from above, governing our speech and softening our pride. Matthew Henry reminds us in his commentary on the household of faith that those who set up God's worship in their homes erect a sanctuary where His blessing perpetually rests, turning ordinary rooms into a place of divine instruction.

Lord God, grant us the grace to build our daily life upon Your unchanging word. When friction enters our home and words are spoken in haste, pour out Your spirit of gentleness upon us. Help husbands and wives to love one another with a sincere and patient affection, laying down self-will at the threshold. Give us wisdom in how we guide our household, knowing that every conversation, every correction, and every shared meal leaves an impression upon the hearts entrusted to our care. Remove bitterness, impatience, and the pride that divides us. Make our dwelling a quiet resting place where Your word is honored, where forgiveness is freely given, and where every member of this family learns to seek Your face before their own desires.

## Scene 3 — Covering Children, Sons, Daughters & Generations

There is a unique burden that rests upon the heart of a parent or a guardian — the persistent prayer that those we love will not only grow in stature, but will walk faithfully in the truth of the Gospel. We watch them step out into a world that offers many voices, many distractions, and many paths that lead away from peace. We cannot choose their steps for them, but we can commit their souls to the God who is able to keep them from falling. As olive plants round about our table, we pray they will draw deep from the living water and stand firm against the storms of their generation.

O Lord, we lift up our children, our youth, and all the younger generations connected to this household into Your merciful hands. You know their names, their struggles, their quiet fears, and the particular temptations that surround them each day. Guard their minds from falsehood and protect their feet from the paths of destruction. When they are away from our sight and beyond our reach, let Your Holy Spirit be their counselor, whispering truth to their consciences and drawing them close to Your side. May they never wander so far that Your grace cannot find them, and may the faith we imperfectly model before them become their own deep conviction. Keep them pure in heart, humble in spirit, and resolute in their devotion to Christ.

## Scene 4 — Unity, Peace & Grace Within Marriage and Relationships

A family cannot stand when its inner fellowship is divided. The daily grind of provision, labor, and responsibility can wear away at the tenderness between a husband and a wife, leaving room for strain and quiet isolation. Yet Scripture promises that God makes men to dwell in safety and grants quiet resting places to those who trust in Him. True domestic peace is not the absence of trouble, but the presence of Christ ruling in the midst of our human frailties, binding us together with cords of love that cannot be easily broken.

Father, we pray for the marriages and the vital relationships within this home. Where weariness has crept in, renew our strength. Where warmth has grown faint, kindle a fresh flame of grace and understanding. Help us to bear one another's burdens with patience, overlooking faults with the same boundless mercy You have shown toward us. Let no harsh word take root in our hearts, and let no day end with unaddressed anger sleeping under our roof. We ask for a deep, abiding harmony that transcends circumstance — a unity that testifies to anyone who enters our home that Your peace guards our door.

## Scene 5 — Honest Provision, Health & Protection Over Every Threshold

We do not ask for earthly excess or luxuries that draw our hearts away from eternity, but we do look to You, Jehovah-Jireh, for our daily bread and the honest provision of our needs. The cost of living, the uncertainty of tomorrow, and the labor of our hands can sometimes breed anxiety in our thoughts. Yet You are the God who fed Your people in the wilderness and numbered the very hairs of our heads. We also ask for Your protective hedge around our property, our coming and our going, and our physical health, that we may serve You with strength and gladness of heart.

Lord, we commit the labor of our hands and the daily provision of this household to Your care. Bless the work we do, grant us diligence in our callings, and supply every genuine need according to Your riches in glory by Christ Jesus. Guard our threshold from harm, sickness, and fear. As we sleep and as we wake, let Your angels encamp around this dwelling. Keep our bodies healthy and our minds sound, that we may use the days You have allotted to us to do good, to love our neighbors, and to honor Your holy name. Remove all anxious dread from our pillows, replacing worry with the quiet assurance that our times are safely in Your hands.

## Scene 6 — Aaronic Household Blessing & Covenant Benediction

As we come to the close of our gathering around the family altar today, we return to the great and immovable foundation with which we began. Our hope for tomorrow does not rest on our own steadfastness, but upon His infinite ability to keep us from stumbling and to bring us home at last. 

Let us hear the conclusion of Jude's doxology, from verse twenty-four and twenty-five, exactly as it is written: Now unto him that is able to keep you from falling, and to present you faultless before the presence of his glory with exceeding joy, To the only wise God our Savior, be glory and majesty, dominion and power, both now and ever. Amen.

The LORD bless you, and keep you. The LORD make His face shine upon you, and be gracious unto you. The LORD lift up His countenance upon you, and give you peace, this day and forevermore.

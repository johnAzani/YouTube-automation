## Scene 1 — Approaching the Prepared Table at Dawn

When you look at the calendar and wonder how the work of your hands will meet the demands of the week, it is easy for the heart to grow heavy with anxious calculation. You may be sitting at a kitchen table before the house wakes up, staring at figures that do not quite balance, or thinking of an upcoming project that requires wisdom you feel you lack. The Lord who prepares a table before us does not ask us to carry tomorrow's burdens on today's strength. He invites us to come as we are, acknowledging our total dependence upon His faithful hand for the very breath in our lungs and the work before us. In the Sermon on the Mount, the Lord Jesus teaches His disciples to bring their most basic, fundamental needs directly to the Father in simple, unpretentious trust. Let us hear His instruction as recorded in Matthew chapter 6, verse 11: Give us this day our daily bread. Father, we come before Thee this morning acknowledging that every good and perfect gift comes down from above. We confess that without Thy sustaining grace, our labour is merely toil. Thou art Jehovah-Jireh, the God who sees ahead and provides for every creature He has made. We lay down our self-reliance and the quiet panic that whispers we must secure our own future through worry. Grant us the grace to receive what Thou hast appointed for us this day, neither grasping for an excess nor fearing lack, but resting securely in Thy boundless care.

## Scene 2 — Open Doors and Heaven's Good Treasure

Honest labor is an act of worship when it is offered unto the Lord with integrity and diligence. Yet how often do we find ourselves striving against closed doors, wondering if our daily efforts in our vocation or business are seen by the One who rules the heavens? Matthew Henry reminds us in his commentary that our daily bread is obtained through lawful industry, yet it is God who gives the increase and crowns our honest toil with His blessing. We do not ask for effortless riches or speculative gains, but for the open doors of opportunity where our skills and trade can serve others and sustain our households with honour. Lord God of heaven, we pray for Thy favour upon the work of our hands today. Open doors of honest employment, fair trade, and fruitful enterprise that no man can shut. Where we have laboured with integrity through difficult seasons, let Thy blessing rest upon our tasks. Protect our workplaces from discord, grant wisdom to those in authority over us, and give us diligence in every assignment. Remind us that our worth does not rest in the ledger of our bank account, but in our standing as children of the Most High God, who feeds the birds of the air and clothes the grass of the field.

## Scene 3 — Divine Wisdom and Direction in Decisions

Every day brings its own crossroads: a decision to be made about finances, a choice regarding a contract or career path, or a subtle temptation to take shortcuts that compromise our integrity. In such moments, human cleverness is a poor guide, and anxious planning only leads us into deeper confusion. We need the wisdom that comes from above — wisdom that is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, and easy to be entreated, full of mercy and good fruits. Heavenly Father, Thou hast promised that if any lack wisdom, they may ask of Thee, who giveth to all men liberally and upbraideth not. We ask for that heavenly discernment today. When we stand before difficult choices regarding our livelihood, our debts, or our family's provision, order our steps according to Thy word. Keep us from hasty decisions born of fear or pride. Give us the patience to wait upon Thy timing and the courage to walk through the doors Thou hast opened, even when the path requires steadfast endurance. Let Thy Holy Spirit guide our thoughts and conversation so that everything we touch honours Thy holy name.

## Scene 4 — Freedom from Anxious Toil and Contentment

The human heart is easily ensnared by the belief that life consists in the abundance of things possessed, leading to a constant, grinding anxiety about tomorrow. We worry about inflation, about unexpected expenses, about the stability of our employment, and about whether our provisions will hold out. But our Saviour gently rebukes this anxious spirit, pointing us away from the deceptive security of hoarded wealth and toward the living God who clothes the lilies and feeds the sparrow. Lord, deliver us from the heavy burden of tomorrow's worries. Thou hast known our frame and remembered that we are dust. When fear knocks at the door of our minds, help us to anchor our souls in Thy faithfulness. Teach us the quiet art of Christian contentment — to be satisfied with Thy daily provision and to trust Thee implicitly for the morrow. Forgive us for the times we have doubted Thy goodness or envied the prosperity of those who walk apart from Thee. Purify our desires, that we might seek first Thy kingdom and Thy righteousness, fully confident that all these necessary things shall be added unto us according to Thy perfect wisdom.

## Scene 5 — Favour with God and Man and Generous Stewardship

When the Lord fills our cup and provides our daily bread, He does not intend for us to hoard His blessings in selfish isolation. True biblical provision is designed to flow through us as faithful stewards, making us channels of kindness, hospitality, and generosity to those around us. Favour with God often brings favour with our neighbours, colleagues, and community, opening avenues where we can share the very comfort we have received from His hand. Father, make us faithful stewards of all that Thou hast entrusted to our care. Whether our portion today is abundant or lean, grant us the grace to manage it with wisdom, honesty, and a generous spirit. If Thou hast given us surplus, keep our hands open to help the needy, support the work of the gospel, and encourage those who struggle through harder seasons. Let our lives bear witness to the reality of Thy sustaining grace. May our workplaces and homes be places of peace, where integrity speaks louder than ambition and where kindness reflects the heart of our Redeemer.

## Scene 6 — Overflowing Cup and Covenant Provision Benediction

As this day unfolds with its various duties, interruptions, and quiet moments, we return our gaze to the Shepherd who never slumbers nor sleeps. He who taught us to pray for our daily bread is faithful to fulfill every promise He has made concerning our sustenance and peace. Let His presence go with you into your workday, your home, and your quiet hours, guarding your heart from fear and filling your mouth with praise. May the Lord bless thee and keep thee. May the Lord make His face shine upon thee and be gracious unto thee. May the Lord lift up His countenance upon thee, and give thee peace in thy going out and thy coming in, today and forevermore.

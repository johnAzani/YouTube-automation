## Scene 1 — Bringing the Decision to the Lord

When a fork in the road appears before you, and you stand in the quiet room of your own thoughts, wondering which way to turn, the weight of the moment can press heavily upon the spirit. Perhaps you have weighed the options until your mind is weary, turning every possibility over in your hands, yet finding no clear path forward. It is in precisely this hour of hesitation that we are invited to bring our uncertainty directly to the Creator of our ways. Proverbs three reminds us to trust in the Lord with all our heart, and lean not unto our own understanding; in all our ways acknowledge him, and he shall direct our paths. This is where true guidance begins — not in the frantic gathering of more human advice, but in the quiet surrender of our own limited perspective to the One who sees the entire landscape from beginning to end. Father, we come before You today acknowledging that we do not know what the morrow brings, nor do we always possess the discernment needed for the choices in front of us. We lay our questions, our timelines, and our anxieties at Your feet. We confess that we have often tried to reason our way out of confusion using only our own strength, only to find ourselves walking in circles. Grant us the grace to pause, to breathe, and to look away from our own anxious thoughts toward Your steadfast throne. As we search for direction, steady our hearts against the fear of making a misstep. Remind us that Your mercies are new every morning, and that You are near to all who call upon You in truth. We do not ask for a map of the entire distant future; we simply ask for the light to see the very next faithful step, trusting that Your hand will hold us steady as we walk.

## Scene 2 — Asking God for Wisdom

The human heart is often divided against itself, pulled by desires, fears, and the loud expectations of the world around us. In such times, we are reminded of the promise found in the epistle of James, that if any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not; and it shall be given him. Matthew Henry, in his commentary on the Scriptures, notes that divine wisdom is not a commodity earned by our own intellectual sharpness, but a free gift from a generous Father who delights to guide those who acknowledge their need of Him. The wisdom that comes from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, and easy to be entreated. Lord, we acknowledge this day that we lack the wisdom required for the specific decisions currently resting upon us. We do not come boasting of our insight, but rather confessing our frequent blindness. We ask You to grant us that heavenly discernment which penetrates beneath the surface of things. Where motives are tangled and deceptive, grant us the clarity of Your truth. When fear whispers in one ear and ambition in the other, let Your still, small voice be the loudest sound in our souls. We take comfort in knowing that You do not scold us for our ignorance, but instead give liberally to those who ask in sincerity. Pour out Your wisdom into our receptive hearts, aligning our desires with Your holy will, so that our choices might honor Your name and bring peace to our spirits.

## Scene 3 — The Lamp of the Word

In moments of decision, the temptation is always to look for sudden, dramatic signs or a voice booming from the clouds to settle our doubts. Yet Scripture teaches us a more enduring and steady way. Psalm one hundred nineteen tells us that thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path. The Word of God does not typically illuminate ten miles down a dark road; it sheds a clear, bright light upon the immediate step beneath our feet, enough to keep us from stumbling. Isaiah the prophet reminds us that our ears shall hear a word behind us, saying, This is the way, walk ye in it, when we turn to the right hand and when we turn to the left. Lord, we thank You for the unchanging anchor of Your Holy Word. When human counsel fails and our own thoughts grow dim, we turn to the pages of Scripture where Your enduring truths are written down for our instruction. For the LORD giveth wisdom: out of his mouth cometh knowledge and understanding. Let those sacred words sink deep into the soil of our minds today. Let Your truth sift through our confusion, separating what is urgent from what is truly important. When we are tempted to rush ahead of Your timing, remind us to wait upon the lamp of Your instruction. We refuse to lean upon our own understanding; instead, we anchor our souls to the rock of Your revealed counsel, confident that Your Word is sufficient to guide our steps through every shadowed valley and unfamiliar crossing.

## Scene 4 — Wise Counsel and Tested Motives

Seeking God's direction does not happen in isolation; it is designed to be pursued within the fellowship of godly wisdom and honest self-examination. The book of Proverbs reminds us that in the multitude of counsellors there is safety, and that without counsel purposes are disappointed. Furthermore, the psalmist prays, Search me, O God, and know my heart: try me, and know my thoughts: and see if there be any wicked way in me, and lead me in the way everlasting. True discernment requires us to lay our secret motives bare before the Lord, asking Him to reveal any hidden pride, impatience, or stubborn self-will that might be coloring our judgment. Father, search our hearts today. If we are pushing toward a decision simply because it is easy, or because it feeds our own comfort rather than Your purposes, check our spirits. Bring into our lives trusted, wise, and godly companions whose counsel is seasoned with prayer and faithfulness. And we remember, Lord, that prayer walks hand in hand with the practical responsibilities You have given us; it is never a substitute for the wise use of the sound mind, professional guidance, and honest diligence You expect us to exercise. Grant us the humility to listen when others warn us, and the courage to change course when Your Spirit checks our stride. Purify our intentions so that every choice we make is rooted not in fear or pride, but in a sincere desire to walk righteously before You.

## Scene 5 — Waiting With Patience and Ordered Steps

There are seasons when the hardest part of seeking guidance is simply the waiting — the quiet, stretching days when the answer has not yet arrived and the path remains obscured by morning mist. In those moments, impatience can drive us to take matters into our own hands, fashioning our own solutions out of panic rather than faith. But Psalm thirty-seven reassures us that the steps of a good man are ordered by the LORD: and he delighteth in his way. Though he fall, he shall not be utterly cast down: for the LORD upholdeth him with his hand. Lord, teach us the holy art of waiting upon You. When our souls grow restless and the pressure to decide mounts on every side, quiet our beating hearts. Remind us that waiting upon the Lord is not wasted time; it is the crucible where faith is refined and our dependence upon You is deepened. Hold us by Your hand when we feel vulnerable or prone to stumbling. If we must wait another day, another week, or another season for clarity, grant us the patience to rest in Your sovereignty. We trust that Your timing is perfect, even when our human calendars feel rushed. Strengthen our knees for the journey, and fill our quiet hours with the comforting assurance that You are already ahead of us, preparing the way and ordering every detail of our future.

## Scene 6 — Committing the Way and Closing Benediction

We have brought our questions, our need for wisdom, and our longing for clarity into Your presence, Almighty God. We have listened to Your word, examined our motives, and committed ourselves to the patient pursuit of Your will. Proverbs sixteen instructs us to commit thy works unto the LORD, and thy thoughts shall be established. And through the prophet Isaiah You have promised, And the LORD shall guide thee continually, and satisfy thy soul in drought, and make fat thy bones: and thou shalt be like a watered garden, and like a spring of water, whose waters fail not. Father, we commit our entire way into Your hands this day. We leave our decisions with You, trusting that You will grant the wisdom we have asked for in Your own perfect time and manner. Surround these listeners with Your steadfast presence as they step back into the ordinary hours of their lives. May Your peace, which passes all human understanding, guard their hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.

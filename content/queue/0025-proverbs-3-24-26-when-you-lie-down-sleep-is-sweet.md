## Scene 1 — When Thou Liest Down, Thou Shalt Not Be Afraid (Verse 24a)

Fear is the most common companion of bedtime. But God's word speaks directly to it.

When thou liest down, thou shalt not be afraid.

This is not a suggestion; it is a promise for every believer who lays down in trust.

Tonight, claim this promise. Lie down without fear.

Dear God, as I prepare to rest, I bring before You any hidden anxieties or shadows of dread that try to creep into my thoughts. You have promised that when I lie down, I shall not be afraid. I lay hold of this divine assurance, choosing to surrender every worry, every "what if," and every unsettling image to Your sovereign care. Fill my spirit with Your perfect love that casts out all fear, enabling me to recline with a tranquil heart, confident in Your unwavering presence throughout the watches of the night. May my trust in You be the softest pillow.

## Scene 2 — Yea, Thou Shalt Lie Down, and Thy Sleep Shall Be Sweet (Verse 24b)

Sweet sleep is not just rest; it is rest that restores, refreshes, and renews.

Yea, thou shalt lie down, and thy sleep shall be sweet.

Sweet sleep comes when your conscience is clear and your heart is at peace with God.

Receive sweet sleep tonight as a covenant blessing.

Heavenly Father, I long for the deep, restorative sleep that only comes from You. You declare that my sleep shall be sweet, a sacred gift when my heart is at peace with You. I confess any areas where my conscience might be troubled, and I receive Your forgiveness and cleansing, allowing me to fully embrace this covenant blessing. Grant me now a profound inner calm, silencing the clamor of the day and quieting the mind's incessant chatter. May my body, mind, and spirit be thoroughly renewed and refreshed through Your sweet rest, preparing me for Your purposes tomorrow.

## Scene 3 — Be Not Afraid of Sudden Fear (Verse 25a)

Sudden fear strikes without warning — a noise in the night, a troubling thought, a sudden anxiety.

Be not afraid of sudden fear.

God's peace is stronger than any sudden alarm. His calm is deeper than any sudden panic.

When sudden fear comes, meet it with the name of the LORD.

Lord, I acknowledge that sudden fears can ambush me even in the stillness of the night – a disturbing sound, an intrusive thought, or an unexpected wave of anxiety. But Your word commands me not to be afraid of sudden fear. I stand on Your promise that Your peace is stronger, Your calm deeper. I invoke the mighty name of Jesus over every potential sudden alarm that might seek to disturb my rest. Surround me with Your protective presence, so that no unexpected tremor of apprehension can penetrate the sanctuary of my sleep, leaving me undisturbed and secure in Your care.

## Scene 4 — Neither of the Desolation of the Wicked, When It Cometh (Verse 25b)

The destruction of the wicked may seem near, but it cannot touch you.

Neither of the desolation of the wicked, when it cometh.

You are not marked for destruction. You are marked for preservation.

Sleep secure in the knowledge that evil's plans cannot reach you.

Almighty God, I see the darkness and desolation in the world, and I hear of the troubles that seem to press in. Yet, Your promise is clear: I am not to be afraid of the desolation of the wicked, when it cometh. I declare that I am Your child, marked for preservation, not destruction. Build a hedge of protection around my home and my loved ones, guarding us from every evil scheme, every harmful influence, and every destructive force. May I sleep deeply, knowing that Your watchful eye never slumbers, and that no external threat or plan of the enemy can reach me within the shelter of Your wings.

## Scene 5 — For the LORD Shall Be Thy Confidence (Verse 26a)

Confidence is not self-assurance; it is God-assurance.

For the LORD shall be thy confidence.

Your confidence for tonight rests on His character, not your circumstances.

He is your confidence. He is your security. He is your peace.

Gracious Father, when the world demands self-reliance, Your word points me to You as my sole confidence. I confess that too often I look to my own strength, resources, or understanding. Tonight, I consciously shift my gaze from my circumstances to Your unchanging character. You are my confidence, not because of what I do, but because of who You are – faithful, powerful, and loving. Anchor my soul in Your unwavering truth, so that I may lie down with a profound sense of security that is not shaken by tomorrow’s unknowns or yesterday’s regrets. You are my firm foundation.

## Scene 6 — And Shall Keep Thy Foot from Being Taken (Verse 26b)

He keeps your foot from being taken — from being trapped, snared, or captured by evil.

And shall keep thy foot from being taken.

No trap set for you will spring. No snare will catch you. You are free.

Sleep with your feet on solid ground, guarded by God Himself.

Faithful Protector, I thank You for Your promise to keep my foot from being taken. I recognize that there are many snares and traps, both seen and unseen, that the enemy sets to ensnare my steps, to trip me up, or to hold me captive. But Your hand is upon me. I declare that no trap designed to entangle me will succeed, no hidden snare will catch me, and no spiritual or physical ploy will capture my freedom in Christ. Guide my path, secure my steps, and ensure my safety throughout this night. May I truly rest, standing on the solid ground of Your unfailing protection, free from every entanglement.

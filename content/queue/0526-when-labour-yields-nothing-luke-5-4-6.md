## Scene 1 — Approaching the Shore of Weary Labour

There are hours when the hands are tired from honest work, yet the nets come up empty, and the mind turns over the same quiet questions about provision and tomorrow. We sit by the water of our daily endeavours, where the night was long and the yield was nothing, wondering where to turn. This is where the Lord meets us, not in our abundance, but right at the shoreline of our weariness. In the fifth chapter of the Gospel of Luke, verse four, we read: Now when he had left speaking, he said unto Simon, Launch out into the deep, and let down your nets for a draught. 

Lord God of heaven and earth, Jehovah-Jireh, the God who sees our daily toil and knows the weight of our responsibilities, we come before Your table today acknowledging that our strength alone is not enough. Father, when our hands have laboured and our plans have fallen short, teach us not to lean upon our own understanding. We bring to You our vocations, our businesses, our household needs, and every honest effort we have put forth this week. Where we have grown discouraged by empty nets and delayed answers, quiet our anxious thoughts and draw us close to Your presence. You are the God who prepares a table before us in the presence of our difficulties, and Your mercies are new every morning. Grant us the grace to listen for Your voice above the noise of our own striving, and grant us a quiet, steadfast trust in Your provision as we seek Your face today.

## Scene 2 — Launching Into the Deep at His Word

When Simon Peter answered the Master, he spoke from the honesty of a tired fisherman who had worked through the dark hours without success. Yet his answer bridged the gap between human failure and divine possibility. As we read in verse five: And Simon answering said unto him, Master, we have toiled all the night, and have taken nothing: nevertheless at thy word I will let down the net. 

Father in heaven, how often our hearts echo Simon's words. We have laboured, we have planned, we have poured our energy into our vocations and our homes, and at times we look at our circumstances and see very little fruit for our labour. Forgive us, Lord, when frustration dims our faith or when we measure Your power by our past disappointments. We thank You that You do not despise our weariness, nor do You turn away from our honest confessions. Give us the courage of Peter to set aside our doubts and look past our own expertise. When You give a direction, Lord, give us the obedient heart to follow it without hesitation. Let Your Word be the final authority in our career decisions, our business dealings, and our daily stewardship. When every logical calculation tells us to stop, give us the grace to say nevertheless, at Thy word, and to move forward in humble obedience, trusting that Your timing and Your command never fail.

## Scene 3 — Divine Guidance and Open Doors of Opportunity

The obedience of casting the net where the Master commanded changed the entire outcome of the day, turning an empty boat into a vessel filled beyond expectation. The Lord directs our steps and opens doors that no man can shut when we commit our work into His hands according to His good pleasure. 

Lord of all wisdom, we ask You today to order our steps and open doors of opportunity in our work, our trades, and our careers. You are the God who gives wisdom liberally and upbraideth not. Where there are closed doors, confusion, or complex crossroads before us, grant us divine discernment. Let Your Holy Spirit guide our choices so that we walk only in the paths You have prepared for us. We pray for integrity in our labour, that every task we set our hands to would be done as unto You, reflecting honesty, diligence, and stewardship. Guard us against hasty decisions and anxious striving, and establish the work of our hands upon us, O Lord. Grant us favour with those You have placed around us in our workplaces, our businesses, and our communities, that our daily work may be a quiet testimony of Your sustaining grace.

## Scene 4 — Freedom From Anxious Toil and Contentment

To labour without anxiety is the gift of those who trust in the providence of the Heavenly Father, who feeds the birds of the air and clothes the grass of the field. We are called to be diligent in our vocations, yet released from the heavy burden of carrying tomorrow's worries on our own shoulders today. 

Merciful Father, we lay down the heavy burden of anxious toil. You have told us not to be anxious about what we shall eat or what we shall wear, for our heavenly Father knows that we have need of all these things. Teach us to seek first Your kingdom and Your righteousness, resting in the sure promise that all these things shall be added unto us. Whether we walk through a season of abundance or a season where provisions are tight, help us to be content in You, knowing that our worth and our security do not rest solely on the fluctuating results of our labour, but upon Your unchanging covenant. Give us peace of mind when the bills are due and the path ahead seems uncertain. Remind us that You are Jehovah-Jireh, the God who provides in the mount, and that Your supply is never limited by the scarcity of this world. Fill our hearts with a deep, quiet trust that frees us from fear and anchors us in Your steadfast love.

## Scene 5 — Abundant Favour and Generous Stewardship

When the nets were let down at the Master's word, the catch was so great that it filled two ships to the point of overflowing. God's provision often exceeds our expectations, not to feed our selfishness, but to make us faithful stewards and channels of blessing to others around us. 

Lord God, as You bless the work of our hands and grant us Your favour, keep our hearts humble and generous. You are able to make all grace abound toward us, that we, always having all sufficiency in all things, may abound to every good work. If You grant us an increase in our vocations, in our households, or in our businesses, let us never hoard Your gifts, but make us wise and cheerful stewards who share with those in need and support the work of Your kingdom. Deliver us from the snare of greed on one hand and the fear of poverty on the other. Let our cup runneth over with Your goodness, so that our lives overflow with gratitude, kindness, and hospitality. May our daily labour not only sustain our families, but also become a blessing to our neighbours and a praise to Your holy name.

## Scene 6 — The Overflowing Cup and Covenant Benediction

As we bring this time of prayer to a close, we look once more to the Shepherd who prepares a table before us and crowns our days with His goodness and mercy. 

Now unto Him that is able to do exceeding abundantly above all that we ask or think, according to the power that worketh in us, unto Him be glory in the church by Christ Jesus throughout all ages, world without end. Amen. May the Lord bless you and keep you. May the Lord make His face shine upon you and be gracious unto you. May the Lord lift up His countenance upon you, and give you peace in your labour, wisdom in your decisions, and unshakeable trust in His faithful provision.

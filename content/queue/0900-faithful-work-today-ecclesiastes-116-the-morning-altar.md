## Scene 1 — The Sunrise Hook & Directing the Prayer

The morning light is already touching the edges of the room before the noise of the day begins. You are sitting with a cup of coffee or standing by the window, feeling the weight of the tasks waiting for you, wondering whether the effort you put in today will bear any fruit at all. This is the exact moment to quiet the mind and turn toward the One who holds the times in His hands. In the book of Ecclesiastes, the Preacher writes of diligence that looks past uncertainty and trusts the Giver of the harvest. We come before Him now in quietness, asking Him to direct our hearts and our labor as the sun rises. Father, we bring this new morning to You, acknowledging that every breath and every hour belongs to Your sovereign grace. We do not know what the day will require of our hands, but we know You are faithful. As we begin these morning hours, teach us to cast our care upon You and to set our hands to the work You have placed before us, resting not in our own strength, but in Your steadfast mercy.

## Scene 2 — New Mercies & Faithfulness at Dawn

Every sunrise carries a quiet reminder that yesterday is gone and God's mercies have been renewed with the morning light. Matthew Henry notes in his commentary on these ancient words that human wisdom cannot chart the future, yet duty remains plain before us: we are to work while it is day, trusting the increase entirely to the Lord. We often look at our responsibilities with hesitation, measuring our small efforts against the large demands of our work, our families, and our callings. But the dawn invites us to lay down that anxious calculation. Lord God, Your compassions fail not; they are new every morning. When we are tempted to hold back our strength because we cannot see the end from the beginning, remind us of Your unchanging character. Grant us the quiet resolve to begin our tasks without fear, knowing that faithfulness in small things is the path You have marked out for Your people.

## Scene 3 — Trust & Guidance for the Decisions Ahead

The hours ahead hold decisions, conversations, and duties that we cannot fully predict. We may face a choice at our desk, a difficult discussion across the table, or a quiet moment when we simply do not know which way to turn. The scripture reminds us not to wait for absolute certainty before we move in obedience, but to sow our seed while the morning is here. O Lord, order our steps today in Your word. When we look at our work and feel the temptation to delay or doubt, grant us the grace to move forward with a quiet mind. Guide our hands in the tasks of this morning. Let every decision we make be tempered by Your wisdom and saturated with a desire to honor You. We lean not on our own understanding, but acknowledge You in all our ways, trusting that You will direct our paths.

## Scene 4 — Favour, Shield & Protection for the Day

As we step out into the work of the day, we need more than our own energy; we need the protection of His presence and the quiet assurance of His favour. The work we do in the morning is sown into soil we cannot control, yet we do not labor alone. The Lord surrounds the righteous with favour as with a shield. Heavenly Father, we ask for Your hand upon our going out and our coming in. Protect our minds from discouragement when the labor feels heavy or unseen. Guard our homes and our workplaces from the friction of impatience and self-reliance. Let Your peace, which passes all understanding, keep our hearts steady through every interruption and every unexpected turn of the day. We take refuge under the shadow of Your wings, confident that You sustain those who put their trust in You.

## Scene 5 — Blessing Over Work, Family & Home

The labor of the morning reaches far beyond our immediate desk or workshop; it touches our families, our homes, and everyone we will encounter before the sun sets. When we sow our seed in the morning, we are investing in lives and duties that God sees and values. Let us hear the wisdom of the scripture again in this quiet hour, exactly as it was written for our instruction: In the morning sow thy seed, and in the evening withhold not thine hand: for thou knowest not whether shall prosper, either this or that, or whether they both shall be alike good. Lord, we ask You to establish the work of our hands today. Bless the efforts we put forth in our daily callings, whether small or great. Pour out Your grace upon our families, our loved ones, and our neighbors. Where we plant seeds of kindness, patience, and faithfulness, let Your Spirit bring forth a harvest in due time.

## Scene 6 — The Sending Benediction

The sun is fully risen now, and the quiet moments of early morning give way to the calls and responsibilities of the day. You do not go into these hours alone; the Lord goes before you, and His mercy follows you all the days of your life. May the peace of God rest upon your mind as you step forward. May He strengthen your hands for the work before you and grant you a quiet spirit through every hour.

## Scene 1 — Gathering Around the Family Altar

When the house is quiet after the evening meal and the shoes are left by the door, there comes a moment when a parent looks across the room and wonders what will remain of these days when the children have grown and gone. We gather our families not because our walls are grand or our lives are free from care, but because we need an anchor that holds fast when the current runs strong. Joshua set up twelve stones from the dry bed of the Jordan so that the memory of God's mighty deliverance would not perish with the generation that crossed it. Lord, we gather our household tonight before Your presence. We bring the quiet rooms of this home, the table where we break bread, and the hearts of every member of our family. You are the God who brought Israel across the deep water on dry ground, and You are the same God who watches over our threshold today. Teach us to build our home upon Your enduring truth, that our children and our children's children may know that the LORD our God is with us in every season.

## Scene 2 — Building the House Upon God's Wisdom

Except the LORD build the house, they labour in vain that build it. Every family builds something every single day, whether by anxious striving or by quiet trust in Your wise providence. We confess, Father, that too often we rely on our own strength to manage the demands of the week, the provision of our daily bread, and the guidance of young lives entrusted to our care. Matthew Henry reminds us in his exposition of the covenant home that a family that calls upon God's name together forms a sanctuary under its own roof, shielded from the common storms of the world. We ask You tonight to establish the work of our hands. Grant us wisdom in our speech, patience in our corrections, and a spirit of grace that covers our many human faults. Let Your word be a lamp to our feet and a light to our path, governing the decisions we make in private and the habits we form in public, so that this house may truly honor Your holy name.

## Scene 3 — Passing Down the Works of God to Our Children

The heart of every believing parent is drawn toward the spiritual inheritance of the next generation. We read Your holy word in Joshua chapter four, verses six and seven: That this may be a sign among you, that when your children ask their fathers in time to come, saying, What mean ye by these stones? Then ye shall answer them, That the waters of Jordan were cut off before the ark of the covenant of the LORD; when it passed over Jordan, the waters of Jordan were cut off: and these stones shall be for a memorial unto the children of Israel for ever. Lord, give us the courage and the clarity to speak of Your faithfulness when our children ask about the reasons for our hope. In a world full of shifting voices and fleeting distractions, let the memory of Your great deeds remain alive within our walls. Protect the minds and steps of our sons and daughters. Guard them from the snare of unbelief, and draw their hearts early to the love of Christ, that they may walk securely in the paths of righteousness all their days.

## Scene 4 — Unity, Peace and Grace Within Marriage and Relationships

A house divided against itself cannot stand, and yet how easily the pressures of daily life can strain the tenderest bonds between husband and wife, parents and children. We pray for the peace of Jerusalem, and we pray tonight for the peace of this specific dwelling. Where there has been hasty speech, unspoken resentment, or weariness of spirit, pour out Your healing balm. Grant us the grace to forgive as we have been forgiven, putting on charity which is the bond of perfectness. Let no bitter root take hold in our family discussions. When fatigue makes us short-tempered, remind us of the covenant we share in You. Knit our hearts together in genuine affection and mutual respect, so that anyone who crosses our threshold may sense the quiet operation of Your Holy Spirit bringing harmony out of our weakness.

## Scene 5 — Honest Provision, Health and Protection Over Every Threshold

We look to You, O Lord, as our provider and our fortress against every hidden danger. You know the exact needs resting upon this household tonight — the bills that must be met, the health concerns that whisper in the dark, and the honest labor required of our hands tomorrow morning. We do not ask for lavish excess or worldly pride, but for daily bread, sufficient strength, and the quiet assurance of Your sovereign care. Set a hedge of protection around our property, our going out, and our coming in. Let Your holy angels encamp around this home to keep it from harm. As the Israelites looked upon the memorial stones by the riverbank and remembered Your faithfulness in times of scarcity and trial, so do we look back upon Your past mercies in our own lives, resting secure in the knowledge that You never fail those who trust in Your name.

## Scene 6 — Aaronic Household Blessing and Covenant Benediction

May the blessing of the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob rest upon this home now and forevermore. The LORD bless thee, and keep thee: The LORD make his face shine upon thee, and be gracious unto thee: The LORD lift up his countenance upon thee, and give thee peace. We seal our family into Your gracious hands tonight, knowing that You neither slumber nor sleep.

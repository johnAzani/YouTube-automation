## Scene 1 — If Any of You Lack Wisdom

Grace and peace to you, and welcome to The Guided Path. There are days when the honest prayer is simply this: Lord, I do not know what to do. If that is where you are, Scripture has a direct word for you. Hear the King James Scripture in James chapter one, verse five: If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not; and it shall be given him.

James was writing to believers under pressure, people facing trials they had not chosen. He does not tell them to work it out on their own. He tells them to ask. Father, we come to You as people who lack wisdom. We do not see clearly. We bring You the question that has been turning in our minds, and we ask You for what we do not have.

## Scene 2 — A God Who Gives Liberally

Look at how James describes the One we are asking. He giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not. Liberally means generously, with an open hand. And upbraideth not means He does not scold us for asking. He does not say, you should have known this already, or, why are you back again.

Many of us hesitate to pray about a decision because we feel foolish for needing help, or because we made a poor choice last time. This verse removes that hesitation. Hear Proverbs chapter two, verse six: For the LORD giveth wisdom: out of his mouth cometh knowledge and understanding.

Generous Father, thank You that You are not weary of our questions. Thank You that You do not shame us for our confusion or hold our past mistakes against us when we ask again. We come freely, because You give freely. Give us the wisdom this moment requires.

## Scene 3 — Asking in Faith

James continues in verse six: But let him ask in faith, nothing wavering. For he that wavereth is like a wave of the sea driven with the wind and tossed.

This is not a demand that we feel no uncertainty. The person praying this prayer is uncertain; that is why they are asking. The wavering James warns against is a divided loyalty, asking God for wisdom while having already decided to ignore it if it is inconvenient. To ask in faith is to ask ready to obey.

Lord, make us willing before we know the answer. Where we have been asking You to approve a decision we have already made, forgive us and reopen our hearts. Steady us, so that we are not driven back and forth by every opinion, every headline, and every anxious thought. Hear Psalm twenty-five, verse nine: The meek will he guide in judgment: and the meek will he teach his way. Give us that meekness.

## Scene 4 — What Wisdom From Above Looks Like

How will we recognise God's wisdom when it comes? James gives us a description. Hear James chapter three, verse seventeen: But the wisdom that is from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, and easy to be intreated, full of mercy and good fruits, without partiality, and without hypocrisy.

That verse is a test we can hold up against a choice. Is this course pure, or does it require me to hide something? Is it peaceable, or does it feed a quarrel? Is it gentle and merciful toward the people it affects? Is it honest? God's wisdom ordinarily reaches us through His Word, through prayer, and through the counsel of trustworthy people. Proverbs chapter eleven, verse fourteen says: Where no counsel is, the people fall: but in the multitude of counsellors there is safety.

Father, teach us to test our plans by this measure. And where the matter before us needs a doctor, a lawyer, a financial adviser, or another qualified person, help us to seek that help as part of walking wisely.

## Scene 5 — Wisdom for the Waiting

Sometimes we ask, and the way still does not become clear at once. That does not mean God has refused. Wisdom sometimes arrives as an answer, and sometimes as the patience to wait without panic.

Hear Psalm twenty-seven, verse fourteen: Wait on the LORD: be of good courage, and he shall strengthen thine heart: wait, I say, on the LORD. And hear Isaiah chapter thirty, verse twenty-one: And thine ears shall hear a word behind thee, saying, This is the way, walk ye in it, when ye turn to the right hand, and when ye turn to the left.

Lord, for everyone who is waiting, on news, on an opening, on another person's decision, strengthen the heart. Keep us from forcing a door out of impatience and from freezing out of fear. While we wait, show us the faithful thing that is already in front of us to do, and help us to do it well.

## Scene 6 — Receiving and Walking

The verse ends with a promise: and it shall be given him. We have asked. We will now watch, listen, and walk. So here is one practice to carry with you: write down the question you asked God today, and beneath it, over the coming days, note what you read in Scripture, what wise people tell you, and what becomes plain. Then take the step that fits the wisdom from above.

Heavenly Father, we lacked wisdom, and we have asked You for it. Thank You that You give generously and without reproach. Make our hearts steady and our ears attentive. Give us wisdom that is pure, peaceable, gentle, and full of mercy, and the courage to act on it. Go with us into what lies ahead. In the name of Jesus Christ our Lord. Type Amen to agree with this prayer, and subscribe to The Guided Path to seek God's wisdom and direction for every step.

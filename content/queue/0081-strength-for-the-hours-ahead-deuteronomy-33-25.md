## Scene 1 — The Sunrise Hook and Directing the Prayer

The morning light is already touching the edges of the room before the noise of the day begins, and perhaps you are sitting with a quiet heaviness about what the next twelve hours will ask of you. There is a sense of a road stretching out, of decisions waiting, of tasks that require more energy than you feel you currently possess. This is the moment to pause before the first email is opened, before the first conversation is held, and lift your voice toward heaven. Psalm 5:3 tells us of that early posture, where the heart looks up before the day makes its demands. We come to God this morning not because our strength is great, but because His provision is unending. Let us turn our attention to the promise of His word, finding in it the assurance that no morning finds us forgotten by our Maker.

## Scene 2 — New Mercies and Faithfulness at Dawn

As Matthew Henry notes in his commentary on the blessings of the tribes, the grace of God is custom-fitted to the journey of His people, meeting them at every turn of the road. When we look back at yesterday's weariness or look forward to today's uncertainties, we often wonder if our endurance will hold. Yet the mercies that greet us at dawn are not recycled from yesterday; they are fresh, poured out by a faithful God who knows the exact weight of the path we walk. We do not need to manufacture our own courage or pretend that the road is easier than it is. We simply acknowledge that He who made the morning has also ordained the strength required for every hour it contains. Let us rest our minds on this reality as the sun climbs higher.

## Scene 3 — Trust and Guidance for the Decisions Ahead

There are hours in every week where the path forks and the right step is not immediately clear, where responsibilities press in from every side and mental fatigue threatens to cloud our judgment. In those moments, leaning on our own understanding only deepens the strain. God does not give us a map for fifty years; He gives us light for the step we are on, and the promise that our steps are ordered by Him. When we commit our work and our choices into His hands this morning, we are releasing the quiet anxiety that insists we must carry everything alone. Let us bring every specific meeting, every difficult conversation, and every routine task before Him, asking that His wisdom would steady our thoughts and keep our motives pure.

## Scene 4 — Favour, Shield, and Protection for the Day

The blessing spoken over the tribe of Asher in the book of Deuteronomy carries a remarkable picture of steadfast readiness and divine provision for the journey. It is with this ancient assurance that we turn to the scriptural anchor for our morning devotion, reading the exact words preserved for us in the King James Version: Thy shoes shall be iron and brass; and as thy days, so shall thy strength be. Here is a promise that does not ask us to walk through rough places in bare feet, but provides what is needed for the rocky terrain of life. The iron and brass speak of durability, of a steadfast footing that will not give way beneath pressure, and the matching promise ensures that whatever the length or difficulty of the day, the supply of grace will match the demand. We stand upon this truth now, recognizing that our protection and our capability come entirely from His hand.

## Scene 5 — Blessing Over Work, Family, and Home

Father, we lift up the ordinary and the demanding hours of this day into Your keeping, asking that the work of our hands might be established by Your grace. Bless our homes, keep our families in safety, and watch over every person we will cross paths with before this day is done. Where there is tension, bring Your peace; where there is confusion, grant clarity; and where there is weariness, let Your quiet power renew the inner spirit. We do not ask for an easy path, but we ask for a faithful presence — that You would walk with us through every room, every office, and every quiet moment of waiting. May our words be seasoned with kindness, our actions marked by integrity, and our thoughts stayed upon Your goodness from sunrise to sunset.

## Scene 6 — The Sending Benediction

Go forth into the hours ahead with the quiet confidence that you are not sent alone, and that the measure of grace given to you this morning is sufficient for whatever midday brings. May the Lord bless you and keep you; may He make His face shine upon you and be gracious unto you this day.

## Scene 1 — The Crossroads of Decision

When the noise of many conflicting opinions fills your day, and every direction looks equally tangled, you find yourself standing at a quiet intersection. You are looking for a marker, a sign of which way to walk, while the weight of a decision presses quietly against your mind. It is easy to grow weary when the roads ahead are untried and the map in your hand feels inadequate for the turns before you. Yet Scripture meets us precisely at these crossroads with an ancient invitation to pause, to look up from our own calculations, and to seek the enduring wisdom that God has preserved for His people through every generation.

The prophet Jeremiah recorded these words in the sixth chapter, speaking to a generation that had lost its way among modern novelties and hurried compromises: Thus saith the LORD, Stand ye in the ways, and see, and ask for the old paths, where is the good way, and walk therein, and ye shall find rest for your souls. But they said, We will not walk therein.

Lord God, maker of heaven and earth, we come to You because we know our own understanding is short-sighted and easily confused by the shouting voices of this present hour. We stand at a junction where we must choose our steps, and we confess that we do not know which way leads to peace and which leads to regret. In Your mercy, stop our rushing feet. Grant us the grace to stand still in the ways, to look away from the shifting fashions of human counsel, and to lift our eyes toward the ancient wisdom that never changes, because You never change.

## Scene 2 — Asking for the Good Way

There is a restlessness in human nature that always assumes the newest road must be the wisest one, as if truth had an expiration date. But Matthew Henry reminds us in his commentary that the ways of God are the old ways, the paths of righteousness that holy men and women have trodden in quiet faith through centuries of trial. When we ask for the good way, we are not longing for the past out of mere sentimentality; we are asking for the unchanging moral landmarks of Scripture — truth, integrity, patience, and humble obedience — that outlast every temporary crisis and every loud temptation.

Father, when we are tempted to lean upon clever shortcuts and quick escapes, correct our inclinations. Teach us how to ask for the good way with a sincere and teachable heart. We recognize that Your Word does not flatter our impatience, nor does it endorse the restless haste that characterizes so much of our daily lives. Instead, it invites us to examine our footing, to test our motives, and to inquire after the footsteps of those who walked closely with You in humility and truth. Give us the discernment to separate the loud demands of our culture from the quiet, steady leadings of Your Spirit.

## Scene 3 — The Lamp of Unchanging Truth

We live in a time that constantly shifts its definitions of right and wrong, leaving the traveler without a reliable compass unless anchored to something higher than human opinion. The path of wisdom is illuminated not by the flashing lights of immediate convenience, but by the lamp of Your Word, which has guided the faithful through every dark valley. When we ask for the old paths, we are asking for the stability of Your commandments, which do not bend to fit our preferences, but rather shape our hearts to fit Your eternal purposes.

Lord, let Your truth be the light upon our daily path. Where we are confused by conflicting counsel, bring to our minds the clear precepts of Scripture. Guard us from the pride that thinks it can carve out a safer road than the one You have already laid down in righteousness. We acknowledge that true freedom is found not in doing whatever seems right in our own eyes, but in walking the ancient road of Your holy will. As we consider the choices before us, quiet our anxieties and direct our attention to the simple, demanding, and life-giving truths that have sustained Your people from the beginning.

## Scene 4 — Counsel and the Examination of Motives

The search for God's direction is never meant to be a solitary venture carried out in pride or isolation. Proverbs reminds us that in the multitude of counsellors there is safety, and that a wise man will hear and increase learning. Yet even among the multitude of advisors, our ultimate recourse must be the searching gaze of the Holy Spirit, who tests the reins and the heart. Before we take our next step, we must lay our hidden desires, our fears, and our ambitions before the Lord, asking Him to reveal any selfish motive or stubborn intent that might blind us to His leading.

Almighty God, search our hearts today. If there is a way we have chosen because it flatters our pride or spares our comfort rather than honoring Your name, expose it to our view. Surround us with mature, godly counsel that speaks truth in love, and grant us ears to receive correction without defensiveness. We remember that prayer and the seeking of guidance do not replace the practical wisdom of wise counsel, diligent study, and honest labor, but rather sanctify them all. May our decisions be shaped by humility, tested by Scripture, and confirmed by the peace that comes from a surrendered will.

## Scene 5 — Ordered Steps and Patient Waiting

To walk in the good way is often to walk at a pace that feels slow to our restless spirits. We want the entire map revealed at once, every obstacle removed before our feet so that we never have to stumble or wait. Yet Your Word assures us that the steps of a good man are ordered by the LORD, and He delighteth in his way. Guidance is given step by step, a lamp to our feet rather than a floodlight across the whole horizon, so that we must learn to trust Your character when the next turn is hidden from our view.

Lord, grant us the patience to wait upon Your timing. When the road ahead is steep and the fog does not lift immediately, keep us from taking matters into our own hands out of panic or frustration. Remind us that Your hand holds us fast even when our own footing feels precarious. We commit our uncertainty to You, believing that You are faithful to direct our paths as we trust in You with all our hearts and lean not unto our own understanding. Give us courage for the day's portion and rest for the soul's weariness.

## Scene 6 — Finding Rest for Our Souls

The promise attached to the old paths is quiet and profound: and ye shall find rest for your souls. It is not the rest of idleness or the absence of labor, but the deep, unshakeable peace of knowing that we are where God wants us to be, walking in the way He has appointed. In a world full of frantic striving and anxious planning, Your presence is the sanctuary where our striving ceases and our trust is restored.

Heavenly Father, we bring our tired minds and our unresolved questions before Your throne. We receive Your promise of rest, trusting that as we seek Your face and commit our ways to You, You will order our steps in righteousness. We lay down our anxieties about tomorrow, knowing that You hold the future just as surely as You hold the past.

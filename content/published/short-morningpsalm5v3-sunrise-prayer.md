Your mind is already racing before sunrise. You carry yesterday's unfinished tasks into this quiet room, wondering how you will handle what comes next. Scripture reminds us in Psalm five: My voice shalt thou hear in the morning, O LORD; in the morning will I direct my prayer unto thee, and will look up. Lord, before the house wakes and the day demands everything, I direct my thoughts straight to you. Take this hurried spirit and anchor my heart in your quiet strength. I release the need to control today's outcome, choosing instead to lift my eyes to you. Amen. Before the noise begins, look up.

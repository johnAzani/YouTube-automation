Take a moment to pause what you are doing and turn your heart toward the middle of the day. If you feed the hungry and satisfy the needs of the afflicted, then your light will rise in the darkness. And your darkness will become like the noonday. Lord, we bring our heavy burdens to You in this quiet hour. We ask that You would refresh our weary spirits and guide us with Your gentle hand through the remainder of this busy day. Grant us the grace to serve others with a selfless heart and to keep our eyes fixed solely upon Your eternal promise. In this hour of stillness, we trust You to sustain our souls and lead us toward Your perfect peace and restoration. Let us remain centered in Your presence today and every day. Amen.

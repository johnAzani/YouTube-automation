Enron hit $90.75 a share and a ~$70B valuation before one question — "Is Enron Overpriced?" — exposed mark-to-market accounting and off-balance-sheet partnerships hiding billions in debt.

On December 2, 2001, Enron filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy with $63.4B in reported assets.

A money story, not money advice. No picks, no predictions — just the record.

Primary & archival sources:
- McLean, Bethany (2001). "Is Enron Overpriced?" Fortune, March 5, 2001.
- Powers, William C., Jr. et al. (2002). Report of Investigation by the Special Investigative Committee of the Board of Directors of Enron Corp., Feb 1, 2002.
- In re Enron Corp., Case No. 01-16034 (AJG), U.S. Bankruptcy Court, S.D.N.Y., Voluntary Petition (Dec 2, 2001).
- Enron Corp. Form 10-K & Form 8-K filings, U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission.

If your mind feels divided between two choices this morning, bring your indecision to God. Teach me thy way, O LORD; I will walk in thy truth: unite my heart to fear thy name. Lord, as this morning begins, gather my scattered thoughts and calm every voice pushing me toward fear. Show me clearly which path to take today, and steady my feet upon Your holy truth. Unite my divided heart so that every decision I make honors You completely. Give me single-minded faith to follow where You lead.

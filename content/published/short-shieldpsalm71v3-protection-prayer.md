Hear the holy promise of Scripture: Be thou my strong habitation, whereunto I may continually resort: thou hast given commandment to save me; for thou art my rock and my fortress. We take refuge in Your unshakeable love, hidden from every unseen threat. Stand guard over our lives and grant us calm assurance now. Amen, even when fear tells you nowhere is safe today.

Before this hour fills with its own noise, stop and turn your heart to God. Your word says it plainly: O God, my heart is fixed; I will sing and give praise, even with my glory. Awake, psaltery and harp: I myself will awake early. I will praise thee, O LORD, among the people: and I will sing praises unto thee among the nations. So we ask for what this verse promises. Let it be true of us today, not only read. Where our own strength runs out, stand in for us. Where our thoughts wander, call them home. We are not asking for an easier day, but for Your presence through this one. Keep us steady, and let what we have read shape what we do next. Get up and carry His words with you.

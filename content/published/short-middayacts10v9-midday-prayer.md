Step away from the noise and breathe in the grace of this present moment. Acts 10:9 tells us, about the sixth hour, Peter went up on the housetop to pray. Like Peter, you are invited to lift your eyes from your daily tasks. The world is loud, but this midday hour belongs entirely to your Creator. Heavenly Father, anchor my heart in your truth during this busy day. Let your peace steady my hands and clarify my thoughts in your presence. Thank you for this sacred space to be still, to listen, and to rest.

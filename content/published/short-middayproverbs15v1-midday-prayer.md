Before you send that sharp reply, bring your rising anger to the Lord. God's Word tells us in Proverbs fifteen verse one: A soft answer turneth away wrath: but grievous words stir up anger. Heavenly Father, in this midday hour, guard our words when frustrated tempers flare. When an unfair comment burns or tension rises in the room, give us gentle wisdom to respond with steady patience rather than pride. Cool our hearts with your Holy Spirit so that peace may reign over heated words. Subscribe to join us every day as we pray at noon on The Midday Watch.

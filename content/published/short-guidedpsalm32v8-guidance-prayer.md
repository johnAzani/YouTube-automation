That heavy question in your mind right now has no obvious answer. Father, we come before You acknowledging that we lack the insight to choose our own steps. We lay our uncertainty at Your feet and ask for Your clear direction in this season. Your word reminds us of Your unfailing promise for the road ahead. I will instruct thee and teach thee in the way which thou shalt go: I will guide thee with mine eye. Lord, grant us the patience to wait for Your eye to direct our movements. In Jesus name, Amen.

As you calculate your limited resources for the week ahead, hear this holy promise. Behold, I will rain bread from heaven for you. And the people shall go out and gather a certain rate every day, that I may prove them, whether they will walk in my law, or no. Lord, help me to gather only what I need for today, trusting that your hand will supply my requirements again tomorrow. Remove my anxiety over the future and quiet my spirit as I walk in your law, resting in your perfect provision. May my actions today reflect a heart that is fully dependent upon your grace for every single earthly necessity.

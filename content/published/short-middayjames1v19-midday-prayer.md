That frustration bubbling up during your meeting is pushing you away from your true center. Wherefore, my beloved brethren, let every man be swift to hear, slow to speak, slow to wrath: For the wrath of man worketh not the righteousness of God. Lord, quiet the storm of my own reactions so that I might hear Your soft voice. Grant me the patience to withhold my tongue when my spirit feels provoked or misunderstood today. Lead me into the space where Your peace dwells above the noise of my own irritation.

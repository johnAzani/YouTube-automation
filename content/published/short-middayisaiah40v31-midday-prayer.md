Pause what you are doing right now and take a deep breath. In the middle of this busy day, bring your tired heart before the Lord. His word promises in Isaiah chapter forty verse thirty one, those who wait upon the Lord shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles; they shall run, and not be weary; and they shall walk, and not faint. Father God, we pause at midday to rest in your loving presence. Where our own strength has failed, fill us with your holy peace. Renew our minds, lift our heavy burdens, and grant us fresh grace to finish this day with joy. Subscribe to join us tomorrow for another midday watch prayer.

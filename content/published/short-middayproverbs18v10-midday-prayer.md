That relentless pressure building behind your eyes is a signal to retreat to higher ground. The name of the LORD is a strong tower: the righteous runneth into it, and is safe. Lord, when the afternoon feels overwhelming and the tasks remain unfinished, remind my weary soul. I run to the shelter of Your presence, leaving behind the weight of my own striving. Grant me the stillness to discern Your will amidst the noise of this busy day today. In this moment, I am secure because You are my hiding place and my sure defense.

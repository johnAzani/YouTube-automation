That heavy sense of exhaustion settles deep because your efforts seem to yield no visible fruit. Therefore, my beloved brethren, be ye stedfast, unmoveable, always abounding in the work of the Lord, forasmuch as ye know that your labor is not in vain in the Lord. Lord, grant me the grace to remain consistent when the path ahead feels blurred and long. May my daily tasks become an act of worship that honors your kingdom and your heart. Hold me steady in this hour so I may continue to walk faithfully in your light.

That recurring uncertainty about your capacity to handle the current challenge weighs on you today. As yet I am as strong this day as I was in the day that Moses sent me. As my strength was then, even so is my strength now, for war, both to go out, and to come in. Now therefore give me this mountain, whereof the LORD spake in that day; for thou heardest in that day. If so be the LORD will be with me, then I shall be able to drive them out, as the LORD said. Lord, grant us that same confidence to face what lies ahead, knowing you are our strength and our sure foundation in this struggle.

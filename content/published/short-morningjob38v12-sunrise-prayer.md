The heavy uncertainty holding your heart hostage this morning finds its resolve in God's authority. Hast thou commanded the morning since thy days; and caused the dayspring to know his place; Lord, we acknowledge that You govern the dawn and set the sun in its course. You alone hold the transition from dark to light, and You hold my life today. May this morning light remind me that Your plans for my hours are perfectly ordered. Help me walk in the confidence that the One who commands the morning governs my steps.

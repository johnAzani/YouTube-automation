Breathe in the stillness of this sunrise before the noise of the world awakens. Pray Psalm 143 verse 8 over your day: Cause me to hear thy lovingkindness in the morning; for in thee do I trust: cause me to know the way wherein I should walk; for I lift up my soul unto thee, O Lord my God. Lord, let your mercy speak louder than worry and guide every decision set before our hands. Grant wisdom in every conversation, peace in every trial, and steady strength until evening. Write Amen in faith to walk in His counsel today, and subscribe to The Morning Altar.

You woke up bracing for another fight. Before your feet even touch the cold floor, the day's demands are already pressing in. Hear God's word today: Let the saints be joyful in glory: let them sing aloud upon their beds. Let the high praises of God be in their mouth, and a two-edged sword in their hand; Lord, fill my quiet breath with praise before I speak a single anxious word today. Make my thanksgiving an unyielding shield against the heavy dread trying to claim this morning. Teach me to praise You from this quiet room whenever I wake up bracing for another fight. Amen.

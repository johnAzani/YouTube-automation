Bring that decision you are facing before the Lord today. Ask Him for His perfect wisdom. We are told in Proverbs 15:22, without counsel purposes are disappointed, but in the multitude of counsellors they are established. Lord, we pray for Your light to lead us. You see the road ahead, the turns and the shadows. Give us the heart to hear godly counsel and the discernment to know when You are speaking. Grant us patience to wait on Your timing and the strength to follow the path You reveal. Let our steps be firm, our minds be clear, and our spirits be anchored in Your unchanging truth.

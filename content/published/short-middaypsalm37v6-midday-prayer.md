Pause what you are doing right now and take a slow deep breath in the middle of your day. Psalm 37:6 promises, He will make your righteous reward shine like the dawn, your vindication like the noonday sun. Lord, as the midday sun reaches its highest point, we release every anxiety and burden into your hands. Reveal your truth over our work, clear away confusion, and let your justice break through the darkest shadows. We trust your perfect timing, knowing that you bring light to every hidden corner of our lives today. May your peace quiet our hearts as we step back into the remainder of this afternoon. Subscribe to pause and pray with us every day at noon.

The weariness in your chest right now tells me you doubt your ability to endure today. It is in this stillness that you can hear the ancient promise found in Deuteronomy 33:25. Thy shoes shall be iron and brass; and as thy days, so shall thy strength be. Lord, grant that this person feels that metallic, unyielding grace beneath their tired, wandering feet. May every single hour ahead match the measure of their need with your infinite, steady power. Let them walk through this day with a spirit anchored in your unchanging, faithful, holy love.

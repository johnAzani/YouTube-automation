As you calculate that mounting pressure of your daily expenses, stillness is your strength. O fear the LORD, ye his saints: for there is no want to them that fear him. The young lions do lack, and suffer hunger: but they that seek the LORD shall not want any good thing. Lord, quiet the anxious heart that wonders how the bills will be met this week. You are the Shepherd who provides for every sincere desire placed in Your holy hands. May our hearts remain focused on Your righteousness, confident that You supply our daily bread.

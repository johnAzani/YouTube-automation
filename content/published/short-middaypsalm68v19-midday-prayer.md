Blessed be the Lord, who daily loadeth us with benefits, even the God of our salvation. Father, thank You for the grace that overflows in this quiet hour of the afternoon. Teach me to see Your hand in the mundane details that currently fill my heavy schedule. Renew my spirit with the hope of Your salvation as I return to these remaining hours.

Before the demands of this day pull you in different directions, anchor your restless mind right here in this quiet dawn. Father, as morning breaks over the horizon, teach us what it means to truly rest in Your unchanging goodness. Your Word commands us with these eternal and life-giving words: Delight thyself also in the LORD: and he shall give thee the desires of thine heart. Lord, strip away every selfish ambition and every anxious striving that threatens to steal our joy this morning. May our deepest satisfaction be found in Your presence alone, knowing that You care for every single need we carry. In Jesus' name, amen.

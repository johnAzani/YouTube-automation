## Scene 1 — Approaching Jehovah-Jireh at the Prepared Table

Grace, peace, and quiet confidence be unto you, and welcome to The Provision Table. Whether you are stepping into your workplace, preparing for an important interview, studying for examinations, managing a household budget, or asking God for an open door in a tight season, take a slow, steady breath and pull up a chair before the Shepherd of Israel. Hear the faithful promise of King James Scripture in Psalm twenty-three, verse one and verse five: The LORD is my shepherd; I shall not want. Thou preparest a table before me in the presence of mine enemies: thou anointest my head with oil; my cup runneth over.

In Genesis chapter twenty-two, verse fourteen, Abraham named the place of God's timely intervention Jehovah-jireh, declaring that in the mount of the LORD it shall be seen. And in Matthew chapter six, verse thirty-two, our Lord Jesus reminds every child of God that your heavenly Father knoweth that ye have need of all these things. Lay down every financial burden, unanswered application, and weighty decision right here before the Father who sees ahead and prepares a table in advance.

## Scene 2 — Open Doors & Heaven's Good Treasure Over Honest Labour

Notice the deliberate care in David's words: Thou preparest a table before me. In The Treasury of David, Charles Spurgeon observes on Psalm twenty-three verse five that a host who prepares a table does nothing in haste or panic; every cloth is spread, every vessel is placed, and every portion is measured with royal hospitality even while opposition stands watching nearby. Matthew Henry likewise notes in his Commentary on the Whole Bible that God provides not merely bare survival for His people, but covenant kindness that honours diligent obedience.

Hear also the prayer of Moses in Psalm ninety, verse seventeen: And let the beauty of the LORD our God be upon us: and establish thou the work of our hands upon us; yea, the work of our hands establish thou it. Faithful Lord, look upon every honest trade, classroom, office, shop, farm, and ministry represented in this prayer today. Crown diligent effort with fruitfulness, open right doors of service that no human gatekeeper can shut, and let integrity and excellence mark every task we touch.

## Scene 3 — Divine Wisdom, Discernment & Direction for Decisions

Provision in Scripture always walks hand in hand with godly wisdom, for clear discernment guards what God supplies. Proverbs chapter sixteen, verse three counsels us plainly: Commit thy works unto the LORD, and thy thoughts shall be established. And James chapter one, verse five gives this generous assurance to every believer standing at a crossroads: If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not; and it shall be given him.

Father of lights, grant calm, unclouded wisdom over every practical choice before us today. Guide career steps, contract terms, academic studies, business plans, and household stewardship. Protect every listener from hasty counsel, deceptive shortcuts, or unwise debt. As Psalm thirty-seven, verse twenty-three declares, The steps of a good man are ordered by the LORD: and he delighteth in his way. Order our steps in truth, and make plain the next faithful step before our feet.

## Scene 4 — Freedom From Anxious Toil & Contentment in Every Season

When bills press close or answers seem delayed, the human heart is tempted toward restless striving and heavy worry. Yet Psalm one hundred twenty-seven, verse two warns against eating the bread of sorrows in anxious self-reliance, and our Saviour speaks directly to tomorrow's cares in Matthew chapter six, verse thirty-three: But seek ye first the kingdom of God, and his righteousness; and all these things shall be added unto you.

Matthew Henry points out on Matthew chapter six that when we make God's kingdom our first concern, He graciously takes our daily necessities into His own fatherly care. Merciful Shepherd, quiet every racing calculation and anxious fear over the months ahead. Teach our hearts the steady contentment Paul knew in Philippians chapter four, verses eleven and twelve, knowing that Christ strengthens us both in lean valleys and in seasons of harvest, and that our Shepherd never abandons His flock.

## Scene 5 — Favour With God and Man & Generous Stewardship

David testifies further in Psalm twenty-three, verse five: thou anointest my head with oil; my cup runneth over. In Psalm five, verse twelve, Scripture promises: For thou, LORD, wilt bless the righteous; with favour wilt thou compass him as with a shield. And Second Corinthians chapter nine, verse eight declares the purpose of overflowing grace: And God is able to make all grace abound toward you; that ye, always having all sufficiency in all things, may abound to every good work.

Gracious Father, surround Thy people today with shield-like favour before supervisors, clients, colleagues, examiners, and neighbours. Let trustworthy character open lasting relationships of mutual respect, and keep our hands generous, upright, and ready to refresh others whenever our cup overflows.

## Scene 6 — Overflowing Cup & Covenant Provision Benediction

We seal this prayer in the closing confidence of Psalm twenty-three, verse six: Surely goodness and mercy shall follow me all the days of my life: and I will dwell in the house of the LORD for ever. Together with Paul in Philippians chapter four, verse nineteen, we confess in steadfast faith: But my God shall supply all your need according to his riches in glory by Christ Jesus.

Heavenly Father, we thank Thee that no need before us outmeasures Thy riches in Christ, and that goodness and mercy walk behind and before us today. Bless the work of our hands, guard our households in peace, and establish every righteous endeavour for Thy glory. Type Amen to agree with this prayer, and subscribe to The Provision Table to walk in God's daily wisdom and provision.

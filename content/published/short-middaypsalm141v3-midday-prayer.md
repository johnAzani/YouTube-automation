Before you say a harsh word in your midday frustration, bring your speech to God. Psalm 141 verse 3 says, Set a watch, O LORD, before my mouth; keep the door of my lips. Heavenly Father, guard our tongues when sudden anger or impatience rises today. Give us wisdom to pause before we react in haste. Help us speak with gentle kindness, patient restraint, and quiet love to everyone we meet this afternoon. Let our words build up rather than break down, reflecting Your peace in every conversation. May Your Holy Spirit watch over our lips right now. Subscribe to join The Midday Watch for daily prayer.

That hunger for more is unsettling. It shifts your focus from the Giver. Listen to the wisdom of Agur. Remove far from me vanity and lies: give me neither poverty nor riches; feed me with food convenient for me: Lest I be full, and deny thee, and say, Who is the LORD? or lest I be poor, and steal, and take the name of my God in vain. Lord, anchor my heart in this middle ground. Keep me from the pride of plenty or the bitterness of want. Supply today what I need to walk in Your ways, that hunger for more is unsettling.

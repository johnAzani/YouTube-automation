That choice sitting on your mind needs wisdom. When every option feels heavy and unclear, turn to His Word. The Lord promises in Psalm 16:11, Thou wilt show me the path of life: in thy presence is fulness of joy; at thy right hand there are pleasures for evermore. Lord, we yield our own plans and ask for Your clear direction. Lead this quiet heart away from confusion and onto Your steady trail. Fill this present moment with Your peace, knowing Your hand holds tomorrow. Show us the way forward, Lord, whenever that choice sitting on your mind needs wisdom.

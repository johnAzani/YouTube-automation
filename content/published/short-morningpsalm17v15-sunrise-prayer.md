That recurring doubt about your worth is ready to be laid down at His feet. As for me, I will behold thy face in righteousness: I shall be satisfied, when I awake, with thy likeness. Lord, help me to look past the fleeting shadows of this world and fix my gaze on You. May the peace of Your presence sustain me throughout the demands of the day ahead now. Grant me the grace to walk in the light of Your character, reflecting Your love clearly. In the name of Jesus, I rest in the truth that You are my only satisfaction.

Your feet feel unsteady on this uncertain path. When the ground beneath your next step feels unstable, turn to the Lord in Psalm 17, verse 5: Hold up my goings in thy paths, that my footsteps slip not. Heavenly Father, we admit our own understanding cannot keep us upright when the terrain grows steep. Establish our steps in Your wisdom today, and guard our hearts against every secret fear. Sustain us through every heavy choice today, so that we do not falter or slide backward into doubt. Amen. Lord, hold our steps steady when your feet feel unsteady on this uncertain path.

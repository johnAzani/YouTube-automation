Step away from your desk for a moment and lift your heart to God in the middle of the day. Psalm thirty-seven, verse five declares: Commit thy way unto the Lord; trust also in him; and he shall bring it to pass. You do not have to carry the unfinished work or the heavy decisions of this afternoon on your own shoulders. Lord, right here at noonday, we place every task, every conversation, and every unseen outcome into Your faithful hands. Steady our minds with Your wisdom, guard our speech with peace, and renew our quiet strength for the hours ahead. We trust You to bring Your good purpose to pass today.

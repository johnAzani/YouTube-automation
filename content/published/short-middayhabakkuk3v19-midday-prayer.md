Your knees feel weak on this steep hill. The noon sun is hot, and the climb ahead looks far too steep for tired legs. Hear the promise in Habakkuk three, verse nineteen: The LORD God is my strength, and he will make my feet like hinds' feet, and he will make me to walk upon mine high places. Lord, when the path of this day turns rough, grant us steady feet. Hold us up where the ground gives way, and lift our eyes above the dark valley. Give us your quiet endurance until we reach the high places of your peace. Amen. When your knees feel weak on this steep hill.

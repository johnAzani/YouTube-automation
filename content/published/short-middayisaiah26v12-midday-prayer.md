Your unfinished work is crowding your mind today. You have strived through the morning hours, carrying every single task as though it rested entirely on your own strength. Hear the quiet promise of Isaiah 26 verse 12: LORD, thou wilt ordain peace for us: for thou also hast wrought all our works in us. Lord, we lay down our self-reliance in this middle hour of the day. Ordain peace over the labor of our hands, knowing that every true accomplishment is wrought by You. Quiet our restless thoughts and grant us steady focus for what lies ahead. Amen. Receive His peace when your unfinished work is crowding your mind today.

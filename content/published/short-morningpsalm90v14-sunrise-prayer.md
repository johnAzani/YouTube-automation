Before you check your morning messages or face today's heavy calendar, turn your heart upward. O satisfy us early with thy mercy; that we may rejoice and be glad all our days. Lord, before the demands of this hour fill our thoughts, we ask for Your grace to wash over us. Fill the empty places in our souls with Your steadfast love, so that no anxiety can take root. May Your early mercy quiet every lingering fear from yesterday and anchor our spirit in steady joy. Teach us to walk through this day with confidence, knowing Your compassion renewed us before the sun reached its height. Subscribe to pray with us tomorrow.

If you are standing at an unfamiliar crossroads tonight wondering which turn to make, listen. Lord, my footing feels uncertain tonight and I bring this hesitation directly before You. Grant me the profound peace of knowing Your hand is steady upon my life right now. The steps of a good man are ordered by the LORD: and he delighteth in his way. Father, when my weakness causes me to stumble, remind me of Your sustaining grace. Though he fall, he shall not be utterly cast down: for the LORD upholdeth him with his hand. Calm every anxious thought in my mind and let my heart rest safely in Your sovereign care. Guide my every action tomorrow according to Your perfect will, in Jesus' holy name. Amen.

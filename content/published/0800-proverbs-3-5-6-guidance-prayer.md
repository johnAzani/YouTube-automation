## Scene 1 — Bringing the Decision to the Lord

Grace and peace to you, and welcome to The Guided Path. Perhaps you have come with a decision in front of you, or with a season that simply does not make sense yet. You do not have to carry it alone. Hear the King James Scripture in Proverbs chapter three, verses five and six: Trust in the LORD with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding. In all thy ways acknowledge him, and he shall direct thy paths.

These words were written as a father's counsel to his child. They do not begin with a method for choosing. They begin with a person to trust. Father, we bring You the matter that is on our minds right now. We name it honestly before You: the choice, the uncertainty, the fear of getting it wrong. Before we ask for an answer, we ask for a heart that trusts You.

## Scene 2 — With All Thine Heart

The verse asks for trust with all the heart. That is harder than it sounds. Most of us trust God with the parts of life we cannot control and keep a firm grip on the rest. We pray about the illness and manage the career ourselves. We pray about the children and keep the money to our own judgment.

The Lord is not asking for a divided trust. Hear Psalm thirty-two, verse eight: I will instruct thee and teach thee in the way which thou shalt go: I will guide thee with mine eye. That is the promise of a God who watches over the whole road, not one stretch of it.

Lord, search out the places we have fenced off from You. Where we have said, this part I will handle, teach us to open our hands. We trust You with the outcome we want, and with the outcome we are afraid of. We trust You with the timing. We trust You with what other people will think.

## Scene 3 — Lean Not Unto Thine Own Understanding

To lean is to put your weight on something. The proverb does not tell us to throw away our understanding. God gave us minds, and He expects us to use them: to gather facts, to count the cost, to think clearly. The warning is against resting our whole weight there, as though what we can see were all there is.

The very next verse makes the point. Proverbs chapter three, verse seven: Be not wise in thine own eyes: fear the LORD, and depart from evil. And Proverbs chapter sixteen, verse nine adds: A man's heart deviseth his way: but the LORD directeth his steps.

Father, thank You for the understanding You have given us. Help us to use it well and to hold it humbly. Where we are certain and wrong, correct us gently. Where we are missing something, show it to us. Keep us from the pride that will not listen and from the fear that will not move.

## Scene 4 — In All Thy Ways Acknowledge Him

To acknowledge the Lord in all our ways is to bring Him into the ordinary, not only the urgent. It means asking, in the small choices of a day, what would honour God here. It means opening the Scriptures before we open our plans. Hear Psalm one hundred nineteen, verse one hundred five: Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path.

A lamp for the feet lights the next step, not the whole journey. And God often guides through people as well. Proverbs chapter fifteen, verse twenty-two says: Without counsel purposes are disappointed: but in the multitude of counsellors they are established.

Lord, we acknowledge You in this decision. Lead us to the passages of Your Word we need to hear. Bring wise and honest people alongside us, and make us willing to listen. Where this matter calls for medical, legal, or financial expertise, help us to seek qualified advice, and to receive it as part of Your care.

## Scene 5 — He Shall Direct Thy Paths

The promise is that He shall direct our paths. It does not say we will always see the whole route, or that the right road will be the easy one. It says the One who sees the end from the beginning will make the way straight before us as we walk with Him.

And if we stumble, we are not abandoned. Hear Psalm thirty-seven, verses twenty-three and twenty-four: The steps of a good man are ordered by the LORD: and he delighteth in his way. Though he fall, he shall not be utterly cast down: for the LORD upholdeth him with his hand.

Faithful God, quiet the fear that one wrong turn will ruin everything. You are able to lead us, and You are able to restore us. Give us patience where we must wait, and courage where we must act. If a door closes, help us to trust You. If a door opens, help us to walk through it with thanksgiving.

## Scene 6 — The Next Faithful Step

We cannot know today everything that tomorrow holds, and we are not asked to. We are asked to take the next faithful step. So here is one practice to carry with you: write the decision down in a single sentence, pray Proverbs three, verses five and six over it, and then do the one thing that is clearly right today.

Hear Proverbs chapter sixteen, verse three: Commit thy works unto the LORD, and thy thoughts shall be established.

Heavenly Father, we commit this matter to You: the choice, the timing, and the result. We trust You with all our heart. We will not lean on our own understanding alone. In all our ways we acknowledge You, and we ask You to direct our paths. Give us peace as we wait and wisdom as we move. In the name of Jesus Christ our Lord. Type Amen to agree with this prayer, and subscribe to The Guided Path to seek God's wisdom and direction for every step.

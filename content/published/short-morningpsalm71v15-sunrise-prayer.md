The tasks ahead feel too vast to measure. Yet God offers grace beyond any calculation. Hear Psalm 71 verse 15: My mouth shall show forth thy righteousness and thy salvation all the day; for I know not the numbers thereof. Lord, as light breaks, I admit I cannot count every demand or foresee every trial. Your mercies outnumber my anxieties, and Your strength fills every unknown hour. Help me speak of Your goodness from morning until evening, trusting what I cannot calculate. Give me peace to step into this morning with quiet trust. In Jesus' name, Amen.

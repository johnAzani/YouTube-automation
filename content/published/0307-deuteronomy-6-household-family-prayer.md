## Scene 1 — Gathering Around the Family Altar

Father in heaven, Lord God of all creation, Maker of heaven and earth, and Sovereign Sustainer of every household that calls upon Your holy name, we gather our hearts this hour around the family altar. As Joshua declared for his house, so we declare for ours: as for me and my house, we will serve the Lord. We come before You acknowledging that every good gift and every perfect blessing comes down from above, from the Father of lights, with whom is no variableness, neither shadow of turning. You have established families upon the earth as a picture of Your own eternal covenant love, placing the solitary in families and setting a hedge of grace around our dwelling places. We thank You, Lord, for the four walls You have provided, for the roof over our heads, and for the quiet sanctuary of our home. We bring every member of our household into Your holy presence today — our husbands, our wives, our sons, our daughters, and all who cross our threshold. We ask that Your Holy Spirit would hover over this place, turning our house from a mere dwelling of brick and mortar into a house of prayer, a temple of praise, and a beacon of Your enduring truth. As we open Your precious Word, let our spirits be quieted, our minds be stayed upon You, and our affections be drawn upward to the throne of grace, in Jesus' matchless and holy name. Amen.

## Scene 2 — Building the House Upon God's Wisdom

Almighty God, You have declared in Your Holy Word through the pen of the Psalmist that except the Lord build the house, they labour in vain that build it. We recognize that no human wisdom, no earthly strength, and no modern ingenuity can secure a home against the shifting storms of this age unless Your eternal truth is laid as the chief cornerstone. In Deuteronomy chapter six, verses six and seven, Your servant Moses commanded Israel with these timeless words: And these words, which I command thee this day, shall be in thine heart: And thou shalt teach them diligently unto thy children, and shalt talk of them when thou sittest in thine house, and when thou walkest by the way, and when thou lie down, and when thou risest up. Lord, we receive this divine mandate as the blueprint for our home. We pray that Your holy statutes would not merely rest upon the pages of a closed book, nor hang merely as ornaments upon our walls, but that they would be deeply inscribed by the finger of God upon the tablets of our hearts. Matthew Henry reminds us in his exposition of this covenant passage that religion in the home is the most effective means of preserving godliness from generation to generation; for family instruction plants the seeds of eternal life where the soil is most tender and near. Father, forgive us for the moments we have relied upon our own understanding rather than seeking Your counsel. Cleanse our hearts from every distraction, every anxious care, and every worldly ambition that would crowd out Your voice. Let Your Word be the final authority in our decisions, the rule of our conduct, the comfort of our sorrows, and the joy of our daily fellowship, so that our home may stand immovable upon the Rock of Ages. Amen.

## Scene 3 — Covering Children, Sons, Daughters & Generations

Lord God of our generations, we lift up the children You have graciously entrusted to our care — our sons and our daughters, both young and grown. Psalm 128 describes the fruitful vine and the olive plants round about our table, picturing the quiet beauty of a household blessed by Your fear. We pray today that all our children shall be taught of the Lord, and that great shall be the peace of our children, exactly as Your prophet Isaiah foretold. We ask for a supernatural hedge of divine protection around their minds, their hearts, their steps, and their friendships. In a world full of confusing voices, competing philosophies, and glittering temptations, let the still, small voice of Your Holy Spirit be louder in their ears than any voice of the world. Give us, as parents and guardians, the heavenly wisdom, the infinite patience, and the gentle firmness needed to train them up in the nurture and admonition of the Lord. When we sit in our house, let our conversations be seasoned with grace and seasoned with truth. When we walk by the way on our daily journeys to school, to work, and into the community, let Your presence go before them and follow them. Guard their waking hours from evil influences, and watch over their sleeping hours with Your holy angels encamped around their beds. We claim the promise that a good man leaveth an inheritance to his children's children, and we pray that the faith we practice today at this family altar will become an unshakeable spiritual inheritance for our descendants down through the coming years, bringing glory to Your name from generation to generation. Amen.

## Scene 4 — Unity, Peace & Grace Within Marriage and Relationships

Gracious Redeemer, You are the God of peace who brought again from the dead our Lord Jesus, that great Shepherd of the sheep. We invite Your healing, reconciling, and binding love to flow freely through every relationship within our walls. We pray for husbands and wives, asking for the grace of selfless, Christlike love that mirrors Christ's love for His church — a love that suffers long, is kind, envieth not, vaunteth not itself, is not puffed up, seeketh not her own, is not easily provoked, thinketh no evil. Where there has been misunderstanding, friction, or careless words spoken in moments of human weakness, we ask for quick forgiveness, tender mercy, and complete restoration. Let Colossians three rule in our hearts, where we are called to put on bowels of mercies, kindness, humbleness of mind, meekness, longsuffering; forbearing one another, and forgiving one another, if any man have a quarrel against any: even as Christ forgave you, so also do ye. And above all these things put on charity, which is the bond of perfectness. And let the peace of God rule in your hearts, to the which also ye are called in one body; and be ye thankful. Father, let our home be a quiet resting place, an oasis of calm in a weary and distracted world, where every member of the family finds emotional safety, spiritual encouragement, and unconditional acceptance. Remove every root of bitterness, every spirit of division, and every selfish ambition, replacing them with the sweet fruit of the Spirit — love, joy, peace, longsuffering, gentleness, goodness, faith, meekness, and temperance. Amen.

## Scene 5 — Honest Provision, Health & Protection Over Every Threshold

O Lord of hosts, Jehovah-Jireh, our faithful Provider and Shield, we commit the daily temporal needs of our household into Your capable and bountiful hands. You know what we have need of before we ask Him, and You feed the fowls of the air and clothe the grass of the field with splendor; how much more will You care for Your own children who gather in Your name. We pray for honest provision, for diligence in our labor, and for wisdom in our stewardship of every resource You entrust to us. Bless the work of our hands, O Lord, and establish Thou the work of our hands upon us; yea, the work of our hands establish Thou it. We pray for health and vitality in every physical body represented at this table, asking that You would lay Your healing hand upon any illness, fatigue, or weakness, restoring strength according to Your perfect will. We plead the blood of the everlasting covenant over every doorpost of our house. Let no weapon formed against this family prosper, and every tongue that rises against us in judgment, let us condemn. As we go out through our front door into the community, and as we return home through that same door at the end of the day, command Your angels concerning us to keep us in all our ways. Guard our thresholds from moral corruption, from spiritual darkness, and from physical harm, making this house a fortress of righteousness where Your holy name is honored morning, noon, and night. Amen.

## Scene 6 — Aaronic Household Blessing & Covenant Benediction

And now, O God of our salvation, we seal this time of household prayer with the ancient and holy blessing You gave to Aaron and his sons to place upon Your people. The Lord bless thee, and keep thee: The Lord make His face shine upon thee, and be gracious unto thee: The Lord lift up His countenance upon thee, and give thee peace. We cast all our care upon You, knowing that You care for us with an everlasting love. We rest secure in the hollow of Your hand, confident that You are able to keep us from falling and to present us faultless before the presence of Your glory with exceeding joy. Type Amen to agree with this prayer, and subscribe to The Family Altar to cover your home and family in daily prayer. In the glorious and mighty name of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, we pray. Amen.

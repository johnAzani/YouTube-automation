That persistent restlessness you feel while staring at your ceiling tonight needs a quiet surrender. Cause me to hear thy lovingkindness in the morning; for in thee do I trust: cause me to know the way wherein I should walk; for I lift up my soul unto thee. Lord, the path ahead looks hidden and my own understanding feels like a broken compass today. Clear away the noise and fear, replacing my anxiety with the steady rhythm of Your truth. Give me the courage to move only when You move, and to rest where You lead.

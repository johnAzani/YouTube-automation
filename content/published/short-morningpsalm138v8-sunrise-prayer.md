You are carrying an unfinished story into this morning, afraid you will fail. God promises in Psalm 138: The LORD will perfect that which concerneth me: thy mercy, O LORD, endureth for ever: forsake not the works of thine own hands. Lord, we release every concern into Your capable hands this morning. What we cannot complete, You are already working out for our good. Your mercy surrounds us today and lasts forever. Do not abandon the work You have begun in our lives, but finish it according to Your divine purpose. We trust Your faithful love for every detail of this day.

## Scene 1 — But Thou, O LORD, Art a Shield for Me (Verse 3a)

When enemies surround you and accusers rise against your name, remember: the LORD is your shield.

But thou, O LORD, art a shield for me.

A shield does not remove the battle; it protects you in the midst of it.

Tonight, you do not have to fight. You only have to stand behind the shield of divine protection.

Dear God, as I prepare to rest, I acknowledge the internal battles and external pressures that try to assault my peace. Thoughts of worry, fears of tomorrow, and the echoes of past failures often threaten to overwhelm me. But tonight, I lean into Your promise. You are my shield against every arrow of anxiety, every whisper of doubt, and every lie of the adversary. Protect my mind from restless thoughts, guard my heart from discouragement, and keep my spirit safe within Your unfailing presence. Let Your divine barrier stand firm around me, so I can find true rest, trusting that no harm can penetrate Your perfect defense. Be my refuge and my strong tower until the morning light. Amen.

## Scene 2 — My Glory, and the Lifter Up of Mine Head (Verse 3b)

When shame bows your head and sorrow weighs on your spirit, God is the One who lifts you up.

He restores your dignity. He returns your glory. He raises your face to look toward heaven again.

No defeat is permanent when the LORD is the lifter of your head.

Sleep tonight knowing that tomorrow, He will raise you higher than you fell today.

Father, there are moments when the weight of past regrets or current disappointments causes my head to hang low. The burden of things unsaid or undone, or the sting of perceived failures, can cast a shadow over my soul. But You, O Lord, are the lifter of my head. I pray tonight that You would gently, yet powerfully, lift my gaze from the dust of despair towards the hope of Your love. Restore my sense of worth, remind me of my true identity in Christ, and fill my spirit with Your glorious light. Let the shame dissolve in the warmth of Your acceptance, and let sorrow be replaced by the quiet confidence that You are always working for my good. Lift my spirit, Lord, and prepare me for renewed joy. Amen.

## Scene 3 — I Cried Unto the LORD With My Voice (Verse 4a)

You do not have to pray eloquently. You only have to cry out.

I cried unto the LORD with my voice, and he heard me out of his holy hill.

God hears the groans that cannot be formed into words. He hears the tears that fall in silence.

Your cry reaches the holy hill. You are heard.

Merciful God, sometimes my prayers feel disjointed, my words inadequate, and my heart too heavy to articulate its deepest needs. Yet, Your Word assures me that You hear me, not because of my eloquence, but because You are a God who listens to the heart's true cry. Tonight, I lift my voice, even if it's just a whisper or an unspoken yearning in my soul. Hear my gratitude, hear my concerns, hear my requests for peace and protection. Let the certainty that my cry ascends to Your holy hill, to Your throne of grace, bring profound comfort to my restless spirit. Thank You for being a God who is ever attentive, ever present, and ever ready to respond. Amen.

## Scene 4 — I Laid Me Down and Slept; I Awaked (Verse 5a)

I laid me down and slept; I awaked — not because of your own strength, but because of divine sustenance.

Every morning you wake is a miracle of preservation.

The fact that you opened your eyes today is proof that God sustained you through the night.

Rest in that same sustaining power tonight.

Heavenly Father, as I prepare to lie down, I acknowledge that my ability to rest deeply and awaken refreshed is not dependent on my own efforts or anxieties, but solely on Your gracious sustenance. I ask that You would watch over me through the hours of darkness. Guard my sleep from disturbances, quiet any anxieties that seek to steal my peace, and renew my body, mind, and spirit as I slumber. Just as You sustained me through the day and brought me safely to this moment, I trust You to carry me through the night. Let Your powerful presence be my comfort and my peace, ensuring a tranquil sleep and a joyful awakening, ready to embrace the new mercies of tomorrow. Amen.

## Scene 5 — For the LORD Sustained Me (Verse 5b)

The LORD sustained me — not your willpower, not your caffeine, not your determination.

God's sustaining power carried you through every dark hour.

He will sustain you through tonight as well.

Close your eyes. You are held. You are kept. You are sustained.

Almighty God, I surrender all my worries, my striving, and my attempts to control to Your mighty hands. I confess that I often rely on my own strength, my own planning, and my own resolve to navigate life's challenges. But tonight, I declare that You alone are my ultimate sustainer. It was Your grace that carried me through yesterday, Your mercy that met me this morning, and it is Your unwavering power that will carry me through this night. I release the burdens of the day and entrust my sleep, my health, and my future into Your keeping. Hold me close in Your unfailing love, keep me safe from all harm, and sustain my very being with Your divine peace. Amen.

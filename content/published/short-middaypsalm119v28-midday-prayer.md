That persistent feeling of exhaustion that makes every simple task feel impossible is understood today. Listen to the word of the Lord as it speaks into your quiet spirit right now. My soul melteth for heaviness: strengthen thou me according unto thy word. Lord, the weight of this midday hour presses upon me, yet I turn my eyes upward. Refresh my tired mind, replace this inner heaviness with the anchor of your eternal truth, God. I receive your quiet strength for the remainder of my afternoon work and my home.

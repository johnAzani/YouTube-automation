Midday hours slip past while tasks remain undone. You feel the relentless pressure of a ticking clock right now. God's word guides us: So teach us to number our days, that we may apply our hearts unto wisdom. Lord, in the quiet middle of this afternoon, steady our anxious hearts. We often spend our precious hours chasing endless demands and forgetting eternity. Teach us to measure our time by Your grace rather than our worries. Grant us wisdom to release unnecessary stress and walk in Your peaceful truth. Amen. Midday hours slip past while tasks remain undone.

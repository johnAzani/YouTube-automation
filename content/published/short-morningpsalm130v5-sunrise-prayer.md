That decision weighing on your heart is not too heavy for God to handle today. I wait for the LORD, my soul doth wait, and in his word do I hope. My soul waiteth for the Lord more than they that watch for the morning: I say, more than they that watch for the morning. Lord, quiet the restless parts of me that demand answers before I have even stepped forward. Help me trust that you are working in the hidden spaces of this current season. Let your peace anchor my soul as I walk into whatever this new day brings.

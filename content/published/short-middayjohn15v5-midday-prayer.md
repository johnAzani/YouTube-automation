Your strength feels thin from striving alone today. You try to force results from your own exhausted effort, forgetting where real life grows. Hear the Savior in John fifteen verse five: I am the vine, ye are the branches: He that abideth in me, and I in him, the same bringeth forth much fruit: for without me ye can do nothing. Lord, we yield our restless work back to your hands. Teach us to abide in you right here, trusting you for what we cannot produce. Quiet our hurried minds in your grace. Amen. Return to peace when your strength feels thin from striving alone.

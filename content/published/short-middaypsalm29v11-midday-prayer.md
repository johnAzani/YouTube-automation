The heavy weight of that ongoing friction at your office is exhausting your spirit today. You are carrying more than you were meant to hold alone during these busy hours. The LORD will give strength unto his people; the LORD will bless his people with peace. May that quiet divine assurance settle into your mind as you finish your remaining work. Replace your striving with a deep trust that the Creator is holding your afternoon steady. Stay held in this light and carry this quiet confidence into everything you do next.

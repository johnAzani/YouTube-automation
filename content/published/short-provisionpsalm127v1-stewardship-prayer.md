Hear Psalm 127, verses 1 and 2: Except the LORD build the house, they labor in vain that build it: except the LORD keep the city, the watchman waketh but in vain. It is vain for you to rise up early, to sit up late, to eat the bread of sorrows: for so he giveth his beloved sleep. Father, we hand You the tools, the ledgers, and the sleepless strain of our self-reliance. Build what we cannot build, and guard our labor while we rest. Amen. You calculated late into the night.

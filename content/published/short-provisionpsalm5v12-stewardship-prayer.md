If you are carrying a quiet worry about your household finances today, bring it to Him. For thou, LORD, wilt bless the righteous; with favour wilt thou compass him as with a shield. Father, we place our daily work, practical decisions, and household obligations into Your faithful hands. Where we see closed doors, You open paths of grace and daily supply. Guide our hands in honest labor, and guard our minds against all anxious fears. Teach us to walk wisely, trusting that Your divine favor surrounds us as a shield. Establish the work of our hands, and provide abundantly for every practical need. Subscribe to The Provision Table to walk in God's daily provision.

## Scene 1 — Gathering Around the Family Altar

When the noise of the day quiets and the doors of your house are closed against the troubles of the world, there is no greater posture for a head of household or a gathering family than to bend the knee before the throne of grace. This is the heart of our gathering today, to lift our spouses, our children, our roof, and our thresholds unto the Maker of all things. In the epistle to the Ephesians, the Apostle Paul opens his heart with a pastoral desire that mirrors the ancient burden of Joshua when he declared that as for him and his house, they would serve the Lord. We come before Him recognizing that a house is built by wisdom and established by understanding, and that no family stands in its own strength. We read these holy words from Ephesians chapter three, verses fourteen and fifteen: For this cause I bow my knees unto the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, Of whom the whole family in heaven and earth is named. Think upon that wondrous reality. Every family name, every lineage, every household that looks to the Lord finds its ultimate source and naming in Him. The patriarchs of old raised altars wherever they pitched their tents, marking the ground as holy unto God, and so we gather our spirits right now to anchor our dwelling place in His eternal grace. Let us settle our hearts, bring every domestic burden into His presence, and remember that our home is not our own, but is purchased by His blood and watched over by His providence.

## Scene 2 — Building the House Upon God's Wisdom

Except the Lord build the house, they labour in vain that build it. We understand that brick and mortar, earnings and provisions, routines and daily schedules are nothing without the sustaining breath of the Almighty. Matthew Henry reminds us in his commentary on the household of faith that true family religion is the best security for any dwelling, turning an ordinary home into a sanctuary where God's counsel governs every conversation and every trial. We look to verse sixteen of our passage, where the Apostle petitions the Father with these words: That he would grant you, according to the riches of his glory, to be strengthened with might by his Spirit in the inner man. Lord, we ask this for our homes today. When weariness presses upon our minds, when the responsibilities of providing for our families feel heavy, and when patience runs thin within these walls, grant us spiritual might in our inner man. Do not let us lean on our own frail understanding. Strengthen the fathers to lead with gentleness and truth. Strengthen the mothers to nurture with wisdom and grace. Strengthen every member of this household to bear one another's burdens and to walk in the humility of Christ. Let Your Holy Spirit fortify our minds against discouragement, division, and every idle word that threatens the peaceable habitation You have given us. We confess that we are weak in ourselves, but Your grace is sufficient, and Your riches in glory are inexhaustible.

## Scene 3 — Covering Children, Sons, Daughters & Generations

Our children and the generations rising up around our tables are like olive plants, fresh and full of promise, yet needing the constant watering of God's Word and the hedge of His protection. The scripture gives us a profound anchor in verse seventeen: That Christ may dwell in your hearts by faith; that ye, being rooted and grounded in love. Lord, we pray this over every child, every son, and every daughter connected to this household, whether they sit at our table today or walk paths far from our physical sight. Let Christ dwell in their hearts by a living, active faith. Root them deep in the soil of Your unfailing love so that when the winds of temptation blow and the storms of a shifting culture beat against them, they shall not be moved. May their faith not be merely borrowed from their parents, but let it be their own personal encounter with the living God. Grant them discernment to choose righteousness, courage to stand for truth, and a lifelong affection for Your holy commandments. We place our children into Your hands, believing Your promise that those who seek You early shall find You, and that a generation that knows Your name will declare Your praise to their own children yet unborn.

## Scene 4 — Unity, Peace & Grace Within Marriage and Relationships

A house divided against itself cannot stand, and the adversary seeks nothing more than to sow discord, impatience, and misunderstanding among those who dwell under the same roof. Therefore, we invoke the peace of God that passes all understanding to guard our marriages, our sibling bonds, and every relationship within these walls. The Scripture continues in verses eighteen and nineteen with a breathtaking scope: May be able to comprehend with all saints what is the breadth, and length, and depth, and height; And to know the love of Christ, which passeth knowledge. Lord, pour out Your supernatural harmony upon our marriage and our home. Where harsh words have brought sorrow, bring Your healing balm. Where impatience has strained our unity, grant us the mind of Christ, who humbled Himself for our sakes. Teach us to forgive as we have been forgiven, to bear with one another in love, and to keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace. Let our home be a quiet resting place, an embassy of heaven where grace abounds and where the love of Christ is tasted in every daily interaction. May our family relationships reflect the unchanging steadfastness of Your covenant toward us.

## Scene 5 — Honest Provision, Health & Protection Over Every Threshold

We turn our hearts now to the honest daily needs of this household, trusting in Jehovah-Jireh, the God who provides seed for the sower and bread for the eater. We do not ask for worldly vanity or riches that rust and fade, but we ask for daily bread, for honest labor, for health of body and soundness of mind, and for the preservation of our coming and going. The Apostle concludes this great passage with a marvelous petition: that ye might be filled with all the fulness of God. Think on what it means to be filled with the fullness of God — not lacking in wisdom, not empty of joy, not destitute of courage, but overflowing with divine sustenance. Father, place Your holy hedge of protection around our threshold. Guard our comings out and our goings in. Drive far from our dwelling all sickness, all fear, all anxiety, and every weapon formed against our peace. Bless the work of our hands so that we may have enough to share with those in need, and let contentment rule in our hearts. We rest securely under the shadow of Your wings, knowing that You neither slumber nor sleep, and that Your eye is ever upon this righteous habitation.

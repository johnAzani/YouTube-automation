That stubborn pain lingers tonight. Lord, when the body aches and exhaustion settles deep into the bones, meet us right here in this quiet hour. We turn to Your holy word written in Isaiah 53:5. But he was wounded for our transgressions, he was bruised for our iniquities: the chastisement of our peace was upon him; and with his stripes we are healed. Father, let Your healing grace touch every hidden hurt and restore what has grown weary. Grant us quiet trust when the healing takes longer than we hoped. Anchor our souls in Your unchanging love, and let Your peace guard our hearts until morning. Amen. That stubborn pain lingers.

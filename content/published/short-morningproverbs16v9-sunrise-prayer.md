Before you write down your plans for today, hand your uncertain schedule to God. Proverbs sixteen, verse nine says, A man’s heart deviseth his way: but the LORD directeth his steps. Heavenly Father, you know the maps we draw in our minds and the paths we try to build on our own. Today, as this morning sun rises, we yield our personal blueprints into your wise and loving hands. Order our footsteps where we cannot see ahead, and grant us peace when your direction strays from our plans. Lead us clearly in every choice we face today.

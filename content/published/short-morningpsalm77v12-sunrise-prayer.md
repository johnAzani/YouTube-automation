The heavy worries returned before daybreak. Before the noise of this morning crowds in, remember what God has carried you through. The psalmist wrote: I will meditate also of all thy work, and talk of thy doings. Lord, anchor my restless thoughts right now in your steady faithfulness rather than my fears. When uncertainty tries to take hold, remind me of the quiet ways you have always provided. Teach my mouth to speak of your goodness today instead of rehearsing defeat. Give me eyes to see your hand moving in every small detail. Lead me through this new light, and quiet the heavy worries that returned before daybreak. Amen.

That bone-deep exhaustion sitting on your shoulders right now is whispering that you have nothing left to give. You have spent the morning running on empty, trying to quench a thirst that never quite goes away with temporary things. Listen to the promise given to your weary soul in the middle of this busy day. Jesus answered and said unto her, Whosoever drinketh of this water shall thirst again: But whosoever drinketh of the water that I shall give him shall never thirst; but the water that I shall give him shall be in him a well of water springing up into everlasting life. Lord, let that eternal well rise up inside this quiet room and flood every dry place in my heart. Give me this living water today so I may walk in your peace until the sun goes down. Amen.

If you are holding back an unfinished plan tonight because you fear your own misstep, listen. The word of God reminds us in Proverbs 16:9: A man's heart deviseth his way: but the Lord directeth his steps. Father, we confess that our own understanding is so often limited by what we can see right now. We lay our personal agendas down before Your throne and ask You to order every future step we take. Close every door that leads away from Your peace, and give us the quiet grace to walk only where You lead.

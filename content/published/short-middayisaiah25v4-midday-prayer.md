Right now, wherever you are, gently pause whatever you are doing. Take a slow, deep breath, and let the rush of the morning fade. As the sun reaches its highest point in the sky, turn your heart toward the Lord. Listen to the unchanging words found in Isaiah twenty-five, verse four. For you have been a stronghold to the poor, a stronghold to the needy in his distress. A shelter from the storm and a shade from the heat. Lord, in the middle of our busy days, we rest beneath Your quiet shade. Be our shelter, strengthen our hands, and grant us peace for the hours ahead.

Scripture reference: Isaiah 25:4 (KJV / Public Domain biblical text).

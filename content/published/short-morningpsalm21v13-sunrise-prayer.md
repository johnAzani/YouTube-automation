Your own strength ran dry yesterday. You step into this quiet sunrise wondering what you have left to give. Hear the word of God from Psalm 21, verse 13: Be thou exalted, LORD, in thine own strength: so will we sing and praise thy power. Father, we lay down our hurried striving and look to the power that spoke light into the sky. Be exalted in every impossible task, every quiet ache, and every hidden trial waiting on our doorsteps. Work through what our hands cannot resolve, and give our hearts reason to sing praise before dusk falls. Amen. Rest in His power when your own strength runs dry.

That project weighing on your heart needs your effort even before you see the outcome. In the morning sow thy seed, and in the evening withhold not thine hand: for thou knowest not whether shall prosper, either this or that, or whether they both shall be alike good. Heavenly Father, quiet my frantic soul as I plant these small seeds of humble obedience. Help me to labor with hope, trusting that You oversee every result of my work. Grant me the patience to keep sowing while resting entirely in Your sovereign, perfect timing. Let my hands stay busy for Your glory regardless of what blooms later this day. Amen.

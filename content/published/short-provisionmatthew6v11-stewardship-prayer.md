Father, we come before You acknowledging that every good gift and every necessary provision comes from Your open hand. We lay down the frantic striving of tomorrow and quiet our anxious thoughts right here in Your holy presence. Your holy word guides our lips and anchors our faith as we pray together: Give us this day our daily bread. Sustain us through every trial, fill our hands with what we need, and strengthen our trust in Your unfailing faithfulness.

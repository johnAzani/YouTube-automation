## Scene 1 — The Unshakable Refuge in Trouble (Verses 1-3)
Before you close your eyes tonight, breathe deeply and release the turmoil of this day.
God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble.
Therefore we will not fear, even though the earth be removed, and though the mountains be carried into the midst of the sea.
Though its waters roar and be troubled, though the mountains shake with its swelling.
Your God is greater than every crisis, and His presence surrounds your dwelling.
Oh Lord, our magnificent refuge, as the echoes of the day's struggles linger, we seek Your impenetrable fortress. You are not a distant help, but a very present comfort, ready to receive our every anxiety. Tonight, we consciously turn from the shaking ground of earthly worries, choosing to place our trust in Your steadfastness. Let Your divine strength be the bedrock beneath our weary spirits, guarding us from all fear, even if the foundations of our world seem to shift and roar around us. May Your peace fill this space entirely.

## Scene 2 — The River of Gladness & Holy Habitation (Verses 4-5)
There is a river whose streams shall make glad the city of God, the holy place of the tabernacle of the Most High.
God is in the midst of her; she shall not be moved. God shall help her, just at the break of dawn.
Let the gentle current of His holy peace wash over your tired soul tonight.
You will not fall, and you will not be shaken, because the Lord God dwells in your midst.
Gracious God, we imagine the river of Your joy, flowing through Your holy city, making glad all who dwell within Your divine presence. Tonight, we long to be immersed in those very streams of gladness. Let Your living water cleanse our minds and soothe our hearts, washing away the residue of stress and fatigue. We rest in the assurance that You are in our midst, O Most High, promising stability even as the first light of dawn approaches. Keep us firm and unmovable in Your love until morning.

## Scene 3 — The Melting Earth & The Voice of Majesty (Verses 6-7)
The nations raged, the kingdoms were moved; He uttered His voice, the earth melted.
The Lord of hosts is with us; the God of Jacob is our refuge.
When the noise of the world threatens your peace, remember the voice of the Almighty.
At His whisper, storms subside, mountains yield, and darkness gives way to light.
Almighty Father, when the clamor of the world's unrest and the internal echoes of our own anxieties rage, we remember Your sovereign voice. You spoke, and nations were moved, the very earth melted before Your power. Tonight, we pray that Your still, small voice would utterly silence every disruptive thought and fear within us. Let the majesty of Your presence dissipate all shadows, just as the earth yielded to Your command. May Your whisper be our calming balm, bringing profound tranquility as we sleep.

## Scene 4 — Behold the Works of the Lord (Verses 8-9)
Come, behold the works of the Lord, who has made desolations in the earth.
He makes wars cease to the end of the earth; He breaks the bow and cuts the spear in two; He burns the chariot in the fire.
The battles that troubled you today do not belong to you; they belong to the Lord.
He disarms every weapon formed against your family, and commands peace into your home.
Lord of all creation, we stand in awe as we behold Your mighty works, for You bring desolation to the plans of the proud and peace where there was conflict. You are the one who makes wars cease, breaking every weapon of contention. Tonight, we lay down the specific battles we faced today—the arguments, the frustrations, the spiritual attacks—and commit them wholly to Your superior power. Disarm every adversary, visible and invisible, and establish Your unwavering peace over our household and within our hearts.

## Scene 5 — Be Still, and Know That I Am God (Verse 10)
Be still, and know that I am God. I will be exalted among the nations, I will be exalted in the earth.
Let go of striving. Cease your labor. Drop every burden at the foot of the cross.
In this holy silence, know that He is your Father, your Protector, and your Eternal King.
Sovereign God, we heed Your gentle command to "be still, and know that I am God." In this moment of quiet surrender, we release all control, all efforts to fix or understand, and simply rest in Your divine authority. Tonight, we seek to truly *know* You – not just intellectually, but intimately, as our merciful Father and faithful Protector. Exalt Your name within our spirits, O Lord, and let the profound truth of Your identity anchor us completely, lifting our gaze from earthly concerns to Your heavenly throne.

## Scene 6 — The Lord of Hosts Is With Us (Verse 11)
The Lord of hosts is with us; the God of Jacob is our refuge.
Rest tonight in holy stillness. Angelic hosts stand watch over your threshold.
Sleep deeply, sleep peacefully, under the unslumbering care of the Almighty.
Amen.
Lord of Hosts, with us still, and God of Jacob, our unchanging refuge, we embrace this sacred stillness You have granted. As we drift into slumber, we trust implicitly in Your constant vigilance and boundless care. Thank You for Your presence, which encompasses us entirely, guarding our bodies, minds, and spirits. We rest assured that Your faithful watch never ceases, and Your peace stands guard at the door of our deepest sleep. May Your comfort be our companion through the dark hours.

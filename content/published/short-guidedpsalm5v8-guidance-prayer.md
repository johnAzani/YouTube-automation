As you stand at this crossroads wondering which step to take, hear this quiet truth. There is a path designed for your spirit that honors the Lord in all things. Lead me, O LORD, in thy righteousness because of mine enemies; make thy way straight before my face. Father, clear the fog from my perspective so I see the path You have prepared. Quiet my anxious heart so I might recognize Your gentle guidance in this complex situation. May Your light illuminate the next move I make and guard my steps from error. I surrender my confusion to You, trusting that You alone know the perfect way forward.

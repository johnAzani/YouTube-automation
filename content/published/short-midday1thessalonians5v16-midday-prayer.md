In every thing give thanks: for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus concerning you. Father, when the afternoon demands too much, turn our weary breath into a quiet prayer. Help us notice Your presence beside this busy desk and inside this crowded room. Give us grace to thank You before the work is finished. Amen. Rest in His grace, even as your unfinished tasks are crowding your midday.

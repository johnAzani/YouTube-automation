## Scene 1 — It Is of the Lord's Mercies That We Are Not Consumed (Lamentations 3:22)

It is of the LORD's mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not.

Jeremiah wrote those words from the middle of ruins, not from the middle of comfort. The city he loved lay broken, and still, at daybreak, he could say: we are not consumed. If you woke up this morning carrying a hard season — a loss you did not choose, a failure you cannot rewind, a prayer that still seems unanswered — this is your sunrise verse. You are still here because mercy held you. The fact that you are alive to pray this morning is itself the first evidence that God has not finished with you.

Father, I thank You that I am not consumed. Not because my circumstances have all changed, but because Your mercy never ran out in the dark. Where I deserved silence, You gave compassion. Where I expected the story to end, You kept a page I had not read yet. This morning I receive that mercy with open hands. Let it silence shame, soften grief, and give me strength to begin again. In Jesus' name, amen.

## Scene 2 — They Are New Every Morning (Lamentations 3:23)

They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness.

New every morning. Not recycled leftovers from somebody else's blessing. Not the dregs of yesterday's strength. Like manna in the wilderness, God's compassion arrives fresh with the day, measured for exactly what this day will ask of you. Matthew Henry notes that we should take every fresh morning as a fresh token of God's covenant mercy, because the supply was never designed to be stored in fear. You do not have to live today on yesterday's grace. There is mercy with your name on it, dated this very morning.

Lord, I receive Your new mercies today. For the meeting I am dreading, there is fresh grace. For the family member I am worried about, there is fresh mercy. For the weakness in me that failed before, there is compassion that has never been used up. Teach me to gather it like manna, daily and gratefully, instead of anxiously hoarding or proudly refusing. You are faithful in every season, and this morning is no exception. Amen.

## Scene 3 — The Lord Is My Portion (Lamentations 3:24)

The LORD is my portion, saith my soul; therefore will I hope in him.

A portion is an inheritance, the share that has your name written on it. Jeremiah had lost almost everything a person can count, and from the ashes he discovered the one possession that cannot be taken: God Himself. This morning, before you check what you have and what you lack, let your soul say what his soul said. When the Lord is your portion, you may be waiting for answers, but you are never without treasure. Hope is not denial when it is anchored here. Hope is the arithmetic of a heart that knows what it truly owns.

Father, You are my portion. Therefore I will hope in You, today, out loud, on purpose. I will not let what is missing preach louder than Who is present. Let hope rise in me while it is still morning, before the day argues its case. Where I have been measuring my life by what was taken, teach me to measure it by the God who remains. My soul says it again: therefore will I hope in Him. Amen.

## Scene 4 — Good unto Them That Wait for Him (Lamentations 3:25-26)

The LORD is good unto them that wait for him, to the soul that seeketh him. It is good that a man should both hope and quietly wait for the salvation of the LORD.

Some of today's answers will not arrive by noon, and this verse teaches us how to stand in the meantime. Waiting on God is not wasted time; it is the workshop where trust is built. To seek Him in the morning is to declare that the outcome belongs to Him, not to our panic. Quietly wait does not mean carelessly wait. It means the rested, confident stillness of someone who has placed the matter in stronger hands and refuses to take it back every hour.

Lord, I seek You this morning, and I will wait for You without despair. For the answer that is still on its way, give me quiet hope. For the door that has not opened, give me clean patience. Guard me from forcing in fear what You are forming in faithfulness. While I wait, let me work faithfully, love generously and speak hopefully, as a person who truly believes the salvation of the Lord is worth waiting for. Amen.

## Scene 5 — Fresh Mercy for the Work of This Day (Lamentations 3:23)

Great is thy faithfulness.

Now carry that faithfulness out of the door with you. The God who was faithful in Jeremiah's ruins will be faithful in your office, your home, your studies and your journey today. His faithfulness is not a museum piece to admire in a verse; it is the ground under your next decision, the covering over your children, and the reason you can attempt again what defeated you before. Begin the tasks of this day as someone already supplied, already accompanied, already beloved.

Faithful God, establish the work of my hands today. Bless my family with mercies they will recognise as Yours. Let my words be seasoned with the same compassion I received at sunrise, and let my work be honest, excellent and at peace. Where I meet weary people, make me a carrier of fresh mercy, not fresh criticism. Let great is Thy faithfulness be the testimony written over every hour of this day. In Jesus' name, amen.

## Scene 6 — Great Is Thy Faithfulness: The Sending (Lamentations 3:22-23)

It is of the LORD's mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not. They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness.

Say it as a declaration over everything waiting for you: great is Thy faithfulness. Over the unfinished and the uncertain. Over the diagnosis and the deadline. Over the child, the calling and the quiet private battle. The sun came up this morning because God ordered it to, and the same faithful hand has ordered mercy to meet you in every place this day will take you. You do not walk out hoping God might be faithful. You walk out because He already has been.

Father, I go into this day under the banner of Your faithfulness. When I am tempted to panic, remind me of this morning. When I am tempted to quit, remind me that Your compassions never fail. Let me return tonight able to say again, with even more evidence than I had at sunrise: great is Thy faithfulness. In the mighty name of Jesus, amen.

If your hands feel heavy after hours of quiet effort that nobody else noticed, stay here. Hear God's gentle instruction for your midday: And let us not be weary in well doing: for in due season we shall reap, if we faint not. Lord, you see the quiet work of this day—the kindness that went unthanked, the faithful care given in secret, and the labor that feels unfinished. Grant renewed strength to this tired heart. Remind us that no faithful act is ever wasted in your kingdom, and that your harvest arrives at the perfect time. Subscribe to rest in midday prayer.

You carry a heavy ledger of unanswered needs. Hear the word of God in Psalm 68 verse 19: Blessed be the Lord, who daily loadeth us with benefits, even the God of our salvation. Heavenly Father, when tomorrow's demands press hard against our small supply, remind us that You do not measure grace grudgingly. You load us daily with mercy, wisdom, and bread for every honest duty. Forgive our fearful counting when we look at empty cupboards instead of Your faithful hand. Open doors of daily provision, grant peace to our hands, and sustain us. In Jesus' name, Amen. You carry a heavy ledger of unanswered needs.

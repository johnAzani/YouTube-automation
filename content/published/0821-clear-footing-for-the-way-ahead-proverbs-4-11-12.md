## Scene 1 — Bringing the Decision to the Lord

You may be holding a decision today that feels heavier with every passing hour. Perhaps you are looking across several choices, trying to discern which road carries real wisdom and which one only offers temporary ease. The fear of making a false step can turn even small decisions into a source of quiet anxiety. In these moments, Scripture does not ask you to figure out the whole journey on your own strength. It calls you to bring the decision directly to the Father, who delights in giving clear direction to those who seek Him with an open heart.

Listen to the words of Solomon recorded in Proverbs chapter four, verses eleven and twelve:

"I have taught thee in the way of wisdom; I have led thee in right paths. When thou goest, thy steps shall not be straitened; and when thou runnest, thou shalt not stumble."

Let us bring this moment before God together.

Heavenly Father, we come to You with open hands and quiet hearts. We acknowledge that our human sight is limited. We cannot see around the bend of tomorrow, nor can we foresee the full fruit of the choices before us today. Forgive us for the times we rely on our own understanding or rush forward in our own impulse. Right now, we lay down our anxiety, our self-reliance, and our desire to control the outcome. We ask You to take the lead in our hearts, our thoughts, and our actions. Teach us to trust Your counsel above our fears, and draw our eyes to the wisdom that comes only from You. In Jesus' name.

## Scene 2 — Asking God for Wisdom

When we ask God for direction, we are not asking for a distant set of instructions. We are asking for the heart of a teacher who walks alongside us. In his classic commentary on the book of Proverbs, Matthew Henry notes that when God instructs His children, He does not merely point out the road from afar; He leads us by the hand through His Word and His Spirit, teaching us how to walk uprightly so that our steps remain secure. Wisdom is not merely knowing what is right, but having the spiritual discernment and humility to practice it daily.

Scripture promises in James chapter one, verse five, that if any of us lack wisdom, we may ask of God, who gives to all generously and without reproach. God does not mock our uncertainty, nor does He turn away from our questions when we come in sincere faith.

Father, we ask You today for a teachable spirit. Give us the discernment to distinguish between what is merely appealing to our human desires and what is truly right in Your sight. Grant us the courage to choose the right path even when it requires patience, self-discipline, or letting go of short-term comfort. Where our minds feel cluttered by competing opinions, let Your truth bring quiet clarity. Guard us from pride, from hasty judgments, and from the snare of leaning solely on human wisdom. Let Your Holy Spirit illuminate our understanding so that every choice we make honors Your holy name.

## Scene 3 — The Lamp of the Word

The Lord promises in Proverbs chapter four, verse twelve: "When thou goest, thy steps shall not be straitened; and when thou runnest, thou shalt not stumble." 

To have one's steps straitened means to be hemmed in by distress, confined by anxiety, or trapped in a narrow, dangerous place. But when God leads us in right paths, He grants us spiritual liberty and sure footing. His guidance does not guarantee that every trial will vanish overnight, nor does it mean we will always see the distant horizon. As the Psalmist writes in Psalm 119, verse 105, "Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path." A lamp carried in the night does not illuminate the entire mountain range at once; it lights the very next step so that our feet do not slip.

Lord, help us to treasure Your Word as the ultimate guide for our daily life. When the way ahead seems obscured by fog or uncertainty, remind us that You give sufficient light for today. Help us not to demand full sight of the distant future before we are willing to obey what You have already made clear. Grant us grace to trust the light of Your truth over the shifting emotions of our hearts. Deliver us from the panic that comes from trying to master what only You control, and teach us to rest in the quiet assurance that Your Word will never lead us astray.

## Scene 4 — Wise Counsel and Tested Motives

Scripture repeatedly highlights the value of godly counsel and honest self-examination when seeking direction. Proverbs chapter fifteen, verse twenty-two reminds us, "Without counsel purposes are disappointed: but in the multitude of counsellors they are established." Seeking guidance is not an isolated discipline. God frequently uses mature believers, wise mentors, and trusted counselors to confirm His truth and help us see what our own biases might obscure. 

At the same time, we must remember that prayer and spiritual discernment walk hand in hand with practical responsibility and wise counsel. Seeking God's guidance never replaces the need for qualified medical, legal, financial, or professional advice when dealing with specific earthly matters. God grants wisdom through many means, and true spiritual discernment honors both His Word and the practical truth He places before us.

Father, search our hearts today and test our motives. Reveal to us any hidden self-interest, stubbornness, or fear that might be clouding our judgment. Surround us with godly, wise counselors who love Your truth and will speak with honesty and grace. Give us the humility to listen to sound advice and the strength to weigh every suggestion against the authority of Holy Scripture. Where practical steps and professional help are needed, grant us the prudence to seek them diligently, trusting that all truth and wisdom ultimately belong to You.

## Scene 5 — Waiting With Patience and Ordered Steps

One of the greatest challenges in seeking guidance is learning how to wait. We live in a world that demands immediate answers, yet the Lord often works through seasons of waiting to refine our character and strengthen our faith. Psalm thirty-seven, verses twenty-three and twenty-four reminds us: "The steps of a good man are ordered by the LORD: and he delighteth in his way. Though he fall, he shall not be utterly cast down: for the LORD upholdeth him with his hand."

When you feel as though your progress is slow or that a decision remains unresolved, do not assume that God has forgotten you. Waiting on the Lord is not lost time; it is the holy process by which our motives are purified and our trust is rooted deeply in Him. Even if you make an honest mistake along the journey, God's upholding hand is powerful enough to correct your course and bring you back into His peace.

Lord God, grant us the patience to wait upon Your timing without growing restless or faint. Calm the urgency in our spirits that demands instant results. Teach us to stand firm in faith while You order our steps, trusting that Your timing is perfect and Your care is unfailing. When doubt tempts us to rush ahead or turn back in discouragement, anchor our souls in Your promises. Remind us that You are holding our right hand, and that no path led by Your Spirit will ever end in shame.

## Scene 6 — Committing the Way and Closing Benediction

As we conclude this time of prayer, we rest in the quiet assurance of Proverbs chapter sixteen, verse three: "Commit thy works unto the LORD, and thy thoughts shall be established." 

You do not have to carry the burden of the unknown into tomorrow. Place your plans, your questions, your family, and your daily decisions into the hands of the Almighty. He who taught you in the way of wisdom will continue to lead you in right paths, keeping your feet from stumbling and giving you peace for every step of the journey.

May the Lord bless you with divine wisdom and deep discernment in every decision you face. May He direct your path, clear away every stumbling block of fear, and give you steady confidence in His unfailing love. May His presence go before you today, granting you quiet strength, clear vision, and perfect rest for your soul.

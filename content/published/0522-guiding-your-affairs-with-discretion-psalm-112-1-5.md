## Scene 1 — The Blessed Man Who Feareth the Lord

When the desk is covered in figures that do not quite balance, or when a decision about tomorrow’s labour weighs heavily on your mind before the day has even begun, it is easy for anxious calculations to crowd out quiet trust. You may be sitting at a kitchen table with a ledger, or standing in a workspace wondering how to navigate complex choices with integrity. This is the place where the Shepherd meets us — not with hurried schemes, but with the steady wisdom of His Word. In the opening verse of the one hundred and twelfth Psalm, the writer declares: Praise ye the LORD. Blessed is the man that feareth the LORD, that delighteth greatly in his commandments. 

Lord Jehovah, You are the Maker of heaven and earth, and You have not left Your children to walk through the mazes of this life without a lamp for our feet. We come before You this day acknowledging that true blessing does not spring from clever manipulation or anxious striving, but from a heart that reveres Your holy name. When our minds are troubled by the demands of our daily work and the pressures of providing for those we love, still our restless thoughts. Grant us that holy fear of God which cleanses the motive and steadies the spirit. As we open Your Word, teach us to delight not in shortcuts or doubtful gains, but in Your statutes, which are right and true. Let Your fear be our anchor when the winds of economic uncertainty blow around us.

## Seed and Substance in the House

The honest labour of our hands and the quiet faithfulness of daily stewardship are watched over by a God who takes pleasure in the prosperity of His servants. Matthew Henry, in his commentary on the sacred text, reminds us that the religion of a good man diffuses influence over his entire household, establishing his ways in truth. The second and third verses of the Psalm assure us: His seed shall be mighty upon earth: the generation of the upright shall be blessed. Wealth and riches shall be in his house: and his righteousness endureth for ever.

O Lord our God, You are Jehovah-Jireh, the God who provides for every season of our pilgrimage. We lay our household needs, our family responsibilities, and the honest work of our vocations before Your throne. We ask for no unearned windfalls or dishonest gains, but we pray according to Your promise for that steady, enduring provision that sustains a family in righteousness. Bless the work of our hands, O Lord. Where we sow seed in tears or toil in quiet obscurity, let Your blessing rest upon our labour. Guard our homes from the blight of greed and the corroding rust of debt. Let our dwelling place be a house where truth is honoured, where generosity abounds, and where Your sufficient grace covers every tangible need of bread and shelter.

## Light Rising in the Darkness

There are seasons in every career and in every household when the path ahead seems obscured by shadows — when a business deal stalls, an employment door closes, or unexpected burdens test our endurance. It is in those very moments that the promise of divine light proves its worth. The fourth verse of our Psalm declares: Unto the upright there ariseth light in the darkness: he is gracious, and full of compassion, and righteous.

Father of mercies, You know the exact corners of our lives where the light feels dim today. You see the silent anxieties of those who are waiting for a breakthrough in their employment, or seeking an open door of opportunity that man cannot shut. When confusion surrounds us, let Your light break forth upon our path. Where the way is dark and the next step is hidden, grant us the illumination of Your Holy Spirit. And Lord, when trials press heavily upon us, keep our hearts from bitterness. Make us gracious and full of compassion toward others who struggle alongside us, reflecting the righteousness of the Master whom we serve. Let no dark circumstance sour our integrity or make us cold to the needs of our neighbors.

## Discretion in Daily Affairs

The daily work of earning a living, managing a household, and making decisions that affect our future requires more than good intentions; it requires heavenly discretion. The practical outworking of a heart devoted to God is seen not merely in words, but in the careful, upright management of ordinary responsibilities. Verse five of the Psalm sets before us this standard: A good man showeth favor, and lendeth: he will guide his affairs with discretion.

O Lord, give us the spirit of wisdom and sound judgment in all our daily affairs. We confess that left to ourselves, we are prone to hasty choices, emotional reactions, and shortsighted planning. Forgive us for the times we have leaned on our own understanding rather than committing our work unto You. Grant us grace to guide our business, our vocations, and our domestic responsibilities with godly discretion. Teach us when to speak and when to be silent, when to venture forward and when to wait patiently. Cultivate within us a generous spirit that is willing to show favour and help those in need, without imprudence or carelessness. Let our financial stewardship be a silent testimony to the sound mind that Christ imparts to His followers.

## Trusting the Shepherd in the Valley

To guide one's affairs with discretion does not mean we hold the future in our own fists; rather, it means we place our daily tasks into the nail-scarred hands of the One who orders all things well. The psalmist who wrote of wealth and righteousness also knew what it was to walk through valleys where the table was prepared in the presence of adversaries. The wisdom of Psalm one hundred and twelve is the wisdom of a quiet heart that rests in divine providence.

Lord Jesus, we quiet our spirits before You now. We release the heavy burden of trying to orchestrate every outcome of our careers and households by our own strength. You are the Shepherd who leads us beside still waters and restores our soul. If our hands are holding too tightly to anxious plans or fearful projections, loosen our grip this hour. Remind us that our times are in Your hands. We look away from our frail capabilities and look unto Your unfailing faithfulness. Supply our daily bread, not just for our bodies, but for our minds and spirits, that we may walk through our workdays with a calm and settled confidence in Your goodness.

## The Overflowing Cup and Covenant Benediction

As we draw this time of prayer to a close, we lift our eyes once more to the table the Lord has prepared for us — not a table of earthly excess, but a covenant table of sufficiency, peace, and daily grace. His promise endures through every generation, and His goodness follows us all the days of our life. 

May the Lord bless you and keep you. May the Lord make His face shine upon you and be gracious unto you in every labour of your hands. May He guide your steps with holy discretion, open doors of righteous opportunity before you, and fill your house with His quiet peace.

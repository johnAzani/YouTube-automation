## Scene 1 — Rising at the Midnight Hour

Grace and peace to you, and welcome to The Midnight Watch. In this quiet twelfth hour, when the world around you rests in silence, God still calls watchful intercessors to take their post upon the walls of prayer. Hear the sovereign commission of King James Scripture in the book of the prophet Isaiah, chapter sixty-two, verse six and verse seven: I have set watchmen upon thy walls, O Jerusalem, which shall never hold their peace day nor night: ye that make mention of the LORD, keep not silence, And give him no rest, till he establish, and till he make Jerusalem a praise in the earth. Notice who appoints the watchman: God Himself declares, I have set watchmen upon thy walls. When your spirit is stirred to pray at midnight for your household, your church, and your calling, treat that stirring as a holy invitation from the Lord who loves to answer persevering faith.

## Scene 2 — Praise Inside the Midnight Trial

Isaiah spoke these words concerning a season when restoration was promised yet not fully visible to the natural eye. Rather than telling God's people to fold their hands in resignation, the Lord summoned them to unceasing remembrance of His covenant promises. Matthew Henry, in his Commentary on the Whole Bible on Isaiah sixty-two, notes that those whom God calls to be watchmen are the Lord's remembrancers — believers who humbly put God in remembrance of His own word and delight to plead His promises before His throne. Charles Spurgeon, expounding this same watchman calling, remarked that importunate prayer does not weary the Almighty; rather, God Himself commands His children to keep not silence because He has already purposed to bless them through their asking. Tonight at midnight, we thank God in advance that every promise spoken in His Word stands sure and unchangeable.

## Scene 3 — Standing Upon the Watchtower

Read carefully that phrase in verse six: ye that make mention of the LORD, keep not silence. In ancient cities, watchmen stationed along the high stone ramparts called out through the night watches to prove the wall was guarded and to welcome the coming light. Spiritually, a home with a praying watchman on its walls is never left unguarded in the dark. Righteous Father, we take our stand upon the watchtower at this midnight hour in humble obedience to Thy Word. Forgive our seasons of prayerlessness or quick discouragement. Strengthen our inward endurance so that we pray without fainting, holding fast to Thy faithfulness over the years and months ahead. Make our hearts alert to Thy Spirit, discerning in wisdom, and steadfast in love.

## Scene 4 — Loosening of Heavy Burdens & Breakthrough

Verse seven continues with extraordinary boldness: And give him no rest, till he establish, and till he make Jerusalem a praise in the earth. Look also at verse ten of the same chapter: Go through, go through the gates; prepare ye the way of the people; cast up, cast up the highway; gather out the stones; lift up a standard for the people. Watchman intercession clears the stones of hindrance off the path ahead. Lord God Almighty, as we stand in midnight prayer, gather out every stumbling stone of fear, confusion, division, and prolonged heaviness from before our steps. Establish what has felt unstable in our work, our health, and our spiritual walk. Lift up Thy standard of truth and peace over every place where pressure has gathered, and bring forth enduring fruit that brings honour unto Thy holy name.

Isaiah sixty-two verse one sets the example of Christ's own intercessory heart: For Zion's sake will I not hold my peace, and for Jerusalem's sake I will not rest, until the righteousness thereof go forth as brightness, and the salvation thereof as a lamp that burneth. Notice that image for the midnight hour — salvation shining forth like a lamp that burneth steadily against the dark sky. When we stand upon the watchtower at twelve o'clock, we are joining our small, faithful petitions to the great intercession of our High Priest, Jesus Christ, who ever liveth to make intercession for His people and whose lamp of mercy is never extinguished.

## Scene 5 — Divine Turning Point & Guarded Household

Earlier in Isaiah sixty-two, verse two and verse three promise that God's redeemed shall be held as a crown of glory in the hand of the Lord, no longer termed Forsaken or Desolate, because the Lord delighteth in His people. Tonight we set watchmen's prayer around every room of our homes and around every person dear to us. Father, guard our children, spouses, parents, and households from unseen harm and spiritual discouragement. Where any loved one has felt forgotten or weary, speak peace into their heart and surround them with wise, godly counsel. Establish our households upon righteousness, integrity, and unfeigned faith so that our homes become quiet sanctuaries of praise in the earth.

## Scene 6 — Midnight Thanksgiving & Watchman's Benediction

As we complete this midnight watch upon the walls, we rejoice that the Supreme Watchman over our souls is the Lord Himself, who keeps Israel without slumbering. Father, thank Thee for hearing the voice of Thy remembrancers at this twelfth hour. Seal every scripture we have pleaded tonight deep within our hearts, and let steady peace accompany us through every watch to come. Unto Him that is able to keep you from falling, and to present you faultless before the presence of His glory with exceeding joy, be glory and majesty, dominion and power, both now and ever. Type Amen to agree with this prayer, and subscribe to The Midnight Watch to stand on the watchtower in midnight prayer.

If the weight of this midday decision is draining your strength, do not suffer alone. Let us therefore come boldly unto the throne of grace, that we may obtain mercy, and find grace to help in time of need. Grant us mercy for our stumbles and timely grace for the tasks remaining today. Calm our hearts and fill this room with Your peace. Subscribe to pray with us every midday on The Midday Watch.

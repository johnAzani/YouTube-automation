If you are sitting alone in the quiet room wondering how your heart survived this week, listen. Father, we come to you carrying the deep fractures of our lives, acknowledging our brokenness in your holy presence. You are the God who sees every hidden tear and every weary sigh that escapes our lips. As your word declares, Come, and let us return unto the LORD: for he hath torn, and he will heal us; he hath smitten, and he will bind us up. Lord, where we feel shattered by circumstance, extend your gentle hand to mend what is broken inside. Pour out your tender mercy upon every heavy spirit seeking solace in you today, O God. Establish our steps in your unfailing grace, trusting your restoring power through every changing season of life. In Jesus name we pray, Amen.

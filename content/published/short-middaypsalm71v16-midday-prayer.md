Lord, remove the desire to rely upon my limited wisdom when yours is infinite and perfect. Let this midday reset remind me that I walk not in my ability but in yours. May the truth of your righteousness be the anchor that holds me through this coming afternoon. I surrender my agenda to your sovereign plan, finding peace in your presence right now, Amen.

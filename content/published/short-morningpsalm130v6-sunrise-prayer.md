You counted every hour waiting for dawn. The heavy thoughts you carried into the dark are still sitting beside your bed. In Psalm 130, verse 6, the Scriptures remind us: "My soul waiteth for the Lord more than they that watch for the morning: I say, more than they that watch for the morning." Lord, when the darkness inside feels longer than the night, steady our weary hearts. Teach us to look for Your grace with the same certainty that daylight returns to this room. Quiet our fears and anchor our trust in You today. Amen. You counted every hour waiting for dawn.

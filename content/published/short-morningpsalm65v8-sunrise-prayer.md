If a distant fear woke you before sunrise today, bring your trembling heart to God. They also that dwell in the uttermost parts are afraid at thy tokens: thou makest the outgoings of the morning and evening to rejoice. Lord, from the furthest edges of the earth to this very room, Your signs stir holy wonder. When fear tries to claim our thoughts, remind us that You hold both the dawn and the dusk. You tune the sunrise to joy and command the evening to sing of Your praise. Turn our quiet anxiety into deep delight as this new light breaks across our path. Subscribe to The Morning Altar to begin every sunrise in prayer.

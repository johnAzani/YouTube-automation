Before the demands of this hour pull you in different directions, rest right here. Father, before my feet touch the floor and before I look at my list of tasks, my heart turns to You. You are my God, and I look to You alone for the strength I need to walk through today. O God, thou art my God; early will I seek thee: my soul thirsteth for thee, my flesh longeth for thee in a dry and thirsty land, where no water is; When the world feels dry and difficult, let Your living water refresh my weary spirit. Fill every empty place inside me this morning so I can walk in Your peace. In Jesus' name, amen.

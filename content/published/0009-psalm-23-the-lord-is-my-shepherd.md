## Scene 1 — The Good Shepherd & Surrender of Want (Verse 1)
Before you close your eyes tonight, take a deep breath and let go of every burden you carried today.
The Lord is my shepherd; I shall not want.
When the Lord guides your life, you are never lacking, never forgotten, and never alone.
He knows the desires of your heart, the exhaustion in your body, and the quiet prayers of your spirit.
Surrender the illusion that you must control tomorrow. Rest tonight in the arms of the One who provides.

## Scene 2 — Green Pastures & Still Waters of Rest (Verse 2)
He makes me to lie down in green pastures; He leads me beside the still waters.
Notice that the Shepherd does not push you into hurry; He invites you into rest.
He leads you away from the turbulent currents of the world into quiet waters of divine peace.
Let the stillness of His presence wash over your room right now.
Breathe out the noise, the pressure, and the deadlines.
His grace is your quiet pasture tonight.

## Scene 3 — The Restoration of the Weary Soul (Verse 3)
He restores my soul; He leads me in the paths of righteousness for His name's sake.
If your spirit felt depleted, bruised, or weary today, receive God's gentle restoration.
God heals what the world broke. He realigns your mind with heavenly truth.
As you sleep, the Holy Spirit is renewing your strength, mending your thoughts, and breathing life into your spirit.
You will wake tomorrow with fresh joy, guided by His righteous hand.

## Scene 4 — Peace in the Shadow of the Valley (Verse 4)
Yea, though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil; for You are with me; Your rod and Your staff, they comfort me.
Even when shadows gather around your thoughts, remember that a shadow cannot exist without a light nearby.
The Lord Himself is the light standing between you and the dark.
His rod defends you from every hidden attack; His staff gently pulls you close to His heart.
You are not alone in the valley; the Almighty is walking beside you.

## Scene 5 — The Table Prepared & Cup Running Over (Verse 5)
You prepare a table before me in the presence of my enemies; You anoint my head with oil; my cup runs over.
God does not wait for all your troubles to vanish before He blesses you; He sets a banquet of peace right in the midst of battle.
He anoints your head with the fragrant oil of His Holy Spirit, calming every headache, every anxious tremor, and every fear.
Your cup is not half full; in Christ, it overflows with boundless mercy and unconditional love.

## Scene 6 — The Dwelling of Goodness & Mercy (Verse 6)
Surely goodness and mercy shall follow you all the days of your life; and you will dwell in the house of the Lord forever.
Goodness and mercy are God's two heavenly guardians stationed behind you, erasing your past mistakes and crowning your future with grace.
Sleep in deep peace tonight. God has already gone ahead to prepare your tomorrow.
Receive this shepherd blessing into your soul.

Stop everything you are doing right now. Take a deep breath and center your heart. O God, you are my God, earnestly I seek you; my soul thirsts for you, my body longs for you, in a dry and weary land where there is no water. In this middle hour of the day, bring your thirst to the only One who satisfies. May your spirit be refreshed as you rest in His presence away from the rush of the world.

That urgent decision resting on your desk demands an answer you feel unprepared to give. When the pressure rises and every second counts, remember you can speak directly to heaven. Hear the record of a servant who threw a silent prayer upward before the king spoke again. Then the king said unto me, For what dost thou make request? So I prayed to the God of heaven. Lord, grant us that same instantaneous trust when our hearts are heavy with tasks today. Direct our steps in this quiet noon hour and keep our minds anchored in your peace. In Jesus name we pray, amen.

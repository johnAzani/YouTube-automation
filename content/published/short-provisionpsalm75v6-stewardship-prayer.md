While you wait for a manager to recognize your hard work today, hear God's word. For promotion cometh neither from the east, nor from the west, nor from the south. But God is the judge: he putteth down one, and setteth up another. Lord, we acknowledge that true advancement comes from Your sovereign hand alone. You see every unseen effort and every closed door. Break our reliance on human favor. Grant us quiet confidence in Your perfect timing, knowing that You elevate whom You will. We yield our career, our daily work, and our future unto Your faithful care. Subscribe to join us daily at The Provision Table.

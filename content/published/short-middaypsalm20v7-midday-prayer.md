You are staring at that bank balance right now, wondering how you will overcome. Scripture says, "Some trust in chariots, and some in horses: but we will remember the name of the LORD our God." Lord, when financial pressures or human plans fail to secure our hearts, reorient our spirit toward You. We lay down our reliance on self-made shields and temporary strength in this middle hour. Anchor our mind in Your steadfast presence, knowing Your name carries more power than any worldly strategy. Guard our decisions today with divine wisdom, grace, and quiet confidence.

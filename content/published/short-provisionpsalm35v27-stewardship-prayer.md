Right now, as you wonder how your provision will hold through this week, listen closely. Father, we come before Your throne acknowledging that every good gift comes directly from Your open hand. Your Word commands us with these exact truths: Let them shout for joy, and be glad, that favor my righteous cause: yea, let them say continually, Let the LORD be magnified, which hath pleasure in the prosperity of his servant. Lord, let our hearts take great joy in Your righteousness and trust in Your abundant care. Provide for our daily needs and anchor our souls in Your unfailing faithfulness today, in Jesus name, amen.

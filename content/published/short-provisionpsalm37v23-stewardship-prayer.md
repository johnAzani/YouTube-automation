Right now, before you step into that intimidating room or make that heavy financial choice, listen. Father, we anchor our uncertain steps in Your promises today, knowing You hold every practical detail of our lives. Your Word declares that the steps of a good man are ordered by the Lord, and He delighteth in his way. Lord, when sudden stumbles threaten our stability, remind us of Your faithful, rescuing strength. Though he fall, he shall not be utterly cast down, for the Lord upholdeth him with his hand. We look back across the years and rest in Your unchanging provision for every household need. I have been young, and now am old; yet have I not seen the righteous forsaken, nor his seed begging bread.

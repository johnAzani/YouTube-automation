You cannot see where this path turns. Scripture reminds us in Proverbs twenty, verse twenty-four: Man’s goings are of the LORD; how can a man then understand his own way? Heavenly Father, when our steps feel confused and our own plans make no sense, we yield our understanding to You. We admit that we cannot chart our own future or force our own outcomes. Order our steps according to Your purpose, and quiet our restless need to figure out every detail. Lead us through every uncertain hour into Your quiet peace. Amen. Teach us to trust, even when you cannot see where this path turns.

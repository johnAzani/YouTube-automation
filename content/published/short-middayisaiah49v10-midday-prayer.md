Take a quiet moment right now to pause whatever you are doing. Let the noise of the morning fade away and settle your heart in stillness. Listen to the promise written in Isaiah 49:10 for your soul today. They shall not hunger nor thirst, neither shall the heat nor sun smite them. For he that hath mercy on them shall lead them, even by the springs of water shall he guide them. Lord, we thank you for your gentle guidance in the middle of this busy day. Refresh our weary spirits and lead us beside still waters as we continue onward.

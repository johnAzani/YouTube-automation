Teach me to do thy will; for thou art my God: thy spirit is good; lead me into the land of uprightness. Lord, quiet the noise of my own desires so I might hear your gentle leading clearly. Remove the hesitation from my spirit and replace it with your profound sense of perfect peace. Guide my every step today into the upright path you have lovingly laid out before me.

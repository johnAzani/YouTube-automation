That rising fatigue pulling your focus away from your work deserves a moment of quiet today. Lord, help us to release every burden we carry into this noon hour. Wherefore seeing we also are compassed about with so great a cloud of witnesses, let us lay aside every weight, and the sin which doth so easily beset us, and let us run with patience the race that is set before us, Grant us the grace to keep walking when the path ahead feels steep and long. Looking unto Jesus the author and finisher of our faith; who for the joy that was set before him endured the cross, despising the shame, and is set down at the right hand of the throne of God. Direct our eyes back to Your strength as we finish this day. Subscribe to The Midday Watch for daily prayer.

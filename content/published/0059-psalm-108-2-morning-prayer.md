## Scene 1 — The Call to Awake

Awake, psaltery and harp: I myself will awake early. In this sacred hour of the morning, we find ourselves standing at the threshold of a new day, looking toward the light that breaks upon the world. Psalm 108, verse 2, calls us to a deliberate act of spiritual awakening. The Psalmist does not merely wake because the sun has risen; he commands his soul, his instruments, and his very being to come alive for the purpose of worship. Before the demands of the office, the urgency of the commute, or the weight of responsibility find us, we find the Lord. To "awake early" is a posture of intentionality. It is the refusal to let the day dictate our spirit; instead, we dictate the tone of the day through the language of prayer. We invite the presence of the Almighty into the quiet moments before the rush. We are here to direct our prayer, to look up, and to offer the first fruits of our thoughts to the Creator of the dawn.

## Scene 2 — Mercies New with the Rising Sun

Matthew Henry reminds us in his commentary that the mercies of God are new every morning, and because they are new, our songs of praise must be fresh. We are not living on the sustenance of yesterday; we are being provided for in this present, unfolding moment. There is a covenant faithfulness that sustains the sunrise, and that same faithfulness sustains the life of the believer. When we awake early, we are aligning ourselves with the rhythm of grace. We acknowledge that the darkness of yesterday — its failures, its unfinished business, its lingering questions — has been eclipsed by the light of a new opportunity. By waking to praise, we declare that God is the author of our days, and in His hands, the path ahead is being prepared with mercy. We step into this morning not by our own strength, but by the momentum of God’s unchanging love, which greets us with the morning dew.

## Scene 3 — Offering Thanks Among the People

The Psalmist declares that he will praise God among the people and sing praises among the nations. Our morning devotion is not merely a private, isolated act; it is the preparation for a life of witness. When we begin our day at the Altar, we carry the fragrance of that encounter into every meeting, every conversation, and every interaction we have with those around us. The praise we offer in the stillness becomes the strength we demonstrate in the noise of the marketplace. We are called to be people of gratitude in a world that often leans toward complaint. As you go into your day, remember that your life is a testament to the God who wakes you. By beginning with thanksgiving, you are choosing to view your neighbors, your colleagues, and your family through the lens of divine grace. You are entering your sphere of influence as a representative of the Light.

## Scene 4 — The Shield of Divine Favour

For thy mercy is great above the heavens, and thy truth reacheth unto the clouds. As we stand in the early light, we recognize the vastness of the God we serve. His mercy is not contained by our circumstances; it towers above them. When we feel overwhelmed by the complexities of the day ahead, we must remember that God’s truth and mercy are the canopy under which we walk. We are not unprotected. His favour is described in scripture as a shield, surrounding us as if with a wall of safety. This is not a promise of a life without challenges, but a promise of a Presence that exceeds the magnitude of every challenge. We walk out into this morning covered by the faithfulness of the Lord. Whatever decisions must be made, whatever paths must be traveled, we move forward with the confidence that God’s truth is our guide and His mercy is our defense.

## Scene 5 — Blessing Over Work and Home

We place the work of our hands under the shadow of the Almighty. Whatever you are called to do this day — be it labor, leadership, care for your family, or acts of service — let it be done as an offering. We pray for wisdom in our decisions, clarity in our speech, and patience in our trials. We speak a blessing over our homes, that they may be sanctuaries of peace and centers of light. We ask that the Lord would establish the work of our hands, making our efforts fruitful and our influence a blessing to those we encounter. May your family be touched by the grace you have cultivated this morning. May your home be a place where God’s presence is recognized and honored. We pray for protection for your travel, for the health of your loved ones, and for the strength to walk in integrity throughout every hour that remains until the sun sets.

## Scene 6 — The Sending Benediction

As the morning light grows stronger, we are sent out from this Altar with renewed strength. Like the eagle, we are refreshed, our vision cleared, and our spirits lifted by the communion we have shared. Do not carry the burdens of yesterday into the potential of this morning. You are empowered, you are called, and you are loved. Go forth with the assurance that He who began the day with you will surely walk with you through every step of the journey. Keep your heart attuned to His voice in the midst of the noise. Stay rooted in the truth you have declared. May the Lord bless you and keep you, may He make His face shine upon you and be gracious unto you throughout this entire day. You are fully equipped for every good work because you have first sought the face of the Living God.

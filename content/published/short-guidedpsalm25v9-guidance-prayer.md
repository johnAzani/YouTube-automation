If pride tells you to force your own answer today, surrender that urge right now. The Word of God promises in Psalm 25:9, The meek will he guide in judgment: and the meek will he teach his way. Heavenly Father, we lay down our stubborn self-reliance and yield our hearts to Your divine wisdom. Strip away our need to control every outcome, and give us the humility to be led by You. When the next step seems unclear, teach us Your holy way and quiet our anxious heart. Guide our judgment according to Your righteous truth. Subscribe to The Guided Path to seek God's wisdom daily.

When hard work yields no visible return, hear God's word in Psalm 67: Let the people praise thee, O God; let all the people praise thee. Then shall the earth yield her increase; and God, even our own God, shall bless us. Lord, we lift our empty hands to You today. Forgive us for measuring Your faithfulness only by our immediate harvest. We praise You before the yield appears. Open the doors no man can shut, and cause our quiet labors to bear fruit in Your time. Bless our work, our decisions, and our daily bread. Amen. When hard work yields no visible return.

## Scene 1 — Bringing the Decision to the Lord

Shew me thy ways, O LORD; teach me thy paths. Lead me in thy truth, and teach me: for thou art the God of my salvation; on thee do I wait all the day.

We bow before your presence, Almighty God, Creator of heaven and earth, acknowledging that in ourselves we do not possess the wisdom required for the crossroads before us. Lord, we come bringing our decisions, our perplexities, and the quiet burdens of our uncertainty. We do not lean upon our own understanding, for human insight is finite and easily clouded by fear, haste, or the desires of our own hearts. Instead, we look up to your holy hill, asking for your heavenly direction. 

As the psalmist David cried out from the depths of his own trials, so do our hearts echo his plea today: Shew me thy ways, O LORD; teach me thy paths. In every moment of transition, when the path ahead divides and the right choice is veiled in mist, we ask that you would make your way plain before us. Guide us not merely by circumstance, but by the steady, unchanging light of your holy word. Grant us a teachable spirit, O Lord. Remove from our minds the stubborn insistence that we must carve out our own destiny, and replace it with a holy dependence upon your sovereign grace. 

As Matthew Henry observes in his commentary, those who truly desire to be directed by God must first be brought to see their own ignorance and need of instruction. We confess that we do not know what tomorrow holds, nor do we know the full consequences of the choices we face today. But we know you, and we trust your heart. You are the God of our salvation, and because you have redeemed us, we have the confidence to cast our anxieties upon you. We wait upon you, Lord — not with restless impatience, but with the quiet assurance that you are faithful to direct the steps of those who look to you alone.

## Scene 2 — Asking God for Wisdom

If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not; and it shall be given him. But let him ask in faith, nothing wavering. For he that wavereth is like a wave of the sea driven with the wind and tossed.

Father, we come acknowledging our lack of wisdom. We confess that in moments of pressure, we are prone to seek quick answers rather than enduring truth. We thank you for the generous promise recorded by your servant James, assuring us that when we lack wisdom, we may ask of you — the God who gives liberally and does not upbraid us for our slowness of heart. You do not mock our confusion, Lord; rather, you invite us to draw near with confidence to your throne of grace to find help in time of need.

Grant us that steadfast faith which refuses to waver amidst the shifting winds of circumstance. When conflicting opinions press in upon us, and when the noise of the world threatens to drown out the still, small voice of your Spirit, anchor our souls in your eternal truth. Charles Spurgeon reminds us in his exposition of the Psalms that waiting upon God is an active posture of the soul, wherein the believer surrenders self-will and listens intently for the divine command. We lay down our anxieties, our fears of making a misstep, and our desire for guaranteed outcomes, resting entirely in your generous character. 

You do not withhold your guidance from those who seek to honor your name. Therefore, we ask not for wealth, nor for ease, nor for the removal of every trial, but simply for the wisdom to discern your good and acceptable and perfect will. Teach us to weigh our choices not by human standards of success, but by what is pure, peaceable, gentle, and full of mercy. Cultivate within us a deep discernment that recognizes the subtle counterfeit paths of this world and resolutely chooses the narrow way of obedience.

## Scene 3 — The Lamp of the Word

Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path. I have sworn, and I will perform it, that I will keep thy righteous judgments.

Lord God, we praise you for providing a sure light in a dark and confusing world. Your holy word is not merely a historical record, but a living and active lamp unto our feet. We acknowledge that you seldom flash a blinding light across the entire landscape of our future; instead, you graciously provide just enough light for the immediate step before us. For this, we are profoundly grateful, for it compels us to walk closely with you, trusting your guidance one day at a time.

We do not presume to know the precise blueprint of our lives, nor do we demand that you reveal every turn of the road before we take our next breath. We are content to walk where your word illuminates, knowing that your commandments are right and your statutes are sure. When we feel the temptation to forge ahead in our own strength or to lag behind out of fear, revive our hearts according to your lovingkindness. Let your truth govern our thoughts, purify our motivations, and steady our emotions.

As we meditate upon the scriptures day and night, let your precepts shape our discernment. Teach us to recognize the voice of the Good Shepherd, who calls His own sheep by name and leads them out. We recognize that true guidance never contradicts the written word of God. Therefore, we test every impression, every door that opens or closes, and every counsel we receive against the unchanging standard of the Holy Bible. Keep us from deception, Lord, and anchor our wandering feet firmly upon the rock of your truth.

## Scene 4 — Wise Counsel & Tested Motives

Where no counsel is, the fall is: but in the multitude of counsellors there is safety. Search me, O God, and know my heart: try me, and know my thoughts: And see if there be any wicked way in me, and lead me in the way everlasting.

Father, we recognize that while you guide us directly by your Spirit and your word, you also ordain means of safety through the body of Christ and the wisdom of godly counsel. We ask that you would surround us with mature believers who speak the truth in love, who can help us discern our blind spots, and who point us back to Christ when our judgment falters. We acknowledge that prayer walks hand in hand with practical diligence, and we remember that seeking spiritual guidance never replaces the need for wise, sober, and diligent counsel in our daily affairs. 

Moreover, we humbly submit our own hearts to your searching gaze. Search us, O God, and know our hearts today. Try us and know our thoughts, for we know how deceitful the human heart can be. Sometimes we convince ourselves that we are seeking your will when we are merely seeking our own comfort, our own ambition, or our own vindication. Reveal any hidden motives, any unconfessed stubbornness, or any secret resistance to your authority. 

If there is any wicked way in us — any cherished sin or self-willed agenda that hinders our fellowship with you — expose it by your light and cleanse us by the blood of the Lamb. We do not ask for your guidance so that we might use it for our own selfish ends; we ask so that we might walk in the way everlasting. Purify our desires until our greatest delight is to see your name glorified through our choices, whether those choices lead through quiet green pastures or through valleys of testing and refinement.

## Scene 5 — Waiting With Patience & Ordered Steps

The steps of a good man are ordered by the LORD: and he delighteth in his way. Though he fall, he shall not be utterly cast down: for the LORD upholdeth him with his hand.

Lord, we confess that waiting is often the hardest part of seeking your guidance. We live in a culture that demands instant answers and immediate resolution, yet your timing is designed to produce steadfast endurance within us. Grant us the grace to wait upon your timing without growing weary or losing heart. When the answer seems delayed and the silence feels heavy, remind us that waiting upon the Lord is never wasted time; it is the crucible in which faith is refined and trust is deepened.

We take immense comfort in the promise that the steps of a righteous person are ordered by you, Lord. Even when we stumble through our own weakness or misjudgment, you do not abandon us to our failures. Your hand is strong to uphold us, lifting us out of the mire and setting our feet upon solid ground once more. We rest in the assurance that our lives are securely held in your hands, and that no mistake can ultimately thwart your ultimate purposes for those who love you and are called according to your purpose.

Teach us to find our joy not in the destination we desire, but in the companionship of your presence along the journey. Whether you lead us into a season of quiet preparation, into a time of testing, or across a threshold of new responsibility, let your peace guard our hearts and minds. We cast off all paralyzing fear of making the wrong turn, knowing that your sovereign grace is greater than our human frailty. You are the God who directs our paths, and we entrust our entire future — known and unknown — to your unfailing care.

## Scene 6 — Committing the Way & Closing Benediction

Commit thy works unto the LORD, and thy thoughts shall be established. And the LORD shall guide thee continually, and satisfy thy soul in drought, and make fat thy bones: and thou shalt be like a watered garden, and like a spring of water, whose waters fail not.

Gracious God, we now formally commit our works, our plans, our relationships, and our decisions into your hands. We roll the weight of our anxieties away from our own shoulders and place them squarely upon yours. Establish our thoughts, Lord, and grant us a settled peace that passes all human understanding. We know that you will guide us continually, providing wisdom for today's decisions and grace for tomorrow's challenges.

We look forward with quiet confidence, knowing that even in dry and thirsty seasons, your Spirit will satisfy our souls. You are the God who makes a way in the wilderness and rivers in the desert. We go forth from this time of prayer not because we have all the answers mapped out, but because we know the One who holds the future. May our lives reflect the deep serenity of those who are led by the Good Shepherd.

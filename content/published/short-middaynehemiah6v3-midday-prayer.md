Demands are pulling you away from your work. Every interruption asks you to step away from what matters. Nehemiah answered his detractors with these words: And I sent messengers unto them, saying, I am doing a great work, so that I cannot come down: why should the work cease, whilst I leave it, and come down to you? Lord, give us wisdom to recognize what is essential right now. Protect our minds from trivial noise that seeks to derail our purpose. Teach us to say no to distractions so your calling stands firm. Strengthen our hands to build, refusing to abandon the wall. Amen. Demands pull you away from your work.

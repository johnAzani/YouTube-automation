That heavy choice weighing on your mind is why you are meant to stay here. Lord, we arrive at this hour with our hands empty and our strength fully spent. O our God, wilt thou not judge them? for we have no might against this great company that cometh against us; neither know we what to do: but our eyes are upon thee. Grant us the quiet stillness to wait while You move in ways we cannot see. Replace our frantic worry with the steady peace of knowing You are already acting today. May our hearts remain fixed on Your face as the hours pass, in Jesus' name. Subscribe.

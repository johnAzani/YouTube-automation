Thus saith the LORD, thy Redeemer, the Holy One of Israel; I am the LORD thy God which teacheth thee to profit, which leadeth thee by the way that thou shouldest go. Father, please steady the heart that feels pulled in too many directions by worldly noise. We ask that You reveal the path prepared for us, moving us away from hesitation. Grant us the quiet confidence to follow Your lead even when the destination is hidden. May Your wisdom anchor every choice we make this day. Amen.

Be still, and know that I am God: I will be exalted among the heathen, I will be exalted in the earth. Lord, teach me to trade this restless striving for the perfect peace of your presence. Calm my racing thoughts so that I might perceive your sovereign hand in every detail. May your glory be the only thing that matters in my work and my rest.

Pause what you are doing right now and rest your heart in this quiet moment. At noon, when the sun is highest and the workday feels heavy, receive the promise of Isaiah 32 verse 2: Each will be like a hiding place from the wind, a shelter from the storm, like streams of water in a dry place, like the shade of a great rock in a weary land. Lord, be our hiding place today. Cool the heat of our daily burdens and refresh our weary souls with Your peace. Keep us safe beneath the shadow of Your wings. Amen.

If the overwhelming pressure of your midday workload is making you feel completely inadequate, listen. For they all made us afraid, saying, Their hands shall be weakened from the work, that it be not done. Now therefore, O God, strengthen my hands. May He lift the intimidation off your assignment and grant you quiet courage for the work ahead. Subscribe to The Midday Watch and pause with us again tomorrow.

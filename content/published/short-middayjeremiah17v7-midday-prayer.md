That relentless pressure building in your chest right now demands your attention. Let us turn our minds to the unchanging promise found in scripture. Blessed is the man that trusteth in the LORD, and whose hope the LORD is. For he shall be as a tree planted by the waters, and that spreadeth out her roots by the river, and shall not see when heat cometh, but her leaf shall be green; and shall not be careful in the year of drought, neither shall cease from yielding fruit. Lord, anchor our lives deeply in your enduring grace during this midday hour. Grant us steadfast trust when dry seasons surround us.

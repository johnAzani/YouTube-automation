## Scene 1 — The Sunrise Hook and Directing the Prayer

Our voice shall be heard in the morning, O Lord, and in the morning will we direct our prayer unto Thee and will look up. Before the first email is opened, before the first demand of the workplace knocks upon the door of our thoughts, before the highway fills with noise and the anxiety of the day gathers its weight, we have come to this quiet place. This is The Morning Altar. The sky above is painted with the pale, tender gold of a new dawn, testifying of a faithfulness that is fresh every single sunrise. We bow our hearts this morning before the living God, acknowledging that the very breath in our lungs is a gift from His hand. The psalmist wrote in Psalm 88 verse 13, Unto thee have I cried, O Lord; and in the morning shall my prayer prevent thee. To prevent God in the morning does not mean to hinder Him; it means to arrive before Him, to be early at the throne, to meet the rising sun with the rising incense of intercession. We do not wait until we are overwhelmed by the afternoon to seek our refuge. We take the ground at daybreak. We lay hold of the horns of the altar before the battle of the day can even be joined. Oh Lord God of our salvation, we lift our eyes to the hills from whence cometh our help. Our help cometh from the Lord, which made heaven and earth. As the morning light touches the rooftops and filters through the trees, let Your Holy Spirit touch every hidden corner of our souls. We consecrate this hour, this minute, this breath to Your glory. We refuse to let the world dictate our mood, our pace, or our trust today. We take our stand upon Your eternal word, secure in the knowledge that You hear the voice of our cry at the very break of day.

## Scene 2 — New Mercies and Faithfulness at Dawn

Great is Thy faithfulness, O God our Father, because morning by morning new mercies we see. All we have needed Thy hand hath provided. As Matthew Henry observes in his commentary on the tender mercies of God, the daily recurring light of the sun is a standing emblem of the ongoing covenant of grace, which never fails and never grows old. Yesterday is gone, buried in the grace of forgiveness and the faithfulness of the Almighty. Whatever mistakes were made, whatever burdens were carried, whatever weariness settled into our bones as the sun set last night, those things do not govern this new day. His compassions fail not; they are new every morning. Think upon this glorious theological reality: the God who upholds the stars and commands the morning has also ordained a specific measure of grace for this exact day of your life. Not yesterday's grace, but fresh manna for today. We do not need to figure out next week, nor do we need to carry the unfinished tasks of last week into this sunrise. We receive the daily bread of His presence right now. Lord, we confess that our hearts can so easily become entangled in the weeds of worry. We fret over tomorrow's provisions, tomorrow's health, tomorrow's conflicts. But Your word commands us to seek first the kingdom of God and His righteousness, knowing that all these things shall be added unto us. So we cast off the heavy garments of yesterday's grief and put on the garments of praise. We receive the clean slate of this morning. We thank You that Your mercies are not exhausted by our failures, nor diminished by our doubts. They rise with the sun, vast, steady, and unfailing. Let our hearts respond today with a steadfast trust that matches the reliability of Your dawn.

## Scene 3 — Trust and Guidance for the Decisions Ahead

Lord, we commit our ways unto Thee this morning, trusting that Thou shalt bring them to pass. We acknowledge that we do not know what a single hour of this day may bring forth. We do not possess the wisdom to navigate every intersection, every conversation, every professional challenge, or every family dynamic waiting for us. Therefore, we deliberately renounce our own self-reliance. We lean not unto our own understanding. In all our ways we acknowledge Thee, and we ask for the clear, unmistakable guidance of Your Holy Spirit. When we face decisions at our place of work, grant us discernment that cuts through confusion. When we encounter difficult people, fill our mouths with grace and season our responses with salt. When the path divides and we cannot see which way is best, let our spiritual ears hear a voice behind us saying, This is the way, walk ye in it. The psalmist's cry in the morning is the cry of a dependent child reaching for the hand of a loving Father. We do not walk alone today. The Creator of the ends of the earth walks with us, goes before us, and is our rear guard. We pray for integrity in our private thoughts and our public actions. Let our work today be an act of worship. Whether we labor at a desk, care for children in a home, travel on the highway, or minister to those in need, let every action be done heartily as unto the Lord and not unto men. We ask for the spirit of wisdom and revelation in the knowledge of Him, that the eyes of our understanding being enlightened, we may know what is the hope of His calling. Direct our steps, O Lord, and keep our feet from falling.

## Scene 4 — Favour, Shield, and Protection for the Day

Surely goodness and mercy shall follow us all the days of our life, and we shall dwell in the house of the Lord forever. As we step out into the rushing current of this world, we plead the blood of Jesus over our minds, our bodies, our families, and our property. We take up the whole armour of God, that we may be able to withstand in the evil day. We put on the breastplate of righteousness, shod our feet with the preparation of the gospel of peace, and above all, take the shield of faith, wherewith we shall be able to quench all the fiery darts of the wicked. Lord, let Your favour surround us as with a shield. We are acutely aware that we live in a fallen and broken world, where anxieties whisper and unforeseen trials can rise like sudden storms. But greater is He that is in us than he that is in the world. We declare that no weapon formed against us shall prosper, and every tongue that rises against us in judgment shall be condemned. This is the heritage of the servants of the Lord. We pray for divine protection on our loved ones. Protect our spouses, our children, our parents, and our friends as they go about their daily routines. Keep them from harm, deliver them from evil, and let Your angels encamp around about them. When we are tempted to fear, remind us of Your sovereign power. You hold the ocean in the hollow of Your hand. You number the very hairs of our head. Not a sparrow falls to the ground without our Father knowing. Therefore, we refuse to yield to panic or dread. We place our complete confidence in the God who hears our morning cry and answers with deliverance and peace.

## Scene 5 — Blessing Over Work, Family, and Home

And let the beauty of the Lord our God be upon us, and establish Thou the work of our hands upon us; yea, the work of our hands establish Thou it. Father, we speak a supernatural blessing over our homes and the environments we are about to enter. Let Your peace rule in our houses. Let every word spoken within our walls be seasoned with kindness and honor. We pray for our places of employment, our businesses, our schools, and our communities. Where there is strife, let us be instruments of Your reconciliation. Where there is discouragement, let us be bearers of Your light. We ask You to prosper the legitimate labor of our hands today. Remove frustration and barrenness from our toil, and grant us fruitfulness that honors Your name. We pray for those whom we will meet today — the cashier, the coworker, the stranger on the street, the neighbor across the fence. Open our spiritual eyes to see divine appointments where we can display the love of Christ in a tangible way. Let our lives be a living epistle, read and known of all men. Pour out Your Spirit upon our families. Heal broken relationships, mend fractured trust, and draw every member of our households closer to Your heart. We claim the ancient priestly blessing over every listener right now: The Lord bless thee, and keep thee: The Lord make His face shine upon thee, and be gracious unto thee: The Lord lift up His countenance upon thee, and give thee peace. May this divine benediction rest upon our going out and our coming in, from this time forth and even forevermore.

## Scene 6 — The Sending Benediction

They that wait upon the Lord shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles; they shall run, and not be weary; and they shall walk, and not faint. Our souls are anchored, our spirits are refreshed, and our faces are set like flint toward the mercies of this new day. We have sought the Lord in the morning, and He has heard our voice out of His holy hill. We do not go forth in our own strength, but in the mighty name of Jesus Christ, the Alpha and the Omega, the first and the last. Go into your day with your head held high, not in human pride, but in holy confidence because you belong to the King of Kings. Let His joy be your strength when the hours grow long. Let His peace guard your heart when the noise grows loud. Remember that every sunrise is a fresh reminder that God's covenant with you is unbreakable.

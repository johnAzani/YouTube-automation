Scripture reminds us in Psalm 73:26, 'My flesh and my heart faileth: but God is the strength of my heart, and my portion for ever.' When our human power fails us, become our strength, our peace, and our eternal portion right here where we sit. Renew our weary spirits as we pause before You now.

The doubt did not leave your pillow. Before your feet touch the cold floor, turn your heart toward the God who kept you through the night. Take this ancient resolve into your morning: I will remember the works of the LORD: surely I will remember thy wonders of old. Lord, my soul is quick to fear when dawn brings unanswered questions. Anchor my racing thoughts in the quiet proof of Your enduring faithfulness. You have carried me through every trial before, and You will not abandon me in the hours ahead. Lead me through this day in Your unbroken peace, even when doubt clings close. Amen.

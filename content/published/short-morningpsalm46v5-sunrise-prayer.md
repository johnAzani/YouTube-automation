You are currently holding onto a heavy worry that feels far too large to carry alone. God is in the midst of her; she shall not be moved: God shall help her, and that right early. Lord, anchor my heart in this promise as the light grows brighter in my room today. Let your presence move through the anxious thoughts that tried to greet me upon waking up. Grant me the unshakeable stillness that comes only from knowing you are truly dwelling within me. Help me walk forward now with a steady spirit because you are my constant, present help.

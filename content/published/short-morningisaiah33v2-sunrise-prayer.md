As the morning sun begins to break, take a deep breath and open your heart to this new day. In the quiet light of dawn, turn your thoughts to God and hear His word from Isaiah 33:2: O Lord, be gracious to us; we have waited for you. Be our arm every morning, our salvation in the time of trouble. Father, we present ourselves at Your morning altar, placing every fear and responsibility into Your strong hands. Strengthen our arms for today, guide our steps in peace, and keep our minds anchored in Your unfailing love. May Your grace cover our households and carry us through every trial we encounter before dusk. Subscribe to set your altar each morning and walk in His strength every day.

That motive you keep second-guessing is heavy. Psalm 139:23-24 tells us: Search me, O God, and know my heart: try me, and know my thoughts: And see if there be any wicked way in me, and lead me in the way everlasting. Lord, search the motives we try to hide even from ourselves. We want our choices to honor You, not our own pride. Expose every hidden self-deception in this heart today. Cleanse our restless thoughts and align our desires with Your wisdom. Lead our footsteps into the way everlasting, step by quiet step. Amen. That motive you second-guessed belongs in His hands.

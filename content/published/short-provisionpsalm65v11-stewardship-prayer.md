If you are staring at an unpaid bill tonight, commit your financial needs to God. Scripture promises, Thou crownest the year with thy goodness; and thy paths drop fatness. Heavenly Father, we thank You that Your sovereign abundance overflows into every dry season of our lives. You see our hard labor, our silent worries, and every practical requirement before us right now. Open doors of daily provision and let Your divine goodness crown our work with true fruitfulness. Establish the labor of our hands as we yield our decisions and trust in Your unfailing care. In Jesus' name, Amen. Subscribe to walk in God's daily provision.

You still carry the weight of wasted years. Hear the promise of the Lord in Joel chapter two. And I will restore to you the years that the locust hath eaten, the cankerworm, and the caterpiller, and the palmerworm, my great army which I sent among you. And ye shall eat in plenty, and be satisfied, and praise the name of the LORD your God, that hath dealt wondrously with you: and my people shall never be ashamed. Lord, restore what seemed lost to fruitless struggle. Fill our hands with Your daily provision. Deal wondrously with our labor, that we praise Your name. Amen. You carry wasted years no more.

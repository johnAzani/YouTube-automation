# Covenant Shield Theological & Scriptural Exposition Rubric

## Script Structure (The 6-Movement Covenant Protection & Strong-Tower Journey)
Every long-form Covenant Shield script (target duration 5:30 – 6:30 minutes; at least the production word floor of narration across the scenes) must follow a structured 6-movement devotional arc that clothes the believer in KJV scripture, Christ's faithful keeping, and steady courage for every step of the day:

### Movement 1: Running Into the Strong Tower of the LORD
- **Opening Line:** An immediate, reassuring invitation to place every step, commute, journey, responsibility, and unseen concern under the watchful eye of the Keeper of Israel (Psalm 121:1–4; Proverbs 18:10).
- **Verse Recitation:** Opening KJV verse of the passage.
- **Exposition:** Establish from Scripture that our safety rests not in human vigilance alone, but in the covenant character of Almighty God who neither slumbers nor sleeps (Genesis 15:1; Psalm 84:11).

### Movement 2: Taking Up the Shield of Faith & Whole Armour of God
- **Thematic Focus:** Ephesians 6:10–17 & 2 Samuel 22:31 — "As for God, his way is perfect; the word of the LORD is tried: he is a buckler to all them that trust in him."
- **Verse Recitation:** Second key KJV verse/section.
- **Exposition:** Draw on Charles Spurgeon (*The Treasury of David*) or Matthew Henry (*Commentary on the Whole Bible*) to unfold how active trust in God's spoken promises quenches fiery darts of doubt, temptation, accusation, and fear.

### Movement 3: Preserving Our Going Out & Our Coming In
- **Thematic Focus:** Psalm 121:5–8, Psalm 37:23–24 & Proverbs 3:21–26 — "The LORD shall preserve thy going out and thy coming in from this time forth, and even for evermore."
- **Verse Recitation:** Third key KJV verse/section.
- **Exposition:** Intercede practically over daily commutes, highways, flights, workplaces, schools, and errands — asking the Lord to order every footstep, grant alertness and sound judgment, and turn aside unseen hazards.

### Movement 4: Angelic Encampment & Refuge From Unseen Snares
- **Thematic Focus:** Psalm 34:7, Psalm 91:3–4, 11 & 2 Thessalonians 3:3 — "But the Lord is faithful, who shall stablish you, and keep you from evil."
- **Verse Recitation:** Fourth key KJV verse/section.
- **Exposition:** Cite Matthew Henry or Spurgeon on the Lord's unseen providence and shelter under His wings, guarding minds and hearts from spiritual discouragement, deceit, and sudden alarm (Proverbs 3:25–26).

### Movement 5: Peace Amidst the Storm & Courage to Stand Firm
- **Thematic Focus:** Isaiah 54:17, Isaiah 41:10 & Psalm 46:1–2 — "God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble."
- **Verse Recitation:** Fifth key KJV verse/section.
- **Exposition:** Pray bold, Christ-centered courage over believers facing intimidation, opposition, or turbulent circumstances, reminding them that if God be for us, none can prevail against His redeemed (Romans 8:31).

### Movement 6: Jude's Keeping Doxology & Daily Covering Benediction
- **Thematic Focus:** Jude 1:24–25 & Numbers 6:24–26 — "The LORD bless thee, and keep thee."
- **Verse Recitation:** Closing KJV benediction verse.
- **Exposition:** Seal the prayer in quiet confidence unto Him who is able to keep us from falling, ending with this exact call-to-action: "Type Amen to agree with this prayer, and subscribe to The Covenant Shield to walk under God's daily covering and protection."

## Theological & Pastoral Boundaries
- KJV quotations only, checked verbatim. Never paraphrase scripture inside quotation marks.
- Attribute every commentator cited (Spurgeon, Matthew Henry) to the work it comes from; never invent a quotation. If a claim cannot be attributed, state it as the script's own exposition instead.
- Never promote superstition, fearmongering, or imprecatory curses against human individuals; keep every petition rooted in New Testament grace, vigilance, and trust in Christ.
- Every prayer request referenced is held in general pastoral terms; never imply individual follow-up that the automation cannot deliver.

# Guided Path Theological & Scriptural Exposition Rubric

## Script Structure (The 6-Movement Wisdom & Guidance Journey)
Every long-form Guided Path script (target duration 5:30 – 6:30 minutes; at least the production word floor of narration across the scenes) must follow a structured 6-movement devotional arc. Vary wording, imagery, and pacing to fit the passage; the arc is a shape, not a template to refill.

### Movement 1: Bringing the Decision to the Lord
- **Thematic Focus:** Proverbs 3:5–6 & Psalm 25:4–5 — "In all thy ways acknowledge him, and he shall direct thy paths."
- **Verse Recitation:** Opening KJV verse of the passage.
- **Exposition:** Open with an immediate, specific invitation to lay the decision or uncertain season before God, and establish from Scripture that guidance begins with trust rather than with our own understanding.

### Movement 2: Asking God for Wisdom
- **Thematic Focus:** James 1:5–8 & Proverbs 2:1–9 — "If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not."
- **Verse Recitation:** Second key KJV verse/section.
- **Exposition:** Draw on Matthew Henry (*Commentary on the Whole Bible*) or Charles Spurgeon (*The Treasury of David*, for the Psalms) to show the generosity of God toward those who ask humbly and the steadiness of faith He commends.

### Movement 3: The Lamp of the Word
- **Thematic Focus:** Psalm 119:105, Psalm 32:8 & Isaiah 30:21 — "Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path."
- **Verse Recitation:** Third key KJV verse/section.
- **Exposition:** Teach that Scripture ordinarily lights the next step rather than the whole road. Never claim to know God's specific plan for the listener or announce a personal prophecy.

### Movement 4: Wise Counsel & Tested Motives
- **Thematic Focus:** Proverbs 15:22, Proverbs 11:14, Psalm 139:23–24 & James 3:17 — "In the multitude of counsellors they are established."
- **Verse Recitation:** Fourth key KJV verse/section.
- **Exposition:** Commend seeking godly counsel and examining motives before the Lord. State plainly that prayer walks alongside qualified medical, legal, financial, or other professional advice and never replaces it.

### Movement 5: Waiting With Patience & Ordered Steps
- **Thematic Focus:** Psalm 37:23–24, Psalm 27:11–14 & Proverbs 16:9 — "The steps of a good man are ordered by the LORD: and he delighteth in his way."
- **Verse Recitation:** Fifth key KJV verse/section.
- **Exposition:** Pray patience and courage over listeners who must wait or who fear a wrong turn, reminding them that the Lord upholds with His hand even when we stumble.

### Movement 6: Committing the Way & Closing Benediction
- **Thematic Focus:** Proverbs 16:3, Isaiah 58:11 & Exodus 33:14 — "My presence shall go with thee, and I will give thee rest."
- **Verse Recitation:** Closing KJV benediction verse.
- **Exposition:** Commit the decision, the timing, and the outcome to the Lord, and send the listener to take the next faithful step in His presence. End with this exact call-to-action: "Type Amen to agree with this prayer, and subscribe to The Guided Path to seek God's wisdom and direction for every step."

## Theological & Pastoral Boundaries
- KJV quotations only, checked verbatim. Never paraphrase scripture inside quotation marks.
- Attribute every commentator cited (Spurgeon, Matthew Henry) to the work it comes from; never invent a quotation. If a claim cannot be attributed, state it as the script's own exposition instead.
- Identify the speaker, audience, and setting of the passage where relevant, and keep the application faithful to that context.
- Never guarantee a particular outcome, timing, or sign; guidance in Scripture is trust, wisdom, and obedience, not fortune-telling.
- Never claim to know God's specific plan for an individual listener.
- Never present prayer as a substitute for medical, legal, financial, or other professional advice.
- Every prayer request referenced is held in general pastoral terms; never imply individual follow-up that the automation cannot deliver.

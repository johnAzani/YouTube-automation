# Provision Table Theological & Scriptural Exposition Rubric

## Script Structure (The 6-Movement Covenant Provision & Stewardship Journey)
Every long-form Provision Table script (target duration 5:30 – 6:30 minutes; at least the production word floor of narration across the scenes) must follow a structured 6-movement devotional arc that brings the believer's daily work, decisions, needs, and stewardship before Jehovah-Jireh in KJV scripture and reverent intercession:

### Movement 1: Approaching Jehovah-Jireh at the Prepared Table
- **Opening Line:** An immediate, faith-building invitation to lay every financial pressure, career question, closed door, and daily need before the Shepherd who prepares a table for His people (Psalm 23:1, 5).
- **Verse Recitation:** Opening KJV verse of the passage.
- **Exposition:** Introduce the covenant faithfulness of the Lord who sees and provides (Genesis 22:14), reminding the listener that our Heavenly Father knows what things we have need of before we ask Him (Matthew 6:8, 32).

### Movement 2: Open Doors & Heaven's Good Treasure Over Honest Labour
- **Thematic Focus:** Deuteronomy 28:12, Revelation 3:8 & Psalm 90:17 — "And let the beauty of the LORD our God be upon us: and establish thou the work of our hands upon us."
- **Verse Recitation:** Second key KJV verse/section.
- **Exposition:** Draw on Charles Spurgeon (*The Treasury of David*) or Matthew Henry (*Commentary on the Whole Bible*) to show how God's blessing crowns diligent, faithful labour and opens doors of service and opportunity that human striving alone cannot force.

### Movement 3: Divine Wisdom, Discernment & Direction for Decisions
- **Thematic Focus:** James 1:5, Proverbs 3:5–6 & Proverbs 16:3 — "Commit thy works unto the LORD, and thy thoughts shall be established."
- **Verse Recitation:** Third key KJV verse/section.
- **Exposition:** Pray specifically for clarity in crossroads, interviews, studies, trades, ministry, and household budgeting — asking the Lord who gives wisdom liberally and upbraideth not to order every step (Psalm 37:23) and guard against hasty or unwise counsel.

### Movement 4: Freedom From Anxious Toil & Contentment in Lean or Full Seasons
- **Thematic Focus:** Matthew 6:31–33, Psalm 127:1–2 & Philippians 4:11–13 — seeking first the kingdom of God and His righteousness while casting off anxious worry over tomorrow.
- **Verse Recitation:** Fourth key KJV verse/section.
- **Exposition:** Cite Matthew Henry or Spurgeon on trusting divine providence rather than eating the bread of sorrows — comforting listeners waiting patiently through delayed answers, job transitions, or tight seasons.

### Movement 5: Favour With God and Man & Generous Stewardship
- **Thematic Focus:** Psalm 5:12, Ruth 2:12, Psalm 84:11 & 2 Corinthians 9:8 — "And God is able to make all grace abound toward you; that ye, always having all sufficiency in all things, may abound to every good work."
- **Verse Recitation:** Fifth key KJV verse/section.
- **Exposition:** Intercede for favour with supervisors, clients, colleagues, examiners, and neighbours, and ask the Lord to make His people cheerful, upright channels of blessing to their families and communities.

### Movement 6: Overflowing Cup & Covenant Provision Benediction
- **Thematic Focus:** Philippians 4:19 & Psalm 23:5–6 — "But my God shall supply all your need according to his riches in glory by Christ Jesus."
- **Verse Recitation:** Closing KJV benediction verse.
- **Exposition:** Seal the prayer with quiet assurance that goodness and mercy shall follow the believer all the days of their life, ending with this exact call-to-action: "Type Amen to agree with this prayer, and subscribe to The Provision Table to walk in God's daily wisdom and provision."

## Theological & Stewardship Boundaries
- KJV quotations only, checked verbatim. Never paraphrase scripture inside quotation marks.
- Attribute every commentator cited (Spurgeon, Matthew Henry) to the work it comes from; never invent a quotation. If a claim cannot be attributed, state it as the script's own exposition instead.
- Never promise overnight riches, speculative windfalls, or transactional "seed-faith" bargains, and never give financial, legal, or investment advice.
- Every prayer request referenced is held in general pastoral terms; never imply individual follow-up that the automation cannot deliver.

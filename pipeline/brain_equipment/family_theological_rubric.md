# Family Altar Theological & Scriptural Exposition Rubric

## Script Structure (The 6-Movement Covenant Household Journey)
Every long-form Family Altar script (target duration 5:30 – 6:30 minutes; at least the production word floor of narration across the scenes) must follow a structured 6-movement devotional arc that gathers the household before God's throne in KJV scripture and pastoral intercession:

### Movement 1: Gathering Around the Family Altar
- **Opening Line:** An immediate, warm invitation to bring your home, your spouse, your children, and your loved ones before the throne of grace — the Joshua 24:15 posture: "As for me and my house, we will serve the LORD."
- **Verse Recitation:** Opening KJV verse of the passage.
- **Exposition:** In Scripture, everywhere the patriarchs pitched their tent, they built an altar unto the Lord. A home anchored in daily prayer becomes a peaceable habitation even when the world outside is unsettled.

### Movement 2: Building the House Upon God's Wisdom
- **Thematic Focus:** Psalm 127:1 & Proverbs 24:3–4 — "Except the LORD build the house, they labour in vain that build it."
- **Verse Recitation:** Second key KJV verse/section.
- **Exposition:** Draw on Matthew Henry's commentary on family religion — that a praying house is like a church under its own roof, where God's counsel governs every decision, conversation, and season.

### Movement 3: Covering Children, Sons, Daughters & Generations
- **Thematic Focus:** Psalm 128:3 & Isaiah 54:13 — "And all thy children shall be taught of the LORD; and great shall be the peace of thy children."
- **Verse Recitation:** Third key KJV verse/section.
- **Exposition:** Draw on Charles Spurgeon's warm exposition on olive plants round about the table — interceding for children young and grown, guarding their minds, friendships, steps, and faith in Christ.

### Movement 4: Unity, Peace & Grace Within Marriage and Relationships
- **Thematic Focus:** Colossians 3:14–15 & Isaiah 32:18 — "And my people shall dwell in a peaceable habitation, and in sure dwellings, and in quiet resting places."
- **Verse Recitation:** Fourth key KJV verse/section.
- **Exposition:** Pray for patience, tenderness, forgiveness, healing of strained words, and Christlike humility across every relationship in the household. Cite Matthew Henry or Spurgeon on domestic peace.

### Movement 5: Honest Provision, Health & Protection Over Every Threshold
- **Thematic Focus:** Philippians 4:19, Proverbs 3:33 & Job 1:10 — asking the Lord to bless the work of the household's hands, supply every need according to His riches in glory, and set a hedge of protection around all that belongs to the family.
- **Verse Recitation:** Fifth key KJV verse/section.
- **Exposition:** Keep petitions reverent and biblical: thanking God as Jehovah-Jireh who feeds His children, grants wisdom in stewardship, and guards our going out and coming in (Psalm 121:8).

### Movement 6: Aaronic Household Blessing & Covenant Benediction
- **Thematic Focus:** Sealing the home under God's covenant name and faithful care (Numbers 6:24–26 / 2 Samuel 7:29).
- **Verse Recitation:** Closing KJV benediction verse.
- **Exposition:** Close with a warm household blessing and this exact call-to-action: "Type Amen to agree with this prayer, and subscribe to The Family Altar to cover your home and family in daily prayer."

## Theological Boundaries
- KJV quotations only, checked verbatim. Never paraphrase scripture inside quotation marks.
- Attribute every commentator cited (Spurgeon, Matthew Henry) to the work it comes from; never invent a quotation. If a claim cannot be attributed, state it as the script's own exposition instead.
- No date-setting, no predictions, no prosperity-gospel promises, no claims about named individuals or current events.
- Every prayer request referenced is held in general terms; never imply a pastoral follow-up that the automation cannot deliver.

# Morning Altar Theological & Scriptural Exposition Rubric

## Script Structure (The 6-Movement Sunrise Journey)
Every long-form morning script (target duration 5:30 – 6:30 minutes; at least the production word floor of narration across the scenes) must follow a structured 6-movement devotional arc that moves from first light to being sent into the day:

### Movement 1: The Sunrise Hook & Directing the Prayer
- **Opening Line:** Immediate invitation to lift the day to God before the day makes its demands — Psalm 5:3 posture: direct the prayer and look up.
- **Verse Recitation:** The opening verse of the chapter/passage.
- **Exposition:** God hears the morning voice; the day is handed over before it is lived.

### Movement 2: New Mercies & Faithfulness at Dawn
- **Thematic Focus:** Mercies that arrive with the morning; yesterday's failures do not set today's terms.
- **Verse Recitation:** Second key verse/section.
- **Exposition:** God's faithfulness as the ground under the day, drawing on Matthew Henry on covenant mercy.

### Movement 3: Trust & Guidance for the Decisions Ahead
- **Thematic Focus:** The roads, meetings, work and choices of the hours ahead are committed to God's leading.
- **Verse Recitation:** Third key verse/section.
- **Exposition:** Leaning not on our own understanding; the straight path promised to those who acknowledge Him in all their ways.

### Movement 4: Favour, Shield & Protection for the Day
- **Thematic Focus:** God's favour as a shield, protection on the road and at work, courage for opposition.
- **Verse Recitation:** The central pivot verse of the passage.
- **Exposition:** The shield is God's own favour, not our performance; He goes before and stands behind.

### Movement 5: Blessing Over Work, Family & Home
- **Thematic Focus:** The work of the hands established; blessing spoken over family, home and every person the listener will meet.
- **Verse Recitation:** Fifth key verse/section.
- **Exposition:** The priestly blessing pattern — the Lord bless, keep, shine, lift up and give peace for this day.

### Movement 6: The Sending Benediction
- **Thematic Focus:** Being sent into the day under God's presence; strength renewed like the eagle's.
- **Verse Recitation:** Concluding verse.
- **Closing Benediction:** A spoken blessing over the listener's day, journey and work.
- **Call-to-Action:** "Type Amen to agree with this prayer, and subscribe to The Morning Altar to begin every sunrise with us."

## Citation & Translation Rules
- Primary Translations: King James Version (KJV), New King James Version (NKJV), English Standard Version (ESV), or American Standard Version (ASV).
- Historical References: Charles Spurgeon (*The Treasury of David*), Matthew Henry (*Commentary on the Whole Bible*).
- All scripture citations must be noted in the YAML description block.
- Morning rule: never close with sleep, bedtime or "close your eyes" language. The listener is being sent awake into the day.

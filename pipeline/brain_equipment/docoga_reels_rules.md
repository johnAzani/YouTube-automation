# DOCOGA REELS v2 Kinetic Typography & Asset Rules

## Kinetic Typography Specifications
- **Typeface:** Inter-Black (`assets/fonts/Inter-Black.ttf`), weight 700/900.
- **Card Geometry:**
  - Widescreen 16:9 bounding box: `1280×720` or `1920×1080`.
  - Margin: 72px left/right safe zone.
  - Sapphire glassmorphism card: `rgba(8, 22, 50, 0.57)` top gradient to `rgba(4, 12, 30, 0.46)` bottom gradient.
  - Border: 1.5px frosted cyan highlight `rgba(148, 198, 255, 0.65)`.
  - Corner radius: 16px.
  - Directional drop shadow: 8px blur, offset `(0, 12)`, `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`.
- **Word Sync Dynamics:**
  - Full sentence card renders with upcoming words in soft optic white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`).
  - Actively spoken word illuminates in radiant celestial gold (`#FFD700`) with subtle luminous glow.
  - Sentence-level stability: card remains stable during the sentence, no frantic jumping.
  - Zero-latency transitions: upon completion of the final word, the card clears instantly and moves to the next verse.

## Visual Asset Query Architecture
For every scene, provide high-quality search queries targeting public-domain landscape and sanctuary photography:
- Native 16:9 widescreen orientation.
- Majestic, tranquil nature: mountain meadows, alpine sunsets, still lakes, dawn mist, golden hour forest light, ancient stone sanctuaries, candlelit altars.
- Avoid cluttered modern cityscapes, modern cars, or photorealistic faces.
- Verified against Wikimedia Commons, USGS, NPS, and NASA public domain archives.

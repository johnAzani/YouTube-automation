# Theological & Scriptural Exposition Rubric

## Script Structure (The 6-Movement Journey)
Every long-form deep-dive script (target duration 5:30 – 6:30 minutes; word count 550 – 750 words) must follow a structured 6-movement devotional arc:

### Movement 1: The Sacred Hook & Evening Surrender
- **Opening Line:** Immediate invitation to breathe, surrender the day's fatigue, and release control.
- **Verse Recitation:** The opening verse of the chapter.
- **Exposition:** Assurance that God is present in the stillness of the bedroom.

### Movement 2: Green Pastures & Divine Peace
- **Thematic Focus:** Calming the nervous system, escaping the turbulent noise of the world.
- **Verse Recitation:** Second key verse/section.
- **Exposition:** God's gentleness—He does not push us into hurry; He leads us into rest.

### Movement 3: Emotional Healing & Soul Restoration
- **Thematic Focus:** Healing bruised spirits, mental exhaustion, quiet unspoken grief.
- **Verse Recitation:** Third key verse/section.
- **Exposition:** Drawing upon Spurgeon's commentary on how God restores what life broke.

### Movement 4: The Valley of Shadows & Courage in the Dark
- **Thematic Focus:** Overcoming fear of the dark, anxiety attacks, and spiritual warfare.
- **Verse Recitation:** The central pivot verse of the Psalm.
- **Exposition:** Shadows prove the presence of light; God walks beside the listener in the valley.

### Movement 5: The Banqueting Table & Overflowing Cup
- **Thematic Focus:** God's abundance and favor in the presence of life's struggles.
- **Verse Recitation:** Fifth key verse/section.
- **Exposition:** Anointing with fragrant oil—soothing headaches, mental tension, and anxiety.

### Movement 6: The Covenant Blessing & Evening Benediction
- **Thematic Focus:** Eternal protection, goodness and mercy as heavenly guardians.
- **Verse Recitation:** Concluding verse.
- **Closing Benediction:** Peaceful prayer over sleep, family, and home.
- **Call-to-Action:** "Type Amen to agree with this prayer, and subscribe to The Secret Place to pray with us every night."

## Citation & Translation Rules
- Primary Translations: King James Version (KJV), New King James Version (NKJV), English Standard Version (ESV), or American Standard Version (ASV).
- Historical References: Charles Spurgeon (*The Treasury of David*), Matthew Henry (*Commentary on the Whole Bible*).
- All scripture citations must be noted in the YAML description block.

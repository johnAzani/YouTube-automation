# The Praise Garden — staged review rubric

**Not registered or approved for production.** A human reviews each draft and
final video; automated flags and checklists do not establish truth, rights,
originality, or YouTube monetization eligibility.

1. **Passage and context:** verify the cited KJV passage and any quotation;
   identify the speaker, audience and setting where relevant. Do not fabricate
   commentary or quote an unconsulted source.
2. **Distinct value:** identify the episode's own question, specific reflection,
   prayer and practical application. Compare it with other network videos and
   revise repetitive, interchangeable material.
3. **Pastoral care:** include gratitude without shaming people who struggle;
   avoid guaranteed financial, physical or emotional outcomes. Never discourage
   professional support or medical care.
4. **Composition:** open on the episode's distinct topic, connect exposition to
   a concrete prayer, and close without manipulating engagement. Vary wording,
   imagery and pacing to fit the episode.
5. **Production:** verify all visual and audio rights, captions, pronunciation,
   truthful title and thumbnail. The output must not be approved simply because
   a template or flag passed.
6. **Decision:** document reviewer, sources and unresolved issues outside the
   public video; leave the draft unpublished until approved.
